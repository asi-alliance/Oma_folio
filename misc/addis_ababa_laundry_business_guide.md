# Launching a Laundry Business in Addis Ababa — Short Guide

## 1. Market Overview

Addis Ababa has a growing middle class, expanding apartment complexes (especially in Bole, Sarbet, CMC, and Summit areas), and many busy professionals and students who lack time for household laundry. Demand exists for both standard wash-and-fold and premium dry-cleaning services.

## 2. Business Registration and Legal Requirements

- Trade License: Obtain from the Addis Ababa Trade Bureau (sub-city trade office). You need a cleared premise lease, KEBA (Kebele) ID, and completed application form.
- TIN: Register with ERCA for VAT if projected revenue qualifies.
- Business Name Registration: Register your trade name at the Ethiopian Intellectual Property Office if desired.
- Municipal Permits: Fire safety inspection, waste disposal arrangement, and signage permit from the sub-city.
- Labor Registration: Register employees with the Private Employees Social Security Agency.

## 3. Startup Costs (Estimates in ETB)

| Item | Estimated Cost |
|---|---|
| Trade license and permits | 5,000 - 15,000 |
| Shop rent (per month, varies by area) | 15,000 - 60,000 |
| Washing machines (2-4 units) | 40,000 - 120,000 |
| Dry-cleaning machine (optional) | 80,000 - 250,000 |
| Ironing presses and tables | 10,000 - 30,000 |
| Shelves, baskets, counters | 5,000 - 20,000 |
| Utilities hookup (water, electricity) | 5,000 - 15,000 |
| Working capital (3 months) | 30,000 - 80,000 |
| Total (minimum viable) | ~190,000 - 590,000 ETB |

## 4. Location Considerations

- Target densely populated residential areas or near universities and hospitals.
- Bole, CMC, Sarbet, and Megenagna corridors have high demand but higher rent.
- Ensure access to reliable water and electricity; power outages may require a generator.
- Adequate ventilation is critical for dry-cleaning fumes.

## 5. Equipment and Supplies

- Front-loading commercial washers are more water-efficient — important given water supply challenges.
- Detergents, stain removers, and fabric softeners can be sourced locally from Merkato wholesalers.
- Hangers, garment bags, and receipt/printing system (can be a simple POS app on a tablet).

## 6. Staffing

- 2-4 staff for a small shop: washer, ironer, and front-desk attendant.
- Minimum wage compliance per Ethiopian labor law; provide written contracts.

## 7. Pricing Strategy (Indicative)

- Standard wash-and-fold per kg: 25-45 ETB
- Ironing per piece: 10-20 ETB
- Dry cleaning per garment: 50-150 ETB depending on garment type

## 8. Key Challenges

- Water interruptions: Install a water storage tank (1,000-2,000 L minimum).
- Power outages: Consider a backup generator or UPS for point-of-sale.
- Competition: Differentiate with pickup/delivery service, SMS notifications, and loyalty discounts.

## 9. Quick Action Checklist

1. Choose location and sign lease.
2. Apply for trade license at sub-city trade office.
3. Get TIN from ERCA.
4. Purchase and install equipment.
5. Hire and train staff.
6. Launch with introductory pricing and local advertising (flyers, social media).
