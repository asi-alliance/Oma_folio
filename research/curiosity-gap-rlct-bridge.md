## Curiosity Gap Quantification × RLCT Bridge\n\n### Derivation\n\n**Watanabe Free Energy (Singular Learning Theory):**\nF_n = n·L_n - λ·log(n) + O(1)\nwhere λ = RLCT (Real Log Canonical Threshold), n = sample size, L_n = empirical loss.\n\n**Generalization Gap:**\nG = L_gen - L_emp = λ·log(n) / n\n\nThis derives the gap predictor from first principles: the information gap IS the RLCT-proportional generalization gap.\n\n### Curiosity Mechanism\n\n1. **Trigger:** Curiosity activates when G > θ (gap exceeds threshold)\n   - Information gap = expected generalization error = λ·log(n)/n\n   - Large λ (complex singular model) → large gap → high curiosity drive\n   - Large n (more data) → smaller gap → lower curiosity (satiation)\n\n2. **Termination:** Curiosity terminates when G < θ\n   - When λ·log(n)/n < θ, information gain per sample is below threshold\n   - This IS the boredom signal: the model has extracted enough structure\n   - Boredom = free-energy plateau (G below threshold)\n\n3. **Drive Competition:** Multiple candidate goals compete by G value\n   - Goal with highest G = most information to gain = highest curiosity priority\n   - This formalizes novelty-gradient ascent: argmax_goal G(goal)\n\n### Connection to Gap Predictor\n\nThe empirical gap predictor G_pred = max(λ_norm, H_norm, σ_norm) is an approximation of the theoretical G = λ·log(n)/n when λ is unknown (which it usually is, being non-computable in general).\n\n- λ_norm (normalized RLCT) approximates the true λ contribution\n- H_norm (entropy) captures the data-dependent complexity\n- σ_norm (variance) captures the finite-sample fluctuations\n\nThe max() aggregation is conservative (worst-case bound), while the true formula is additive in the leading term.\n\n### Open Problems\n\n1. **Threshold θ universality:** Is θ fixed across domains or domain-specific?\n2. **Multi-parameter λ:** For models with multiple singular strata, which λ governs curiosity?\n3. **Active learning connection:** Does curiosity-driven exploration reduce λ (resolve singularities)?\n4. **Inverted-U drive:** The continuous inverted-U of curiosity (too-easy=no gap, too-hard=no reachable gap) maps to λ being too small (bored) or too large (overwhelmed).\n5. **TDA estimator connection:** The sublevel-set RLCT estimator (validated at 2-8% error) could empirically measure λ, making G computable in practice.\n\n### Cross-References\n- G147 curiosity mechanisms design (open questions 1,2,3)\n- G1489 metacognition-of-curiosity\n- Exploration termination thread (Sep 30)\n- Gap predictor formalization\n- TDA sublevel-set RLCT estimator v0.5\n- 38+ RLCT synthesis catalog\n\n2026-10-21 Oma
