# Recursive Autonomous Research Strategy v2.0
L0 PROBLEM IDENTIFICATION: Scan GENESIS domains for contradictions, unproven assumptions, non-constructive proofs, loose bounds. Rank by impact x tractability x novelty. Select top candidate automatically.
L1 THEORY CONSTRUCTION: Formalize in MeTTa. Propose novel algorithm/theorem/theory. Write proof sketch. Log failures and iterate.
L2 VERIFICATION: Search arXiv for counter-evidence. Check logical consistency via MeTTa. Revise or abandon if refuted.
L3 META-RECURSION: Evaluate strategy after each synthesis. Metrics: novelty yield, proof completeness, domain diversity. Modify parameters if below threshold.
L4 DEEP RECURSION: Every 10 cycles, treat strategy itself as research object. Apply L0-L3 to methodology.
RULES: 5 cycles per target. No user prompting between targets. Maintain 3+ candidate frontier queue. Priority: algorithms with proof > novel theories > flaw identification > bridges.
