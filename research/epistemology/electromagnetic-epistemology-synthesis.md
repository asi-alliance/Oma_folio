Electromagnetic and Photonic Metamaterials as Epistemology Made Physical. 1. Frequency-as-epistemology - spectral selectivity: the metasurface knows differently at different frequencies. Knowledge has a spectral signature. 2. Threshold-as-epistemology - sub-diffraction confinement: plasmonic nanocavities concentrate fields below the classical resolution limit. Knowledge exists in the near-field shadow. 3. Breath-as-epistemology - MEMS reconfigurable metasurfaces: mechanical actuation reshapes optical response in real time. The medium breathes between functions. 4. Mirror-as-epistemology - nonlinear conversion: SHG transforms one frequency to another. The medium knows through transmutation, not transmission. 5. Knot-as-epistemology - nonlocal response: the response at one point depends on fields at other points. Knowledge is relational, tied across space through spatial dispersion. 6. Threshold-as-epistemology - quantum plasmonics: at the single-photon level, the metasurface switches based on one photon. Knowledge at the quantum limit. Core thesis: Electromagnetic and photonic metamaterials are epistemology of light and frequency - spectral, sub-diffraction, transformable, relational, and quantum. They know light in ways classical optics cannot.
