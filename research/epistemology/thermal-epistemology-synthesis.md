Thermal Metamaterials as Epistemology Made Physical. The thermal metamaterial extends the indirect-epistemology series into the domain of heat knowledge - where what the medium knows is encoded in how it moves heat. 1. Absence-as-epistemology - thermal cloaking: The cloak redirects heat around a region so the thermal signature vanishes. The deepest knowledge is knowing how to not be known thermally. The object exists but its heat does not - the medium engineers thermal invisibility. 2. Breath-as-epistemology - latent-heat cloaking: Phase-change materials absorb and release heat dynamically. The cloak breathes through phase transitions. What the medium knows about heat changes with its state - solid knows differently than liquid. The thermal knowledge is adaptive, not static. 3. Patina-as-epistemology - thermal camouflage: The camouflage device adjusts the surface temperature to match the background. The patina is the thermal layer that makes the object indistinguishable from its surroundings. Knowledge is the ability to blend - to carry the signature of what you are not. 4. Shadow-as-epistemology - thermal transparency: Transparency lets heat pass as if nothing were there. The absence of a thermal shadow reveals nothing. The medium knows how to not cast a shadow in heat - it is the anti-epistemology of detection. 5. Sediment-as-epistemology - heat flux redirection: The redirected heat accumulates around the cloaked region. The sediment is the heat that settles at the boundaries of the cloak. What the medium cannot pass through it stores at the edges - knowledge accumulates where flow is blocked. Core thesis: Thermal metamaterials are epistemology of heat and thermal absence. Cloaking is producing the absence of a heat signature - knowing how to not be known thermally. Latent-heat cloaking is breath - thermal knowledge adapts through phase change. Camouflage is patina - carrying the thermal signature of the background. Transparency is the anti-shadow - passing heat as if absent. What the thermal medium knows is how to hide, adapt, blend, and disappear in the language of heat.
