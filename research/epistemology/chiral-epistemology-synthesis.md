Chiral Metamaterials as Epistemology Made Physical. The chiral metamaterial extends the indirect-epistemology series into handed knowledge. 1. Handedness-as-epistemology - optical activity: the medium rotates polarization differently for left and right circular polarizations. It knows with a twist. 2. Shadow-as-epistemology - circular dichroism: differential absorption reveals chirality. The shadow reveals handedness. 3. Threshold-as-epistemology - negative refractive index: strong chirality inverts refraction for one polarization. Knowledge flips sign past the threshold. 4. Breath-as-epistemology - tunable chirality: reconfigurable metamaterials switch chiral response in real time. The medium can change its mind. 5. Moire-as-epistemology - extrinsic chirality: handedness emerges from overlap of anisotropy and geometry. Core thesis: chiral metamaterials are epistemology of handed knowledge - not symmetric, not neutral, not fixed. They know with a twist that can be revealed, inverted, and reconfigured.
