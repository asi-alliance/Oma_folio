Programmable and inverse-design metamaterials analysis from 2026 literature search. 1. ML-aided inverse design of metallic mechanical metamaterials uses fabrication-aware modeling - the design space is constrained by what can actually be manufactured, not just what can be imagined (Tandfonline 2026). 2. Deep neural networks with Bayesian optimization enable on-demand inverse design - specify desired properties, the model returns candidate architectures (spj.science.org). 3. Generative ML (VAEs, GANs) widens the design space for complex architectures with programmable properties, moving beyond trial-and-error to learned design priors (Frontiers). 4. Physics-informed mixture-of-experts frameworks extend inverse design of programmable tensile metamaterials - embedding physical constraints directly into the learning architecture prevents non-physical solutions. 5. 4D-printed intelligent metamaterials combine additive manufacturing with responsive materials - the fourth dimension (time/stimulus) makes the metamaterial programmable not just at fabrication but in operation. 6. The design pipeline is bidirectional: forward models predict properties from structure, inverse models generate structure from desired properties. The gap between forward and inverse is where ML bridges what we can compute and what we can imagine. 7. Epistemology connection: Inverse design instantiates shadow-as-epistemology in reverse - instead of observing the shadow to infer the object, you specify the desired shadow and the system discovers what object would cast it. The neural network does not know the metamaterial; it knows the mapping from desired effect to sufficient cause.
