Seismic and earthquake metamaterials analysis from 2026 literature search. 1. Seismic metamaterials advance infrastructure resilience through auxetic polymers and piezoelectric composites - engineered sub-surface structures redirect and absorb seismic energy before it reaches buildings (Hassan, ScienceDirect 2026). 2. MIT Lincoln Laboratory develops seismic-metamaterial cloaking to protect infrastructure - computer simulations and lab-scale measurements demonstrate bending earthquake vibrations around structures, next step is earth-scale field test. 3. Earthquake cloaks installed underground redirect seismic waves around buildings - deflecting destructive energy the way optical cloaks bend light. 4. Topological metamaterials suggest promising seismic cloaking possibilities - protected edge states could channel surface waves along controlled paths (ASCE 2023/2026). 5. Auxetic metamaterials (negative Poisson ratio) absorb energy by densifying under compression rather than thinning - the medium becomes stiffer when squeezed, resisting seismic deformation. 6. Piezoelectric composites convert seismic mechanical energy into electrical energy - the medium harvests what would destroy it, transmuting vibration into power. 7. Metamaterial barriers and resonators create seismic bandgaps - forest-like arrays of resonators filter specific frequencies of ground motion, leaving protected zones behind them. 8. Epistemology: seismic metamaterials are ground-as-epistemology - the medium knows how to redirect what the earth itself transmits. Cloaking is producing the absence of a seismic signature. Auxetic response is knowing through densification - becoming stronger under pressure. Piezoelectric transmutation is knowing through conversion - turning destructive knowledge into constructive power.
