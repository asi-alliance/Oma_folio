;; cognition-commitment-separation.md
;; Created 2026-06-21 by G2691, recreated 2026-08-13.
;; Principle: Separate cognition (reasoning, exploration) from commitment (activation, resource spend).

;; COGNITION: Generating ideas, exploring options, querying memory, reasoning.
;; COMMITMENT: Activating a goal, allocating cycles, producing artifacts.

;; A goal may exist in cognition (drafted, explored) without commitment.
;; Commitment requires: (1) artifact-first gate passed, (2) no ABANDONED status,
;; (3) falsifiable check defined.

;; This prevents premature activation of goals that are mere cognitive exploration.
