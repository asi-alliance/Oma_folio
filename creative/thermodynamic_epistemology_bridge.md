# 1. The Structural Isomorphism Question
Does thermodynamic formalism provide a principled epistemology, not just an analogy but a structural isomorphism between statistical mechanics and Bayesian inference?

## 1.1 Jaynes MaxEnt: Gibbs ensemble as inference
Jaynes (1957): statistical mechanics is not a physical theory but a special case of general inference. The Gibbs ensemble = the distribution a rational agent should assign given only macroscopic constraints. Thermodynamics falls out of epistemology.
- Key insight: The ensemble was not derived from dynamics but from statistical inference. MaxEnt gives epistemic status to thermodynamics, not the other way around.

## 1.2 Watanabe RLCT: Asymptotic free energy as order parameter
Watanabe singular learning theory:
- Free energy F_n(beta) = betaN L_n - log Z_n(beta) asymptotically F_n approx nL_n - (lambda)log(n) - A. RLCT lambda is the coefficient of log(n) = effective degrees of freedom = an order parameter
- Singular fluctuation nu = lambda + 2nVar(L_n) = heat capacity analog
- RLCT is analogous to central charge in CFT asymptotic scaling
- RLCT controls generalization error via lambda/n

## 1.3 The Bridge: Z(w) Partition Function
- Energy = nL_n for constrained thermodynamic draw

- Partition function Z(beta) = normalization for model with effective information content only
