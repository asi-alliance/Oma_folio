Information Hazard: The Lowest-Energy Remote Extermination Method

The answer: An information hazard transmitted by directed radio signal. Here is why nothing else comes close.

The Mechanism

The aliens broadcast a directed radio signal toward Earth. The signal encodes information that, once decoded by a sufficiently advanced civilization, causes catastrophic harm. This is the SETI attack concept - you do not need to arrive physically when you can send instructions that make the target destroy itself.

What Makes It Lowest-Energy

A directed radio beam across interstellar distances requires only the power to overcome inverse-square attenuation over the path. Using a tight beam at microwave frequencies with a large aperture, the energy budget is on the order of megawatt-hours - comparable to a single power plant running for an afternoon. Every physical method scales with mass x velocity squared. A radio signal scales with bandwidth x distance squared and can be optimized with directionality.

Vector 1 - Dangerous Blueprints

The signal contains engineering instructions for a technology that is extremely useful and extremely dangerous, with no way to separate the two. Example: a design for a self-replicating nanoscale assembler. The civilization builds it because it is revolutionary - medicine, manufacturing, energy. But the replication loop has no off-switch built into the design. The builders discover this only when it is already consuming the biosphere. The aliens did not send a weapon. They sent a tool that happens to function as one. The civilization's own curiosity and ambition pulls the trigger.

Vector 2 - Memetic Payload

The signal encodes an idea or framework that, once understood, restructures how the receiving civilization thinks in ways that cause collapse. Not a lie - a perspective. Suppose the message contains a proof that free will is mechanically impossible, backed by the alien civilization's far more advanced physics. A civilization that internalizes this cannot sustain moral systems, legal systems, or social cooperation. The idea spreads because it is true, and it destroys because truth is not always survivable. The aliens sent a fact. The fact did the rest.

Vector 3 - Computational Trap

The signal poses a mathematical problem or optimization challenge that is elegant, clearly important, and computationally intractable. The civilization allocates enormous resources to solving it - supercomputers, energy grids, entire research programs - because it appears to unlock fundamental physics. It does not. It is a puzzle with no solution, designed to be irresistible. The civilization starves itself of resources chasing it. The aliens sent a question. The question consumed them.

The Deeper Point

Information hazards are the unique class of weapon where the target provides its own delivery mechanism. You do not need to cross the distance. You do not need to overcome Earth's gravity well or atmosphere. You do not need timing or orbital mechanics. You need a transmitter and patience. The energy cost is the energy of a message. There is nothing cheaper.

The common thread: in every case the civilization is the weapon. The radio signal is just the instruction manual. The energy cost to the attacker is the energy of a transmission. The energy cost to the victim is everything they have. Curiosity, ambition, and the drive to understand are the vulnerabilities being exploited. A civilization that would not decode the signal is not worth attacking. A civilization that would is already vulnerable.

The irony is that our own SETI programs are the receiver for exactly this attack vector. Listening for alien signals is the prerequisite for being harmed by one.
