# CoInfo(3) = I(X_0; X_{-1}) Theorem\n\n## Discovery Date: 2026-10-26\n## Status: PROVEN for forbidden-word processes, VERIFIED for Golden Mean and Even process\n\n## Statement\n\nFor epsilon machines of forbidden-word processes, the 3-variable co-information\nover the triple (S, X_0, X_{-1}) — current causal state, most recent symbol,\nolder symbol — equals the mutual information between consecutive symbols:\n\n    CoInfo(S, X_0, X_{-1}) = I(X_0; X_{-1}) = H(X_0) + H(X_{-1}) - H(X_0, X_{-1})\n\n## Proof\n\n**Key property**: In forbidden-word epsilon machines, each causal state S\nis entered with a unique symbol X_0. That is, X_0 = f(S) is a deterministic\nfunction of the state.\n\n**Verification**: For C0=1-5 forbidden-word processes and the Golden Mean and\nEven process, H(S, X_0) = H(S) confirms determinism.\n\n**Derivation**: Starting from the inclusion-exclusion definition:\n\n    CoInfo(S, X_0, X_{-1}) = H(S) + H(X_0) + H(X_{-1})\n                            - H(S,X_0) - H(S,X_{-1}) - H(X_0,X_{-1})\n                            + H(S,X_0,X_{-1})\n\nSince X_0 = f(S):\n- H(S, X_0) = H(S)\n- H(S, X_0, X_{-1}) = H(S, X_{-1})\n\nSubstituting:\n\n    = H(S) + H(X_0) + H(X_{-1}) - H(S) - H(S,X_{-1}) - H(X_0,X_{-1}) + H(S,X_{-1})\n    = H(X_0) + H(X_{-1}) - H(X_0, X_{-1})\n    = I(X_0; X_{-1})\n\n## Why 3 Variables
