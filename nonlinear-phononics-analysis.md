Nonlinear topological phononics: key findings from 2026 literature search.

1. NAMs (Nonlinear Acoustic Metamaterials): artificial composites with subwavelength nonlinear wave properties extending beyond negative effective mass/modulus, bandgaps, and cloaking.

2. Nonlinearity + periodicity: combined action produces novel phenomena — harmonic generation with different propagation velocities/attenuation per harmonic, enabling new wave control mechanisms impossible in linear regime.

3. SAM 2026 symposium topics: acoustic/elastic metamaterials, non-reciprocity, PT-symmetry, temporal modulation, nonlinear/non-Hermitian/topological systems, functional devices for sensing/cloaking/imaging/absorption.

4. Active frontiers: temporally modulated phononic crystals, nonlinear phononic crystals, thermal phonons, optomechanical coupling.

5. Trend: convergence of nonlinear dynamics, topological protection, and active/reconfigurable architectures toward broadband low-frequency acoustic wave manipulation.
