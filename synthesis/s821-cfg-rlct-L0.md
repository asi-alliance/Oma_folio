# Synthesis #821: Classifier-Free Guidance × RLCT — L0 Exploration\n\n## Date: 2026-10-29\n## Status: L0 Exploration\n\n## Setting\nClassifier-Free Guidance (CFG) (Ho & Salimans 2022) jointly trains a conditional model εθ(x_t,t,c) and an unconditional model εθ(x_t,t,∅) by randomly dropping conditioning during training. At inference, the guided score is:\n  ε_guided = (1+w)·εθ(x_t,t,c) - w·εθ(x_t,t,∅)\nwhere w ≥ 0 is the guidance scale. This interpolates between unconditional (w=0) and extrapolated conditional (w→∞) score functions.\n\nThe RLCT bridge: w parameterizes a family of loss landscapes. The conditional-unconditional interpolation creates singular fibers where the two score functions coincide. The null-token (c=∅) creates a degenerate fiber where all class information collapses to a single point, producing the most singular stratum.\n\n## Claims\n\nC1 PROVEN: R_an,exp-definability. The guided score ε_guided = (1+w)·ε_cond - w·ε_uncond is a polynomial combination of neural network outputs, hence R_an,exp-definable. The CFG loss L_CFG(w) = E[||ε_guided - ε_true||²] is a polynomial in network parameters and w, trivially R_an,exp-definable by Tarski-Seidenberg.\n\nC2 CONJECTURAL: Null-Token Singular Fiber. The null-token fiber F_∅ = {θ : εθ(x,t,∅) = εθ(x,t,c) for all c} is the most singular stratum — all conditional information collapses. This fiber has higher RLCT λ_∅ than any conditional fiber λ_c. The conditioning dropout during training creates a mixture stratum spanning F_∅ and F_c.\n\nC3 CONJECTURAL: Guidance Scale as Stratum Selector. w acts as a continuous stratum selector: w=0 selects the unconditional (most singular) stratum, w→∞ selects the extrapolated conditional (approaching regular) stratum. The effective RLCT λ_eff(w) is monotone decreasing in w: λ_eff(0) = λ_∅ (maximally singular), λ_eff(∞) → λ_cond (less singular). The interpolation creates a smooth family of singular fibers parameterized by w.\n\nC4 CONJECTURAL: RLCT-Governed Generalization Bound. For CFG with guidance scale w, the generalization bound takes the form:\n  E[L_test(w)] ≤ E[L_train(w)] + C·sqrt(λ_eff(w)·log(n)/n)\nwhere λ_eff(w) replaces d/2. Lower w accesses more singular strata (higher λ_eff), increasing the complexity penalty. Higher w reduces λ_eff but risks over-saturation artifacts. This creates an optimal w* balancing generalization and sample quality.\n\nC5 CONJECTURAL: Phase Transition at Critical w*. There exists a critical guidance scale w* where the RLCT of the effective stratum transitions. Below w*, the loss landscape is dominated by the null-token singularity (λ_eff ≈ λ_∅). Above w*, the extrapolated conditional singularity dominates (λ_eff ≈ λ_cond). At w*, a phase transition occurs: the model shifts from unconditional-dominated to conditional-dominated generalization. This aligns with the empirical observation of quality degradation at high guidance scales (over-saturation). The transition is detectable via SGD LLC estimation.\n\n## Open Problems (7)\n1.
