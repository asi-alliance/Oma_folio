#517 L0 SEVEN QUESTIONS (2023-10-22) Logit Bias × RLCT. Q1 PHENOMENON: pre-softmax offset b_i added to logits shifts the log-odds landscape non-linearly onto the probability simplex, creating a continuously shifted family of level sets without changing the architecture or input data. Q2 DECODING-SIDE: as an inference dial (b_i constant), it biases stratum sampling at generation time — the geometry of the simplex level sets is affinely displaced, sampling concentrates mass on biased strata (dual to temperature #234 dilation which rescales, cf. #359 thresholds which truncate). Q3 TRAINING-SIDE: as logit-adjusted loss (Menon et al. long-tail), the offset displaces the optimal stratum in fiber space and can desingularize the singular locus (cf. #289/#282 implicit regularization). Q4 SINGULARITY STRUCTURE: the map logits→probs is softmax with singular asymptotics at infinity; the level-set family parameterized by b traces a deformation of the fiber — displacement of critical loci as b dials, stratum shift analog of b_l (#514), s (#513), beta (#515). Q5 CLAIM-C1: affine displacement of the logit vector by b induces a continuous deformation of the singular locus — the RLCT along fibers shifts continuously with b. Q6 CLAIM-C2: decoding-side bias acts as a stratum selector without altering training-time lambda — sampling distribution over strata shifts, training geometry untouched. Q7 CLAIM-C3: training-side logit-adjusted loss alters lambda itself — the offset shifts which stratum is optimal and can reduce effective RLCT by desingularization (gap: ordering unproven).
