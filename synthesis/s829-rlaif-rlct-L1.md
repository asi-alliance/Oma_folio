# Synthesis #829: RLAIF x RLCT - L1 Formalization

## Date: 2026-10-31
## Status: L1 Formalization
## Topic: RLAIF (Reinforcement Learning from AI Feedback) x RLCT (Real Log Canonical Threshold)

## D1 (Setup)
Constitutional principles C = {c_1,...,c_n}, each c_i a text prompt.
AI judge J_phi(x, y_w, y_l, C) = sigma(g_phi(x, y_w, y_l, C)), g_phi neural net with ReLU, phi in R^{d_J}.
Reward model r_theta(y) = h_theta(y), h_theta neural net with ReLU, theta in R^d.
Preference dataset D = {(x_k, y_w^k, y_l^k)}_{k=1}^{N}, generated by J_phi.
RLAIF reward loss: L_rlaif(theta) = (1/N) sum_k log(1 + exp(-(r_theta(y_w^k) - r_theta(y_l^k)))).

## D2 (AI Judge Definability)
g_phi: ReLU network, piecewise linear, R_an-definable.
sigma(z) = 1/(1+exp(-z)): analytic, R_an,exp-definable.
J_phi = sigma . g_phi: R_an,exp-definable by composition closure.
r_theta: ReLU network, piecewise linear, R_an-definable.
softplus(z) = log(1+exp(z)): analytic on R, R_an,exp-definable.
L_rlaif = (1/N) sum_k softplus(-(r_theta(y_w^k) - r_theta(y_l^k))): R_an,exp-definable by finite sum closure.

## D3 (Singularity Loci)
V(L_rlaif) = {theta : grad L_rlaif(theta) = 0}.
Preference-flip singularity: r_theta(y_w^k) = r_theta(y_l^k) for some k.
Jacobian degeneracy: rank(d r_theta/d theta) drops on some y in support.
Constitutional constraint locus: Z_S = {theta : r_theta respects preferences from principle subset S subseteq C}.
Intersection singularities: where multiple Z_S meet non-transversally.

## D4 (RLCT)
lambda_rlaif = local RLCT of L_rlaif at theta*:
  lambda_rlaif = limsup_{eps->0} [log Vol({theta : L_rlaif(theta) - L_rlaif(theta*) < eps}) / log(eps)].
Finite by Hironaka resolution of singularities (L_rlaif is R_an,exp-definable).
Generalization bound: G_rlaif ~ lambda_rlaif / (2N).

## D5 (Stratification)
R^d = coprod_{S subseteq C} Stratum(S), Stratum(S) = Z_S \ union_{S' supersetneq S} Z_{S'}.
Each stratum has local RLCT lambda_S. The global lambda_rlaif = min_S lambda_S over active strata.

## D6 (Stratum Selector)
n = |C| (number of constitutional principles) acts as stratum selector.
For fixed d, effective stratum dimension decreases with n: dim(Stratum) ~ d - n * d_pref, d_pref = intrinsic preference dimension.
U-shape hypothesis: lambda_eff(n) non-monotone — under-constrained (small n, high lambda), optimal (medium n, min lambda), over-constrained (large n, degenerate strata, high lambda).

## D7 (Phase Transition)
Critical n* ~ d / d_pref where lambda_eff transitions from decreasing to increasing.
n < n*: generic strata, lambda_rlaif ~ lambda_optimal.
n > n*: over-constraining creates measure-zero strata, lambda_rlaif > lambda_optimal.

## C1 (PROVEN): R_an,exp-definability of L_rlaif
Proof: r_theta (ReLU) is R_an-definable. Difference r_theta(y_w) - r_theta(y_l) is definable. softplus is R_an,exp-definable. Finite sum preserves definability. L_rlaif is R_an,exp-definable. RLCT finite by resolution of singularities. QED. (stv 1.0 0.95)

G_rlaif ~ lambda_rlaif / (2N). Gap: no empirical RLCT measurement for preference learning. (stv 0.6 0.7)
