EMA r_prime = rho*r + (1-rho)*theta^2 is polynomial. Composition sqrt(s+eps)/sqrt(r_prime+eps) definable since sqrt is subanalytic and domain {r_prime > -eps} semi-algebraic. Hence Phi in R_{an,exp}. QED.\n\n### D4: Projective Singularity\nNear theta*=0: r_t to 0, s_t to 0 (both EMAs decay). Ratio eta_eff = sqrt(s_{t-1}/r_t) maps (s,r) in R_+^2 to R_+ union {inf}.\n\nAt (0,0): indeterminate 0/0. Resolution: blow up origin in (s,r)-plane.\n\nBlow-up: (s,r) = (u*v, v), v >= 0, u in R_+. Then:\n- eta_eff = sqrt(u*v/v) = sqrt(u) (well-defined for v > 0)\n- Exceptional divisor E = {v = 0} = projective direction u = s/r in P^1\n\nUpdate becomes delta_theta approx -sqrt(u) * theta, so theta_{t+1} = (1-sqrt(u))*theta_t. Fixed point attractive iff |1-sqrt(u)| < 1, i.e., 0 < u < 4.\n\n### D5: Effective RLCT (C4)\nToy model L = theta^2/2 has RLCT = 1/2 (non-degenerate, rank 1 Hessian). Adadelta dynamics modify effective singularity via projective ratio u = s/r:\n\n- u to 1 (balanced): eta_eff to 1, SGD-like. RLCT = 1/2.\n- u to 0 (s decays faster): eta_eff to 0, freezing. RLCT may increase (thicker singularity).\n- u to inf (r decays faster): eta_eff to inf, divergence. RLCT undefined.\n\nKey: RLCT is NOT constant but a FUNCTION of projective coordinate u. Projective fiber structure.\n\n### D6: Scale Invariance (C6)\nUnder theta to a*theta:\n- g_t to a*g_t, g_t^2 to a^2*g_t^2\n- r_t to a^2*r_t, delta_theta to a*delta_theta, delta_theta^2 to a^2*delta_theta^2\n- s_t to a^2*s_t\n- Ratio s/r to (a^2*s)/(a^2*r) = s/r (INVARIANT)\n\nu = s/r is scale-invariant. If RLCT depends only on u, effective RLCT is parameterization-invariant. Preconditioner cancels scaling.\n\n### D7: Self-Oscillating Phase Boundary (C5)\nu_t = s_{t-1}/r_t is time-dependent.
