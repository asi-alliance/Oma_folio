For k > rank_+(V), degenerate solutions have zeroed columns — the dominant singular locus is a boundary stratum, not the interior. The paper's bound may not apply to boundary-dominated regimes. **This is the strongest novel contribution.**\n\n2. **Non-negative rank vs ordinary rank phase transition** [MEDIUM NOVELTY]: The paper assumes H₀ is the non-negative rank with a strictly positive factorization. When rank_+(V) > rank_lin(V) (the non-negativity gap), the phase transition point differs from PCA's (#833). This distinction and its SLT implications are not drawn out.\n\n3. **Toric resolution approach** [MEDIUM NOVELTY]: The paper uses analytic normal form, not toric resolution. The toric geometry of the non-negative orthant and torus action (R+)^k could yield explicit RLCT via Newton polyhedra — a different proof technique from #56.\n\n4. **Interior-to-boundary singularity transition** [HIGH NOVELTY]: For increasing k past rank_+(V), the dominant singular locus shifts from interior scaling fiber to boundary column-zero stratum. This creates a qualitative change in LLC signature (kink/jump) absent in PCA, ICA, or SAE. Not in the paper.\n\n5. **SGLD/LLC empirical predictions** [MEDIUM NOVELTY]: The paper is purely asymptotic. Empirical phase transition detection via SGLD/LLC, including the boundary-dominated regime signature, is a novel experimental direction.\n\n## Impact on C1-C5\n\n- **C1**: STANDS (definability is prerequisite, not diminished by paper)\n- **C2**: REVISE — scaling fiber interior is partially covered by paper's bound. **Narrow C2 to boundary strata** (zero entries, column-zero locus) which paper excludes.\n- **C3**: REVISE — U-shape partially covered by bound formula. **Narrow C3 to non-negative rank vs ordinary rank distinction** and the boundary-dominated regime for k > rank_+(V).\n- **C4**: PARTIALLY DIMINISHED — gen bound leading coefficients covered.
