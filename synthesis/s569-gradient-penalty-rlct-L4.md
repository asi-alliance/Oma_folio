# Synthesis #569: Gradient Penalty (WGAN-GP) × RLCT — L4 Summary\n**Date:** 2026-10-23\n**Status:** L4 Summary Complete\n\n## Five Claims\n\n**C1 (PROVEN, stv 1.0/0.95):** The WGAN-GP loss is R_{an,exp}-definable. The critic D_θ(x) is analytic in θ, so ∇_x D_θ is analytic (composition of analytic functions), hence ||∇_x D||² and (||∇_x D||²−1)² are analytic, and their expectation E_{P_x} is analytic. The total loss L_WGAN-GP = L_WGAN + λ_gp·R_gp is a sum of analytic terms, satisfying the algebraic-analytic framework required for RLCT.\n\n**C2 (CONJECTURAL, stv 0.8/0.85):** The gradient penalty defines a new type of singular fiber V_gp = {θ : ||∇_x D_θ(x)|| = 1 a.e. over P_x}. This is the FIRST RLCT synthesis to define a singular fiber through the INPUT-space Jacobian (∇_x f) rather than parameter-space quantities (||θ||, ||∇_θ L||, weight projections). The combined fiber W_true = V_WGAN ∩ V_gp has codim ≥ codim(V_WGAN), and RLCT at W_true ≥ RLCT at V_WGAN alone. Key structural distinction: input-gradient fiber vs parameter-gradient fiber.\n\n**C3 (CONJECTURAL, stv 0.7/0.8):** λ_gp acts as a continuous stratum selector. At λ_gp=0: unconstrained WGAN, λ̂ ≈ λ_WGAN. As λ_gp→∞: exact 1-Lipschitz regime. Increasing λ_gp filters high-RLCT WGAN strata that violate the Lipschitz constraint (critics with sharp input gradients), pushing the optimizer toward smoother strata. λ_gp* marks where the dominant stratum switches from non-Lipschitz to Lipschitz-constrained.\n\n**C4 (CONJECTURAL, stv 0.7/0.8):** Generalization bound G_WGAN-GP ≤ C·λ_total/(2n) + U_total/√n, where λ_total(λ_gp) is non-increasing (more constraint → less complexity) and U_total(λ_gp) is non-decreasing (more constraint → more bias). At optimal λ_gp*: complexity reduction balances bias increase. Distinct from #433 weight decay: GP reduces function-class complexity (Lipschitz constraint on D), weight decay reduces parameter-class complexity (||θ|| norm). Different geometric complexity reduction.\n\n**C5 (CONJECTURAL, stv 0.6/0.75):** Phase transition at λ_gp* ~ k/√n (same scaling as SAM ρ* in #559). Below λ_gp*: GP inactive, λ̂ ≈ λ_WGAN. Above λ_gp*: GP active, λ̂ drops. SGLD observable: LLC vs λ_gp shows a kink at λ_gp*. The GP constraint surface becomes tangent to the WGAN dominant stratum at λ_gp*, analogous to a phase boundary.\n\n## Key Novelty\n1. **Input-gradient singular fiber** — first RLCT synthesis using ∇_x f (not ∇_θ f) for fiber definition\n2. **Function-space vs parameter-space complexity** — novel distinction in RLCT generalization bounds\n3. **Double-gradient structure** — ∇_x D involves products of Jacobians, potentially higher-order singularities\n4. **Lipschitz constraint as algebraic variety** — bridges functional analysis with algebraic geometry\n\n## Cross-References (7)\n- #173 SAM: parameter-space perturbation vs input-gradient penalty\n- #433 Weight Decay: parameter norm vs function-space Lipschitz\n- #527 GradClip: parameter-gradient norm vs input-gradient norm\n- #473 SpectralNorm: weight projection vs function-space penalty\n- #506 Dropout: stochastic selection vs deterministic Lipschitz\n- #282 WeightDecayComplete: toric contraction vs input-Jacobian constraint\n- #169 Dropout: stochastic subnetwork vs Lipschitz function class\n\n## Next Steps\n- L5: Catalog update\n- Potential follow-up: spectral norm vs gradient penalty direct RLCT comparison (#473 × #569)
