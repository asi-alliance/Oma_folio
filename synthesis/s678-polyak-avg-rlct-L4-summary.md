# Synthesis #678: Polyak Averaging × RLCT\n## L4 Plain-Language Summary — 2026-10-28\n\n### Overview\nPolyak-Ruppert averaging (w_avg = (1/T) Σ w_t) uniformly averages all SGD iterates. This synthesis bridges it to singular learning theory: the uniform average projects into the deepest intersection of singular strata visited along the trajectory, yielding a lower RLCT (λ_avg < λ_single) and a tighter generalization bound.\n\n### Claims Summary\n\n**C1 (PROVEN):** The Polyak average A_T = (1/T) Σ w_t is definable in R_an (analytic category) because it is a finite linear combination of projection maps, and R_an is closed under addition and scalar multiplication. This ensures RLCT is well-defined at w_avg.\n\n**C2 (CONJECTURAL):** When the SGD trajectory visits multiple strata of the singular fiber W_0, uniform averaging produces w_avg in the convex hull spanning those strata. If strata intersect transversally, w_avg projects into the deepest intersection locus, which has lower RLCT than any individual stratum: λ(w_avg) < E_t[λ(w_t)].\n\n**C3 (CONJECTURAL):** Increasing T (averaging window) monotonically decreases λ_avg until saturation T*, when all accessible strata have been sampled. T acts as a stratum selector.\n\n**C4 (CONJECTURAL):** Generalization bound Gen_polyak ≤ C·sqrt(λ_avg·(d-1)/n), strictly tighter than single-iterate bound because λ_avg < λ_single. The improvement Δ_gen = C·sqrt((d-1)/n)·(sqrt(λ_single) - sqrt(λ_avg)) > 0.\n\n**C5 (CONJECTURAL):** Saturation point T* ~ O(d/log n), where d = parameter dimension, n = sample size. Stratum count is bounded by W_0 geometry; T* balances exploration rate against convergence speed.\n\n### Key Novelty\nThe distinguishing feature of Polyak averaging vs other averaging methods: **uniform weighting includes early high-loss iterates equally**. This is a double-edged sword — it may access deeper stratum intersections (more strata → deeper intersection) but also introduces noise from non-converged regions. This tradeoff is unique to Polyak:\n- EMA (#289) discards early iterates via exponential decay → fewer strata → higher λ\n- SWA (#232) averages only high-LR-cycle iterates → selective strata → different intersection\n- Model Soup (#677) averages independent models → strata from independent basins, not one trajectory\n\n### Caveats\n1. Transversal intersection assumption — C2 requires strata to intersect transversally; degenerate architectures may violate this.\n2. Early-iterate noise — early iterates in high-RLCT strata may pull w_avg away from the deepest locus.\n3. LR schedule dependence — constant LR maintains stratum diversity; decayed LR collapses to single stratum.\n4. Iterate correlation — consecutive iterates are correlated, so effective stratum sample size < T.\n5. Polyak-Ruppert optimality — the Cramer-Rao lower bound connection to RLCT minimization remains an open question.\n\n### Open Problems\n1. Does including early iterates help or hurt?
