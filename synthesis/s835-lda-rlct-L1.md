#835 Latent Dirichlet Allocation x RLCT - L1 Formalization\n\n## Definitions\n- D1: LDA generative model: theta_d ~ Dir(alpha) i.i.d. per doc d=1..M; phi_k ~ Dir(beta) per topic k=1..K; z_dn|theta_d ~ Mult(theta_d); w_dn|z_dn,phi ~ Mult(phi_{z_dn}). Marginal likelihood per doc: integral over simplex Delta_{K-1} of Dir(theta;alpha) prod_k (prod_n p(w_dn|z_dn=k)) theta-paths; equivalently via Gamma ratios.\n- D2: Parameter space: interior of simplices theta_d in Delta_{K-1}, phi_k in Delta_{V-1} (open semialgebraic; boundaries are boundary strata).\n- D3: Topic permutation fiber: sigma in S_K acts (theta_d, phi) -> (theta_{sigma(d)}, phi_sigma); exchangeable likelihood, full S_K orbit in zero-loss set when topics unanchored.\n- D4: Dirichlet-smoothed fiber: unlike flat mixtures, prior Dir(alpha) assigns non-constant density along and transverse to the fiber; transverse scaling at the fiber governed by alpha.\n- D5: Empty-topic boundary stratum: {theta_dk = 0 for all d} (topic k unused); degenerate vertex strata where theta_d collapses to a vertex (hard assignment).\n- D6: Effective RLCT lambda(alpha,beta) over the Dirichlet-smoothed fiber and boundary strata; hyperparams as continuous selectors.\n- D7: Variational mean-field posterior q(theta,phi,z) = prod_d q(theta_d) prod_k q(phi_k) prod q(z_dn); collapsed Gibbs as exchangeable alternative; KL gap between q and exact posterior as functional of (alpha,beta).\n\n## Claims\n- C1 (PROVEN): Definability in R_an,exp. Dirichlet normalizer = Gamma(alpha)/prod Gamma(alpha_k); Gamma is log-analytic on (0,inf) hence definable in R_an,exp (same class as #244 multinomial-partition functions). Multinomial products are polynomial in theta/phi; per-doc integral over the simplex of a definable function is definable (Watanabe o-minimal integration argument). All strata semialgebraic. Same closure class as #268/#833/#834.\n- C2 (CONJECTURAL): The S_K permutation fiber is smoothed, not removed, by the Dirichlet prior: prior density along the orbit is constant (exchangeable Dir), but transverse decay is alpha-controlled. Per #69 lesson: RLCT is set by transverse KL x prior scaling, so lambda(alpha) varies continuously with alpha even though fiber dimension is constant. Distinct from #268 (no prior on the fiber) and #834 (labeled-anchor asymmetry instead of prior smoothing).\n- C3 (CONJECTURAL): (alpha,beta) as continuous stratum selectors: alpha -> 0 concentrates theta near vertices (hard-assignment limit, k-means-like codim-jump strata); alpha -> large regularizes toward uniform theta (approaching regular d/2 behavior). Predicted phase transition in lambda(alpha) at critical concentration alpha_c; sharpness open.\n- C4 (CONJECTURAL): Gen bound G <= lambda(alpha,beta) log(n)/2n + hyperparam misspec bias; over-specified K creates unused-topic boundary strata (D5) with their own RLCT contributions; choosing K = stratum resolution (cf. BIC-style model selection but singularity-aware).\n- C5 (CONJECTURAL): Variational mean-field collapses exactly onto boundary strata (theta_dk -> 0 for unused topics), underestimating posterior spread near singular strata; the variational-vs-exact KL gap is a signature of the singular locus. Collapsed Gibbs (exchangeable, z-integrated) respects the fiber symmetry and should localize RLCT differently than mean-field. Distinct from #327 hard pseudo-labels and #268 EM.\n\n## Gaps\n1.
