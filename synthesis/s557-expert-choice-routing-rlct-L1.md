# Synthesis #557: Expert Choice Routing × RLCT L1 Formalization\n# Date: 2026-10-23\n# Status: L1 — formal definitions and claims\n\n## Formal Definitions\n\n**D1 (Expert Choice Routing Map):** Let $g_\\theta: \\mathcal{X} \\to \\mathbb{R}^E$ be the router producing scores for $E$ experts. For expert $e$, let $\\sigma_e = \\text{argsort}(g_\\theta^e(\\mathbf{x}_1), \\ldots, g_\\theta^e(\\mathbf{x}_N))$ be the descending-sort permutation of $N$ tokens by expert-$e$ score. Expert $e$ processes tokens $\\{\\mathbf{x}_{\\sigma_e(1)}, \\ldots, \\mathbf{x}_{\\sigma_e(k)}\\}$ where $k$ is the capacity. The EC routing map is:\n$$R_{EC}(\\theta, \\mathbf{X}) = \\left\\{ \\left(e, \\mathbf{x}_{\\sigma_e(i)}\\right) : e \\in [E], i \\in [k] \\right\\}$$\nThis is a function $R_{EC}: \\Theta \\times \\mathcal{X}^N \\to \\mathcal{A}$ where $\\mathcal{A}$ is the set of balanced expert-token assignments.\n\n**D2 (Loss Function):** $L_{EC}(\\theta) = \\frac{1}{N} \\sum_{e=1}^{E} \\sum_{i=1}^{k} \\ell(f_{\\theta_e}(\\mathbf{x}_{\\sigma_e(i)}), y_{\\sigma_e(i)})$ where $f_{\\theta_e}$ is expert $e$'s sub-network. Only the top-$k$ tokens per expert contribute to the loss.\n\n**D3 (R_{an,exp} Definability):** The argsort operation is piecewise-definable: for each pair $(i,j)$, the comparison $g_\\theta^e(\\mathbf{x}_i) > g_\\theta^e(\\mathbf{x}_j)$ is a definable predicate in $R_{an,exp}$ (polynomial inequality on router logits). The permutation $\\sigma_e$ is definable as a composition of definable comparisons. The top-$k$ selection $\\{\\sigma_e(1), \\ldots, \\sigma_e(k)\\}$ is definable. The expert sub-networks $f_{\\theta_e}$ are standard definable maps (affine + activation). Thus $L_{EC} \\in R_{an,exp}$.\n\n**D4 (Permutation Singular Locus):** The singular locus $V_{perm}$ consists of parameter configurations where the sort order is ambiguous — i.e., where two tokens tie at a rank boundary:\n$$V_{perm} = \\bigcup_{e=1}^{E} \\bigcup_{i=1}^{N-1} \\left\\{ \\theta : g_\\theta^e(\\mathbf{x}_{\\sigma_e(i)}) = g_\\theta^e(\\mathbf{x}_{\\sigma_e(i+1)}) \\right\\}$$\nFor each expert $e$ and adjacent pair $(i, i+1)$, the tie locus is a codimension-1 hypersurface in router parameter space (transversality assumption). The capacity-boundary subset $V_{perm}^{(k)} = \\{\\theta : g_\\theta^e(\\mathbf{x}_{\\sigma_e(k)}) = g_\\theta^e(\\mathbf{x}_{\\sigma_e(k+1)})\\}$ is the critical stratum boundary.\n\n**D5 (Singular Fiber):** The singular fiber over the EC routing map is:\n$$W_{EC} = \\{\\theta \\in \\Theta : \\text{Fisher}(L_{EC}, \\theta) \\text{ is degenerate}\\} \\cup V_{perm}$$\nThis includes both the standard Fisher-degeneracy fibers (analogous to any neural network) and the permutation-tie strata.\n\n**D6 (RLCT Candidate):** $\\lambda_{EC} = \\lambda_{local} + \\lambda_{perm} + \\lambda_{capacity}$ where:\n- $\\lambda_{local}$: RLCT of the expert sub-networks (standard, as in dense models)\n- $\\lambda_{perm}$: contribution from permutation strata (sorting ties). Under transversality, each tie hypersurface contributes a codimension-1 stratum with local RLCT $\\leq 1$. With $E \\cdot (N-1)$ potential ties but most non-degenerate, $\\lambda_{perm} \\leq E \\cdot (N-1) \\cdot 1$ but is effectively much smaller.\n- $\\lambda_{capacity}$: contribution from the capacity cutoff stratum. This is a subset of $V_{perm}^{(k)}$ — the stratum where the $k$-th and $(k+1)$-th tokens tie. $\\lambda_{capacity}$ captures the singularity from the hard cutoff.\n\n**D7 (Stratum Selector):** The capacity $k$ acts as a stratum selector: $k \\mapsto \\mathcal{S}_k$ where $\\mathcal{S}_k$ is the singular stratum structure determined by the top-$k$ cutoff. Increasing $k$ merges strata (more tokens assigned, fewer boundary effects); decreasing $k$ fragments strata. The optimal $k^* \\sim n^{1/(2\\lambda_{EC}+1)}$ balances capacity fragmentation against expert specialization.\n\n## Claims\n\n**C1 (PROVEN):** $L_{EC} \\in R_{an,exp}$. The argsort is piecewise-definable via definable comparisons; top-$k$ selection is definable; expert sub-networks are definable. Closure under composition preserves definability. \\square\n\n**C2 (CONJECTURAL):** The permutation singularity $V_{perm}$ creates a codimension-1 stratum at each sorting tie, forming a hyperplane arrangement in router logit space. This permutation stratum is absent in token-choice MoE (#434), where gating is continuous (softmax) without discrete sorting.\n\n**C3 (CONJECTURAL):** $\\lambda_{EC} < \\lambda_{TC}$ (token-choice MoE) because EC routing eliminates the load-balancing auxiliary loss singularity present in #434.
