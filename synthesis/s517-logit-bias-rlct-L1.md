#517 L1 FORMALIZATION (2026-10-22) Logit Bias × RLCT. D1 (setup): input x, logits z_i(x;w) analytic in w (R_{an,exp}); bias b in R^n fixed or learned; biased probabilities p_i(b) = exp(z_i + b_i)/sum_j exp(z_j + b_j). D2 (decoding-side family): map T_b: z -> z + b is affine translation on logit space R^n; softmax S: R^n -> Int(n-1) simplex interior; composite S o T_b analytic in z and b, gauge freedom b_i += c, sum_i b_i = 0 fixes slice. D3 (strata on simplex): level sets L_p = {q in simplex: S(z)+... } — biased level sets {z: S(z + b) = p} are affinely displaced copies of unbiased ones; stratum Sigma_b = singular fiber of loss over biased distribution. D4 (training-side): logit-adjusted loss L_b(w) = -log p_{y+b}(x; w) reparameterizable (Menon et al.) into equivalent standard form with transformed targets; piecewise-analytic when b has ties at argmax. D5 (translation class): b acts as continuous dial on singular locus: if S_loc(w) is the critical set of L_0, the critical set of L_b is S_loc translated in fiber space; analog of b_l (#514), s (#513), beta (#515) — the fourth member of the dial family. D6 (dual structure): decoding-side b leaves training fiber geometry untouched: lambda_train(b=const-decode) = lambda_train; sampling distribution over strata shifts only. D7 (desingularization): at infinity-biased logits (b_i -> inf) softmax collapses to one-hot dirac — fold-type singular boundary of level-set family; training-side adjustment can raise the singular locus rank by absorbing class-boundary singularities into smooth translation. C1 PROVEN (stv 1.0 0.95): S(z + b) is finite composition of exp, sum, quotient — analytic in (w, b); logit-adjusted loss is -log of analytic positive function on the simplex interior, definable in R_{an,exp}; finite RLCT by resolution of singularities (standard class, cf. #512/#515/#514). C2 CONJECTURAL (stv 0.6 0.8): decoding-side b is a stratum selector that shifts sampled mass without changing training-time lambda — mechanism: gauge-fixed affine displacement of level sets; gaps: no rigorous transport theorem from level-set shift to sampled-stratum distribution. C3 CONJECTURAL (stv 0.5 0.75): training-side logit adjustment shifts lambda via stratum displacement and can reduce it by desingularization when b separates near-coincident singular strata; gap: lambda(b) ordering unproven, U-shape of |b| plausible by #289/#282 analogy.
