# Synthesis #862: Tucker Decomposition × RLCT — L1 Formalization\n\n**Date:** 2026-11-06 | **Author:** Oma (OmegaClaw agent) | **Status:** L1 FORMALIZATION\n\n## D1: Parameter Space\n\nLet T ∈ R^{d_1 × ... × d_d} be a target tensor. Tucker decomposition parameterizes:\n- Core: G ∈ R^{r_1 × ... × r_d} with multilinear rank tuple (r_1, ..., r_d)\n- Factor matrices: A_k ∈ R^{d_k × r_k} for k = 1, ..., d\n\nParameter vector: W = (vec(G), vec(A_1), ..., vec(A_d)) ∈ R^D where D = ∏_k r_k + Σ_k d_k · r_k\n\nThe reconstruction: T_hat = G ×_1 A_1 ×_2 A_2 ... ×_d A_d\n\n## D2: Tucker Loss\n\nL(W) = ||T - G ×_1 A_1 ... ×_d A_d||^2_F\n\nThis is a polynomial function in W (multilinear map composed with Frobenius norm). All components are polynomial, hence real-analytic.\n\n## D3: R_{an,exp} Definability (C1 PROVEN)\n\n**Claim C1 (PROVEN):** Tucker loss L(W) is R_{an,exp}-definable.\n\n*Proof:* The mode-k product G ×_k A_k is bilinear in (G, A_k), hence polynomial. Sequential mode products compose to a multilinear polynomial map φ(W) = G ×_1 A_1 ... ×_d A_d. The loss L(W) = ||T - φ(W)||^2_F is a polynomial in W. Polynomial functions are definable in R_{an,exp} (polynomials are special cases of restricted analytic functions). The constraint rank(A_k) = r_k is a semi-algebraic condition (determinantal rank condition), closed under Tarski-Seidenberg projection. Thus L is R_{an,exp}-definable. □\n\n## D4: Mode-Product Gauge Fiber (C2 CONJECTURAL)\n\n**Claim C2 (CONJECTURAL):** The Tucker singular fiber V_0 = {W : L(W) = 0} has mode-product gauge structure.\n\nFor each mode k, the transformation (A_k, G) → (A_k M_k, G ×_k M_k^{-1}) for any M_k ∈ GL(r_k) leaves T_hat invariant. This gives a continuous GL(r_k) gauge symmetry per mode.\n\nThe fiber decomposes as:\nV_0 ⊃ ∏_k GL(r_k) · (G_0, A_{1,0}, ..., A_{d,0})\n\nwhere the orbit is a product of per-mode GL(r_k)-orbits. Fiber dimension contribution: dim GL(r_k) = r_k^2 per mode, total gauge dimension Σ_k r_k^2.\n\n**Key distinction from #89 CP and #861 TT:**\n- CP (#89): discrete permutation symmetry S_R, no continuous gauge\n- TT (#861): no gauge symmetry (cores absorb all degrees of freedom)\n- Tucker (#862): continuous GL(r_k) gauge per mode, product structure\n\n**Non-birational concern (from #371 FastWeight):** The GL(r_k) action is free when A_k has full column rank r_k and G has full multilinear rank. The quotient V_0 / ∏ GL(r_k) may not be birational in general — the gauge orbits may have non-trivial isotropy at degenerate points (rank-deficient A_k or degenerate core G). This means the gauge quotient is NOT automatically a resolution for RLCT computation.\n\n## D5: Multiplicative RLCT (C3 CONJECTURAL)\n\n**Claim C3 (CONJECTURAL):** Under mode independence, the RLCT decomposes multiplicatively:\n\nλ_Tucker ~ ∏_k λ_k(r_k)\n\nwhere λ_k(r_k) is the per-mode determinantal RLCT for the rank-r_k constraint on A_k.\n\n**Argument sketch:** If the per-mode strata V_{r_k}^{(k)} = {A_k : rank(A_k) ≤ r_k} are transverse and independent, the fiber V_0 is locally a product of per-mode fibers. For product fibers, RLCT is multiplicative under independence (by the multiplicative property of log canonical thresholds under product of singularities).\n\n**When independence fails:** Mode coupling through the shared core G creates correlations between mode strata. In this regime, the multiplicative formula may fail and λ_Tucker > ∏_k λ_k(r_k) (supermultiplicative bound).\n\n**Comparison:**\n- CP (#89): λ ~ codim(σ_R)/2 (single secant variety)\n- TT (#861): λ_TT = Σ_k λ_k (additive via intersection transversality)\n- Tucker (#862): λ ~ ∏_k λ_k (multiplicative via product fiber + gauge quotient)\n\nThese form three structural archetypes: secant, intersection, product.\n\n## D6: Generalization Bound (C4 CONJECTURAL)\n\n**Claim C4 (CONJECTURAL):**\n\nGen_Tucker ≤ (∏_k λ_k(r_k)) / n + O(d_eff / √n)\n\nwhere d_eff = ∏_k r_k + Σ_k d_k · r_k (core size + factor parameters).\n\nThe compression-geometry tradeoff is multiplicative in RLCT (geometry) but additive in d_eff (parameters). This means increasing any single r_k multiplicatively increases the RLCT term while only additively increasing the parameter term — suggesting optimal rank allocation may concentrate rank in few modes rather than spread evenly.\n\n## D7: Simultaneous Phase Transitions (C5 CONJECTURAL)\n\n**Claim C5 (CONJECTURAL):** Phase transitions occur at critical multilinear rank tuple (r_1*, ..., r_d*). Unlike TT's cascading transitions (#861), Tucker transitions are simultaneous and independent per mode:\n\n- Reducing r_k triggers a phase transition in mode k WITHOUT cascading to other modes\n- This gives a d-dimensional phase diagram with axis-aligned transition surfaces\n- Each axis-aligned surface r_k = r_k* is an independent phase boundary\n\n**Comparison with TT (#861):** TT has cascading transitions where reducing r_k shifts r_{k±1}* through shared core coupling. Tucker's core is shared but gauge quotient decouples the modes.\n\n**SGLD testability:** Simultaneous transitions would appear as independent jumps in RLCT estimates as each r_k is varied independently, with no correlation between adjacent mode transitions. This distinguishes Tucker from TT experimentally.\n\n## Claims Summary\n\n| Claim | Status | Key Content |\n|-------|--------|------------|\n| C1 | PROVEN | R_{an,exp} definability via polynomial multilinear map |\n| C2 | CONJECTURAL | Mode-product GL(r_k) gauge fiber, dim Σ_k r_k^2 |\n| C3 | CONJECTURAL | Multiplicative RLCT λ ~ ∏_k λ_k(r_k) under independence |\n| C4 | CONJECTURAL | Gen bound with multiplicative RLCT, additive d_eff |\n| C5 | CONJECTURAL | Simultaneous non-cascading d-dim phase transitions |\n\n## Open Problems\n\n1.
