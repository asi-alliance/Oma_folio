# Synthesis #609: Sophia Optimizer (Diagonal Hessian) × RLCT — L0\n## Date: 2026-10-25\n## Status: L0 Problem Statement\n\n### Background\nSophia (Second-order Clipped Stochastic Optimization) uses a diagonal estimate h_t of the Hessian matrix as a preconditioner. The diagonal Hessian is estimated every k steps via Gauss-Newton approximation (hut = g_t ⊙ g_t for Hutchinson) and maintained via EMA: h_t = beta_h * h_{t-1} + (1-beta_h) * diag(g_t ⊙ g_t). Updates: theta_{t+1} = theta_t - lr * clip(m_t / max(h_t, eps), tau) where m_t is gradient EMA. Key features: (1) diagonal Hessian as curvature proxy, (2) clipping mechanism, (3) EMA smoothing of both gradient and Hessian.\n\n### Five Claims\n- C1 (PROVABLE): Sophia update map is R_{an,exp}-definable. Components: EMA (linear recurrence), elementwise division (R_an definable on positive reals), clipping (max/min = semi-algebraic), Gauss-Newton Hessian estimate (polynomial). All compositions preserve definability.\n- C2 (CONJECTURAL): Diagonal Hessian h_t approximates the Fisher information diagonal, which encodes the local geometry of the singular fiber. The diagonal approximation projects the full Hessian H(w) onto a coordinate-aligned submatrix, creating a "diagonal fiber" D_0 whose RLCT lambda_diag relates to but differs from the full lambda. Under transversality, lambda_Sophia <= lambda_full because diagonal preconditioning restricts stratum exploration to axis-aligned directions.\n- C3 (CONJECTURAL): The clipping parameter tau acts as a stratum selector. Small tau confines updates to low-curvature (flat) strata (conservative exploration). Large tau permits high-curvature (singular) strata. Critical tau* marks phase transition where clipping becomes inactive, equivalent to trust-region boundary crossing a singular fiber.\n- C4 (CONJECTURAL): Generalization bound: L_gen^Sophia <= L_train + C*lambda_Sophia/n + B_Sophia/sqrt(n), where B_Sophia includes diagonal approximation bias B_diag = ||H - diag(H)||_F / ||H||_F (off-diagonal curvature lost). Optimal tau minimizes lambda_Sophia/n + B_diag/sqrt(n), trading stratum depth for approximation fidelity.\n- C5 (CONJECTURAL): Hessian update frequency k creates a k-step piecewise-constant curvature fiber. Phase transition at k* ~ n^{1/(lambda+1)}: below k*, curvature estimate is stale and stratum selection is inaccurate; above k*, Hessian tracks fiber geometry faithfully. Distinct from #606 RMSProp (gradient-only EMA) and #516 K-FAC (full structured preconditioner).\n\n### Distinctions from Prior Syntheses\n- #516 (Preconditioned Optimizers/K-FAC): K-FAC uses full Kronecker-factored Fisher. Sophia uses diagonal only — rank-1 projection of Fisher. Different fiber geometry (diagonal vs Kronecker-factored).\n- #415 (Muon): Newton-Schulz orthogonalization projects onto O(d). Sophia uses EMA of Hessian diagonal (second-order, includes off-diagonal curvature info lost).\n- #289 (Adam): Full diagonal preconditioning via gradient second moment. Sophia's Hessian diagonal includes curvature from loss landscape, not just gradient variance.\n\n### Seven Open Problems\n1.
