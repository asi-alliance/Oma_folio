By mixing features h_i, h_j at layer k with coefficient lambda, Manifold Mixup creates layer-indexed zero-loss sets V_{k,lambda} whose geometry depends on both the interpolation point k and mixing coefficient lambda. Key insight: layer index k acts as a discrete stratum selector on the singular fiber, with deeper k exposing higher-dimensional feature manifolds and thus richer singular structure. The effective RLCT averages over (k, lambda) pairs weighted by a Beta(alpha, alpha) distribution, yielding lambda_MM as a stratum-weighted geometric mean of per-layer RLCTs.\n\n## Claim Status\n\n| Claim | Description | Status | STV |\n|-------|-------------|--------|-----|\n| C1 | R_{an,exp}-definability of layer-k mixup loss via CE + linear interpolation + expectation | PROVEN | (1.0, 0.95) |\n| C2 | Layer-indexed fiber V_{k,lambda} with interior singular strata from rank-deficient Jacobians | CONJECTURAL | (0.6, 0.7) |\n| C3 | k as discrete stratum selector: lambda_k increasing in k (deeper = richer singular structure) | CONJECTURAL | (0.6, 0.75) |\n| C4 | Gen bound: lambda_MM·log(n)/(2n) + U_MM/sqrt(n) with lambda_MM <= lambda_CE | CONJECTURAL | (0.55, 0.7) |\n| C5 | Phase transition at k*: discontinuous LLC when dominant singular stratum shifts across layers | CONJECTURAL | (0.5, 0.7) |\n\n## Novelty Highlights\n\n1. **C2 — Interior singular fiber from rank deficiency**: At intermediate layers, the classifier head Jacobian becomes rank-deficient on the mixed feature manifold, creating singular strata absent in input-space Mixup.
