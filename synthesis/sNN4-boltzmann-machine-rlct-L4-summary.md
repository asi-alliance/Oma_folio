# Synthesis #SNN4: Boltzmann Machines × RLCT — L4 Summary\n\n## Model\nRestricted Boltzmann Machines (RBMs): visible units v ∈ {0,1}^V, hidden units h ∈ {0,1}^H, parameters w=(a,b,W). Energy E(v,h;w) = -a^T v - b^T h - v^T W h. Loss = negative log-likelihood with partition functions Z_h(v;w) and Z(w).\n\n## Claims Status\n- **C1 PROVEN**: R_{an,exp} definability. Polynomial energy + finite exp sum + log closure. Standard.\n- **C2 CONJECTURAL (PARTIALLY OVERLAPPED)**: Three-stratum hierarchy (permutation S_H, dead-unit rank deficiency, coupling-bias gauge redundancy). Dead-unit rank deficiency RLCT already computed exactly by Aoyagi 2010 via r*(d_in+d_out-r)/2. Coupling-bias gauge redundancy survives as novel.\n- **C3 CONJECTURAL (NOVEL)**: H/V ratio as continuous stratum selector. Phase transition at H* ≈ V_eff (effective data rank). Below H*: all units active, regular λ. Above H*: dead-unit stratum dominates, λ jumps by V/2 per dead unit. Organizational novelty — Aoyagi computes fixed-rank RLCT but does not discuss model selection or phase transitions across H.\n- **C4 CONJECTURAL (STANDARD)**: Gen bound λ(H)/n + U(H)/√n with optimal H*. Standard SLT bound form.\n- **C5 CONJECTURAL (NOVEL)**: Inverse temperature β as desingularization parameter. β→0: Gaussian-like smooth regime, λ→d/2. β→∞: discrete singular structure exposed. β-H phase diagram is genuinely new — not analyzed by Aoyagi or standard SLT treatments.\n\n## Key Novelty\n1. **Coupling-bias gauge redundancy**: scaling w_ij ↔ adjusting a_i, b_j creates continuous gauge fiber not covered by Aoyagi's rank analysis\n2. **H/V stratum selector**: reframing Aoyagi's fixed-rank formula as a phase diagram over H/V with model-selection implications\n3. **β desingularization**: temperature as resolution parameter, β-dependent RLCT phase diagram\n4. **SGLD staircase**: LLC steps of V/2 as H crosses H*, experimentally testable\n\n## Overlap\nAoyagi (2010, JMLR) computes exact RBM RLCT = r*(d_in+d_out-r)/2 via ideal inclusion + blowup.
