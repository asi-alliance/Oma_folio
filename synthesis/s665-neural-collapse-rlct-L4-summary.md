This is not a coincidence — it is the global optimum of cross-entropy in the terminal phase.\n\nThe key insight: this ETF convergence creates a genuine singularity in the loss landscape. When K classes sit in a d_h-dimensional hidden space (K < d_h), the ETF orbit has a positive-dimensional stabilizer O(d_h-K+1). This means infinitely many parameter configurations map to the same ETF geometry — a classic Watanabe singularity.\n\nSingular Learning Theory tells us that at such singularities, the Real Log Canonical Threshold (RLCT, λ) — not the Fisher information rank — governs generalization difficulty. This synthesis formalizes the ETF singular fiber, conjectures how λ scales with class count K, and predicts a phase transition detectable via SGLD.\n\n## Claims Recap\n\n**C1 (PROVEN):** The NC-constrained loss is definable in R_an,exp.
