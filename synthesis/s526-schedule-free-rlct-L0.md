# Synthesis #526: Schedule-Free Optimizer × RLCT — L0 Exploration\n\nDate: 2026-10-23\nCandidate: Schedule-Free Optimizer (Defazio et al. 2024, "The Road Less Scheduled")\n\n## Mechanism\n\nSchedule-Free SGD replaces learning rate scheduling with iterate averaging. Maintains three sequences:\n- x_t: primal (raw SGD iterates), x_{t+1} = x_t - η·∇L(x_t)\n- y_t: averaged (output), y_t = (1-c_t)·y_{t-1} + c_t·x_t\n- z_t: momentum, z_t = (1-β)·z_{t-1} + β·∇L(x_t)\n\nThe β parameter interpolates: β=1 → primal averaging (y tracks x directly), β=0 → Polyak-Ruppert averaging (y is cumulative average of all x_t). Output is y_t (the averaged point), gradients computed at x_t (the primal).\n\nKey: NO learning rate decay schedule. Averaging replaces scheduling as the variance-reduction mechanism.\n\n## Bridge to RLCT\n\nThe averaged point y_t is a convex combination of primal iterates {x_1,...,x_t}. Each x_i lies somewhere in the loss landscape — potentially on different singular fibers or even different strata. The averaging y_t = Σ c_i·x_i is a weighted linear combination, which (per #394 SWA) is NOT fiber-preserving in general: singular fibers are typically non-convex, so y_t may lie on a different stratum than any individual x_i.\n\nDistinction from #394 SWA: SWA averages over a cyclical LR orbit (discrete set of checkpoints), Schedule-Free averages continuously over the entire trajectory with decaying weights c_t. SWA's orbit is periodic (cyclical LR), Schedule-Free's trajectory is aperiodic (constant LR). SWA uses uniform weights, Schedule-Free uses PR weights (c_t ~ 1/t for β=0) or primal weights (c_t=1 for β=1).\n\nDistinction from #289 EMA: EMA uses exponential decay weights (fixed decay α). Schedule-Free's PR weights decay as 1/t (non-exponential, heavier tail). This means Schedule-Free retains more influence from early iterates than EMA, potentially sampling a wider range of strata.\n\nDistinction from #420 Lookahead: Lookahead averages k-step forward explorations from a slow point. Schedule-Free averages the full trajectory from a fixed starting point.\n\n## Candidate Claims\n\nC1 (Definability): The Schedule-Free update is R_{an,exp}-definable. Primal: x_{t+1} = x_t - η·∇L(x_t) is definable if L is (#8,#16). Averaging: y_t = (1-c_t)·y_{t-1} + c_t·x_t with c_t definable (c_t = 1/(t+1) for PR, or polynomial in t). The averaging map is affine = definable. Composition closure: t-fold composition of definable maps is definable (per #485 RLT, #363 adaLN). PROVEN.\n\nC2 (Averaged RLCT): λ_SF ≤ λ_SGD. The averaged point y_t samples a convex hull of trajectory points. By the same non-fiber-preservation argument as #394, y_t can cross stratum boundaries. The convex hull of an orbit explores more of the loss landscape than any single point. If the trajectory visits multiple strata with λ_i, the averaged point's effective RLCT is λ_eff ≤ min_i λ_i (weighted averaging over strata). CONJECTURAL — but note #289 EMA weakly falsified this for exponential averaging on a toy model. Schedule-Free's heavier-tail PR weights may behave differently.\n\nC3 (β as stratum exploration dial): β controls the effective averaging window. β=1 (primal): y_t ≈ x_t, no averaging, λ_eff ≈ λ_SGD. β=0 (PR): maximum averaging, y_t ≈ (1/t)Σx_i, explores widest stratum range, λ_eff most reduced. Intermediate β: partial exploration. β acts as a continuous dial for stratum exploration breadth. Critical β* may exist where averaging window matches the stratum crossing timescale. CONJECTURAL.\n\nC4 (Generalization bound): Gen error ≤ λ_eff(β)·log(n)/(2n) + bias(β)/√n. Bias increases with averaging breadth (PR averaging introduces lag bias), variance decreases via averaging. Optimal β* minimizes the sum. CONJECTURAL.\n\nC5 (Phase transition): At critical β*, the LLC (measured via SGLD on y_t) shows a cusp — transition from SGD-like singularity (high λ) to averaged/desingularized (low λ). Testable: run Schedule-Free at various β, measure LLC of y_t endpoint. CONJECTURAL.\n\n## Open Problems\n\n1. Why did #289 EMA fail to reduce LLC?
