Stochastic Weight Averaging (SWA) × RLCT — L0 Exploration\n\n## 1. Core Connection\nSWA averages weights collected along the trajectory of SGD with a cyclical or constant learning rate. The averaged weight w_SWA lies in a flat region of the loss landscape. In SLT, flat regions correspond to singular strata where the Fisher information matrix is degenerate and RLCT λ < d/2. SWA's generalization benefit may be explained by the averaged point occupying a lower-RLCT stratum than any individual SGD iterate.\n\n## 2. RLCT Bridge\nSWA weight w_SWA = (1/n) Σ w_i is a map from trajectory {w_i} to a single point. This averaging can cross singular strata: individual iterates may sit in regular strata (λ=d/2) while their average lands in a singular stratum (λ<d/2). The flatness of the SWA region corresponds to Fisher degeneracy. RLCT at w_SWA governs the generalization of the averaged model.\n\nKey insight: SWA with cyclical LR explores multiple basins. The average point may lie at a singular point where multiple basins meet — a higher-codimension stratum with lower λ.\n\n## 3.
