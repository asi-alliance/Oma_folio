# Synthesis #252: Test-Time Training (TTT) × RLCT\n## Date: 2026-10-26\n## Status: L0 EXPLORATION\n\n## Hypothesis\n\nTest-Time Training (TTT) updates a subset of model parameters (fast weights) via inner-loop gradient descent on a self-supervised objective during inference. This creates a transient trajectory in parameter space that crosses singular stratum boundaries. The RLCT of the TTT-adapted model differs from the base model, and this delta captures the geometric cost of adaptation.\n\nKey insight: Fast weights W_t are updated by k steps of SGD on a self-supervised loss L_ssl(x, W). The trajectory {W_0, W_1, ..., W_k} may cross from one singular stratum to another. At singular points, the local geometry changes qualitatively — directions that were degenerate become active, or vice versa. The RLCT lambda(W_k) reflects which stratum the adapted model lands on.\n\n## Background\n\n- **TTT Layers** (Sun et al. 2024/2025): Replace fixed RNN hidden states with learnable fast weights. Inner model f(W; x) updated via self-supervised reconstruction loss. TTT-Linear (linear inner model) and TTT-MLP (MLP inner model) variants.\n- **Outer loop**: Learn the self-supervised task and initialize inner model weights. This is meta-learning — learning how to learn at test time.\n- **Inner loop**: k steps of SGD on L_ssl using the current input batch. Only fast weights W change; main model weights theta remain frozen.\n- **RLCT**: Real Log Canonical Threshold from Singular Learning Theory. Measures the complexity of the loss landscape near singular points. Lower RLCT = flatter, more degenerate = better generalization (under Watanabe's generalization bound).\n\n## Five Candidate Claims\n\n**C1 (Definability) — PROVABLE**: The TTT loss landscape L_total = L_main(theta, W_k) + alpha * L_ssl(x, W_0) where W_k = W_0 - eta * sum_{t=0}^{k-1} grad_W L_ssl(x_t, W_t) is definable in R_{an,exp}. The k-fold composition of gradient steps is analytic (gradient of analytic is analytic), and the outer loss is piecewise-analytic for ReLU networks. R_{an,exp} is closed under composition and addition.\n\n**C2 (Stratum Crossing) — CONJECTURAL**: The fast-weight trajectory W_0 -> W_k crosses singular stratum boundaries in the parameter space. Crossing occurs when the inner-loop update activates or deactivates a ReLU unit (changes sign of pre-activation), which corresponds to moving between strata in the ReLU stratification. The probability of crossing depends on k (number of inner steps), eta (learning rate), and the geometry of the base model's singular neighborhood. More singular base models (lower RLCT) have more strata nearby, hence higher crossing probability.\n\n**C3 (RLCT Delta) — CONJECTURAL**: The RLCT of the adapted model lambda(W_k) differs from lambda(W_0). If the trajectory crosses to a MORE singular stratum, lambda(W_k) < lambda(W_0) — adaptation improved generalization geometry. If it crosses to a LESS singular stratum, lambda(W_k) > lambda(W_0) — adaptation degraded geometry. The expected RLCT delta E[lambda(W_0) - lambda(W_k)] is positive when TTT helps (moves toward more singular strata) and negative when TTT hurts (distribution shift too large, overshoots to less singular strata). The inner step count k acts as a continuous (in the large-k limit) stratum selector.\n\n**C4 (Generalization Bound) — CONJECTURAL**: Watanabe's generalization bound for the TTT-adapted model:\nG_TTT(n) <= lambda(W_k) * log(n) / (2n) + U(W_k)/sqrt(n) + B_ssl(k)\nwhere B_ssl(k) is a bias term from the self-supervised objective mismatch. The key prediction: there exists an optimal k* where lambda(W_k*) * log(n)/(2n) + B_ssl(k*) is minimized. Below k*, the model hasn't adapted enough (lambda still high). Above k*, the SSL objective bias dominates (B_ssl grows with k as the model overfits the self-supervised task). This gives a U-shaped generalization curve in k.\n\n**C5 (Phase Transition) — CONJECTURAL**: There exists a critical distribution shift magnitude delta* such that for delta < delta*, TTT reduces RLCT (helps), and for delta > delta*, TTT increases RLCT (hurts). At delta = delta*, the fast-weight trajectory crosses from a helpful singular stratum to an unhelpful one. This phase transition is testable via SGLD LLC estimation on models before and after TTT adaptation, under controlled distribution shift magnitudes.\n\n## Open Problems\n1.
