The radius parameter epsilon controls neighborhood size.\n\nThis synthesis formalizes DBSCAN through the lens of Singular Learning Theory (SLT) and the Real Log Canonical Threshold (RLCT).\n\n## Claims\n\nC1 (PROVEN): The DBSCAN surrogate loss -- a piecewise-constant function built from polynomial distance inequalities, finite counting, and graph connectivity -- is definable in R_{an,exp}.
