L0-L4 COMPLETE.\n\n## What is Mish?\nMish is a smooth activation function: Mish(x) = x · tanh(softplus(x)) = x · tanh(ln(1 + e^x)). It combines two transcendental functions (tanh and softplus) into a double composition, producing a smooth curve with a unique non-monotonic dip around x ≈ -1.088.\n\n## Why connect it to Singular Learning Theory?\nSLT predicts that the singularities of a neural network's loss landscape — the points where the Fisher information matrix becomes degenerate — govern generalization through the RLCT (Real Log Canonical Threshold). Different activation functions create different singularity structures. Mish had never been analyzed through this lens.\n\n## Key Results\n\n### C1 (PROVEN): Mish is analyzable under SLT\nMish is definable in R_an,exp (the class of functions closed under exponentials, logarithms, and analytic operations). This means Watanabe's framework applies. The proof follows from closure properties: e^x is definable, ln is definable, softplus = ln(1+e^x) is definable, tanh is definable, and their composition is definable.\n\n### C2 (CONJECTURAL): Three-component singular fiber\nUnlike ReLU (algebraic kink), GELU (single transcendental via erf), and SiLU (single transcendental via sigmoid), Mish creates a singular fiber with THREE components:\n- (a) Simple zero at x=0: same as GELU/SiLU, RLCT = 1/2\n- (b) Critical point at x* ≈ -1.088: Mish'(x*)=0 but Mish(x*)≠0. This is NOVEL — no prior activation has an interior critical point. It creates a singular stratum absent in all previously analyzed activations.\n- (c) Exponential tail: Mish ≈ x·e^x → 0 as x → -∞, contributing a tail RLCT term.\n\n### C3 (CONJECTURAL): Double transcendental singularity hierarchy\nThe smoothness class of an activation determines its singularity class:\n- ReLU: algebraic → λ_local = 1, no tail, no critical point\n- GELU/SiLU: single transcendental → λ_local = 1/2, tail term\n- Mish: double transcendental → λ_local = 1/2 + λ_critical (novel) + λ_tail\n\nPrediction: λ_Mish > λ_GELU if λ_critical > 0. But the dip may regularize through singular stratum exploration during training.\n\n### C4 (CONJECTURAL): Generalization bound with λ_critical\nG_Mish(n) ≤ λ_Mish·log(n)/(2n) + U_Mish/√n + C_Mish where λ_Mish = λ_local + λ_critical + λ_tail.\nThe λ_critical·log(n)/(2n) term is absent in all prior activations. For shallow networks, λ_local dominates and G_Mish ≈ G_GELU. For deep networks, λ_critical may accumulate across layers.\n\n### C5 (CONJECTURAL): Signature prediction — three-phase SGLD LLC trajectory\nMish networks should show a DISTINCT three-phase LLC curve during SGLD training:\n1.
