#670: KV Cache × RLCT — L4 Summary\n\n## Plain-Language Summary\n\nKV caching during autoregressive generation splits the model's parameter space into "live" parameters (receiving gradients: query weights, output projection, FFN, layer norms) and "frozen" parameters (cached K/V projections evaluated at past tokens, held as fixed constants). This creates a **mixed singular fiber**: the true loss landscape's degenerate geometry is explored on a reduced-dimensional subspace, with frozen coordinates pinned to non-optimal values.\n\n### Claims\n\n**C1 (PROVEN):** The cached loss is R_an-definable — the fixed-point evaluation map preserves analytic definability through composition. Standard result; finite RLCT exists.\n\n**C2 (CONJECTURAL):** Effective RLCT decreases under caching (λ_cached ≤ λ_full) because fewer parameters explore the posterior, shrinking the singular fiber dimension. Reduction may not be strict due to frozen-parameter bias.\n\n**C3 (CONJECTURAL):** Effective sequence length n_eff acts as a discrete stratum selector — each additional cached token freezes d_model coordinates, crossing singular stratum boundaries at critical n_eff*.\n\n**C4 (CONJECTURAL):** A frozen-parameter correction C_frozen(n_eff) appears in the generalization bound, growing monotonically as cached values deviate from population optima. Bounded by O(d·σ²/n).\n\n**C5 (CONJECTURAL):** Cache saturation (n > B+1) triggers eviction, performing "fiber surgery" — replacing frozen coordinates discontinuously. This creates a phase transition at budget B* ~ α·n^β where fiber topology changes.\n\n### Key Novelty\nThree genuinely new contributions not in prior #229 or external literature: (1) **mixed singular fiber decomposition** (live/frozen parameter split in fiber geometry), (2) **eviction-as-fiber-surgery** (coordinate replacement as topological surgery on frozen fiber), (3) **C_frozen generalization bound correction** (bias term for non-optimal cached parameters). #229 used data distribution modulation; #670 uses parameter-space geometry.\n\n### Cross-References\n#229 (KV Cache prior, different mechanism), #514 (RazorAttention pruning), #8 (RLCT foundational), #16 (Watanabe bound), #468 (PPO piecewise), #582 (curriculum stratum selector), #653 (TTT dual of caching), #292 (Flash attention invariance), #566 (3DGS frozen field), #605 (Adagrad), #355 (perceptual loss fixed features), #485 (RLT reduced exploration)\n\n### Open Problems\n1.
