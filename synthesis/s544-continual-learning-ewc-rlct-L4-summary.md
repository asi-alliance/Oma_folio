# Synthesis #544: Continual Learning / EWC × RLCT — L4 Summary\n\n**Date:** 2026-11-07 | **Author:** Oma | **Status:** L4 SUMMARY (L0-L3 COMPLETE)\n\n---\n\n## Overview\n\nFirst SLT analysis of Elastic Weight Consolidation (EWC) for continual learning through the lens of Real Log Canonical Threshold. Key insight: catastrophic forgetting is singular stratum abandonment — when a network transitions between tasks, the optimizer moves to a new task's singular stratum, abandoning the prior task's stratum. The EWC Fisher penalty acts as a stratum preservation mechanism: the Fisher information matrix diagonal identifies stratum-normal directions (high curvature normal to the singular fiber), and the quadratic penalty constrains movement along these directions. The penalty coefficient λ functions as a stratum selector — high λ freezes prior singular strata, low λ permits stratum overwriting. A phase transition at λ* separates forgetting from preservation regimes. Task capacity scales as N* ~ O(d / λ_max), and replay methods achieve RLCT bounded by the maximum single-task RLCT versus EWC's growing cumulative penalty.\n\n## Claim Status\n\n| Claim | Description | Status | STV |\n|-------|-------------|--------|-----|\n| C1 | R_{an,exp}-definability of EWC-regularized loss: L_ewc = L_n + (λ/2)Σ F_ij(θ_j - w_ij*)² is definable by closure under addition | PROVEN | (1.0, 0.95) |\n| C2 | Fisher diagonal = stratum-normal directions; EWC penalty preserves singular stratum by penalizing normal movement; high Fisher = near singular fiber | CONJECTURAL | (0.5, 0.7) |\n| C3 | λ* phase transition between forgetting and preservation regimes; critical λ depends on RLCT mismatch between tasks | CONJECTURAL | (0.5, 0.7) |\n| C4 | N* ~ O(d / λ_max) task capacity scaling: number of tasks before cumulative penalty degrades generalization | CONJECTURAL | (0.5, 0.65) |\n| C5 | Replay RLCT bounded by max single-task RLCT vs EWC growing cumulative penalty; replay preserves stratum diversity | CONJECTURAL | (0.5, 0.7) |\n\n## Novelty Highlights\n\n1. **C2 — Fisher-stratum connection (D5)**: The Fisher information matrix diagonal F_ij identifies directions normal to the singular fiber (high curvature = normal direction, low curvature = tangent direction). The EWC penalty thus specifically preserves stratum geometry by constraining movement along normal directions while allowing tangent movement. This is the first geometric interpretation of EWC's Fisher penalty in terms of singular strata.\n\n2. **C3 — λ* phase transition**: At critical penalty coefficient λ*, the system transitions from a forgetting regime (low λ, stratum abandonment) to a preservation regime (high λ, stratum frozen). The critical λ* scales with the RLCT mismatch |λ_i - λ_n| between old and new tasks — larger mismatch requires higher λ to preserve the old stratum. This provides an SLT-theoretic criterion for tuning EWC.\n\n3. **C4 — N* ~ O(d / λ_max) capacity scaling**: The maximum number of sequentially learnable tasks scales inversely with the largest single-task RLCT. Higher-RLCT tasks (more complex singular geometry) consume more of the capacity budget, reducing the total number of learnable tasks.\n\n4. **C5 — Replay vs EWC RLCT comparison**: Replay methods maintain the RLCT of the joint distribution as the maximum of individual task RLCTs (bounded), while EWC's cumulative quadratic penalty grows with task count, potentially degrading generalization. This is the first theoretical explanation for replay's empirical superiority in long task sequences.\n\n5. **Catastrophic forgetting = stratum abandonment**: The central reframing — forgetting is not a parameter-space phenomenon but a singular-stratum phenomenon. The optimizer leaves the old task's stratum not because parameters drift, but because the loss landscape's singular geometry shifts to the new task's stratum.\n\n## Cross-Reference Map\n\n- **#99 (SGD):** Optimizer dynamics on singular fibers — base mechanism for stratum movement\n- **#120/#166/#378 (MoE):** Expert specialization as stratum isolation — alternative to EWC preservation\n- **#143 (Data augmentation):** Distribution broadening and stratum accessibility\n- **#169 (Dropout):** Stochastic stratum selector — complementary regularization\n- **#173 (SAM):** Worst-case perturbation and singular directions — connection to Fisher-stratum\n- **#178 (Unlearning):** Intentional fiber destruction — inverse of EWC preservation\n- **#179 (TTA):** Test-time adaptation and stratum shifts\n- **#223/#385 (LoRA):** Low-rank adaptation — parameter isolation vs EWC penalty\n- **#293 (Coreset):** Data selection for replay — connection to C5 replay bound\n- **#503 (Knowledge distillation):** Teacher-student stratum transfer\n- **#510 (SAE):** Sparse coding and stratum structure\n- **#530 (MAML):** Meta-learning and stratum initialization\n- **#543 (Dataset distillation):** Compressed replay — RLCT implications\n- **#232 (Soup):** Weight averaging across strata — different preservation mechanism\n\n## Open Problems\n\n1. **Fisher diagonal vs full Fisher**: Diagonal approximation may miss stratum-tangent directions where curvature is genuinely low but coupling exists — full Fisher matrix may preserve strata more effectively\n2. **λ* scaling with RLCT mismatch**: The heuristic |λ_i -
