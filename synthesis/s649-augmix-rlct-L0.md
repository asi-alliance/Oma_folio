#649 | AugMix × RLCT | L0: Claims\n\n## Technique\nAugMix (Hendrycks et al. 2020): For input x, sample K=3 augmentation chains, each applying 1-3 random operations (from a set of 14: translates, shear, rotate, posterize, sharpen, etc.) with random intensities. Each chain produces augmented x_k. The final input is a convex combination: x_aug = Σ_k w_k · x_k + w_0 · x, where weights (w_0,...,w_K) ~ Dirichlet(α). A consistency loss L_JS = JS(p(y|x), p(y|x_aug1), p(y|x_aug2)) enforces prediction consistency across augmentations and clean input. Total loss: L = L_CE(y, p(y|x)) + λ · L_JS.\n\n## C1: R_{an,exp}-definability (PROVEN)\nAugmentation operations (translate, shear, rotate, posterize, sharpen, blur, etc.) are semi-algebraic or analytic maps on pixel space. Dirichlet-weighted convex combination is polynomial (linear in weights). JSD divergence: JS(p_0,...,p_K) = H(Σ w_k p_k) - Σ w_k H(p_k) where H is Shannon entropy. Entropy H(p) = -Σ p_i log(p_i) involves log — definable in R_{an,exp} (log is globally subanalytic). JSD is R_{an,exp}-definable. L_CE is definable. Total L = L_CE + λ·L_JS is R_{an,exp}-definable. QED.\n\n## C2: Consistency fiber singularity (CONJECTURAL)\nAugMix creates two intersecting fiber structures:\n(a) Augmentation fibers: each augmentation chain k maps x → x_k, creating K augmentation-transformed loss landscapes with their own singular strata (as in #603 AutoAugment).\n(b) Consistency fiber: the JSD constraint V_cons = {θ : JS(p_θ(·|x), p_θ(·|x_aug_k)) ≈ 0} defines a consistency manifold in parameter space. The zero-loss set of L_JS is where all augmented predictions agree — this is a quotient singularity (collapsing augmentation-orbit predictions to a single distribution).\n\nKey claim: The composite loss L = L_CE + λ·L_JS has singular fiber V = V_CE ∩ V_cons. The consistency term λ controls the dimensionality of this intersection — large λ shrinks V_cons toward a thin consistency manifold, creating a higher-codimension singularity with potentially LOWER RLCT (desingularization via consistency constraint).\n\n## C3: λ_cons as stratum selector (CONJECTURAL)\nThe consistency weight λ in L = L_CE + λ·L_JS acts as a continuous stratum selector:\n- λ → 0: pure CE loss, V = V_CE, λ_RLCT = λ_CE (no consistency constraint)\n- λ → ∞: V = V_cons dominates, trajectory trapped in consistency manifold, λ_RLCT → λ_cons (consistency stratum)\n- λ* optimal: balance between CE descent and consistency regularization\n\nThe Dirichlet α parameter controls augmentation diversity (α → 0: one chain dominates, α → ∞: uniform mixing), providing a SECOND stratum selector orthogonal to λ.\n\n## C4: Generalization bound (CONJECTURAL)\nG(λ, α) = λ_augmix(λ, α) · log(n)/(2n) + B(λ, α)/√n\nwhere B(λ, α) measures the bias from constraining predictions to the consistency manifold — the KL divergence between the unconstrained and consistency-constrained optimal predictors.\n\nKey prediction: λ_augmix(λ, α) ≤ λ_CE for moderate λ — the consistency constraint desingularizes the loss landscape by collapsing augmentation-orbit prediction variance, analogous to how #615 showed consistency regularization lowers RLCT.\n\n## C5: Phase transition at (λ*, α*) (CONJECTURAL)\nSGLD-detectable phase transition:\n- For λ < λ*: LLC constant at λ_CE level (consistency term too weak to bind)\n- For λ > λ*: LLC decreases (consistency constraint active, desingularization)\n- At λ*: kink in LLC trajectory\n- α controls augmentation diversity: low α (homogeneous augmentations) → less effective desingularization; high α (diverse) → stronger\n- Optimal (λ*, α*) minimizes G(λ, α) — interior optimum in both parameters\n\n## Distinction from prior syntheses\n- #603 AutoAugment: policy stratum selection (discrete sub-policy choices). AugMix adds JSD CONSISTENCY LOSS — a fundamentally different mechanism (consistency fiber, not policy boundary singularity).\n- #615 Consistency Regularization: generic consistency loss. AugMix-specific: Dirichlet-weighted multi-chain augmentation + JSD (specific divergence, specific augmentation structure).\n- #143 Data Augmentation: group orbit quotient. AugMix: stochastic chain mixture + consistency — not a group action (operations are not invertible, mixture is stochastic).\n- #258/#589/#402 Mixup: input-space interpolation. AugMix: augmentation-chain mixture + consistency loss (label-space JSD, not input interpolation).\n\n## Open problems\n1.
