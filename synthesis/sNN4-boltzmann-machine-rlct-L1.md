# Synthesis #SNN4: Boltzmann Machines × RLCT — L1 Formalization\n\n## D1: Parameter Space\nLet V visible units v ∈ {0,1}^V, H hidden units h ∈ {0,1}^H. Parameters w = (a, b, W) where a ∈ R^V (visible biases), b ∈ R^H (hidden biases), W ∈ R^{V×H} (coupling weights). Energy: E(v,h;w) = -a^T v - b^T h - v^T W h (polynomial in w).\n\n## D2: Boltzmann Loss (Negative Log-Likelihood)\nNLL: L(w) = -log p(v;w) = -a^T v - log Z_h(v;w) + log Z(w)\nwhere Z_h(v;w) = Σ_h exp(-E(v,h;w)) (hidden marginal), Z(w) = Σ_{v,h} exp(-E(v,h;w)) (full partition).\nComposite: L(w) = (1/n)Σ_k [log Z(w) - a^T v_k - log Z_h(v_k;w)].\n\n## D3: R_{an,exp} Definability (C1 PROVEN)\nE(v,h;w) is polynomial in w for fixed (v,h). Z_h(v;w) = Σ_h exp(poly(w)) is finite sum of exp(polynomial) → definable. Z(w) = Σ_{v,h} exp(poly(w)) finite sum → definable. log of definable positive function is definable. NLL is arithmetic combination of definable functions → L ∈ R_{an,exp}. QED.\n\n## D4: Singular Fiber — Dead-Unit and Permutation Strata\nV_0 = {w : L(w) = L(w*)} = true parameter set. Multiple singular strata:\n- S_perm: w and σ(w) identical for σ ∈ S_H (hidden permutation). Stabilizer strata have positive dimension when units coincide.\n- S_dead(k): k hidden units have |w_{·j}| = 0 and b_j = 0. Dead unit j contributes constant factor to Z, creating fiber of dimension V+1 per dead unit. RLCT locally λ_dead(k) = (V·k)/2 (each dead unit removes V parameters from active set, reducing effective dimension by V/2 in RLCT).\n- S_redundant: two units j,j' have proportional weight columns w_{·j} = c·w_{·j'}, continuous gauge (scaling c ↔ bias adjustment).\nDominant stratum = max over active unit count: the minimal-λ stratum uses all H units when H ≤ V_eff, dead-unit strata dominate when H > V_eff.\n\n## D5: H/V Ratio as Stratum Selector\nα_BM = H/V (hidden-to-visible ratio). Strata indexed by effective active units H_eff ≤ H.\n- α_BM < α* ≈ 1: all units active, minimal λ ≈ dim/2 = (V+H+VH)/2 (regular regime).\n- α_BM > α*: excess units die, λ(H) increases by V/2 per dead unit beyond V_eff.\n- Phase transition at H* ≈ V_eff = rank(data covariance). Analogous to Hopfield α_c (#842) and MoE expert collapse (#120).\n\n## D6: Generalization Bound\nG(H,n) ~ λ(H)·log(n)/(2n) + U(H)/√n\n- λ(H): effective RLCT = (dim_active(H))/2 where dim_active counts only non-dead parameters.\n- U(H): estimation variance, grows with total parameters including dead ones (overparameterization cost).\n- Optimal H* minimizes λ(H)·log(n)/n + U(H)/√n: tradeoff between dead-unit RLCT inflation and active-unit estimation cost.\n- Prediction: generalization gap has kink at H* (SGLD-testable).\n\n## D7: Phase Transition at H*\n- H << H*: undercomplete, all units active, λ regular ~ (V+H+VH)/2.\n- H ↑ H*: capacity saturation, units begin competing, redundancy strata emerge.\n- H > H*: dead-unit stratum dominates, λ jumps by V/2 per dead unit. Non-identifiability increases.\n- Temperature β (inverse Boltzmann temperature): acts as desingularization parameter. High β sharpens energy landscape (approaches hard constraint), low β smooths (mean-field). β → 0 reduces to Gaussian-like regime, β → ∞ exposes discrete singular structure.\n- SGLD signature: LLC staircase as H crosses H*, each step = one dead unit activating the V/2 RLCT contribution.\n\n## Claims Status\n- C1 PROVEN: R_{an,exp} definability (polynomial energy + finite exp sum + log closure)\n- C2 CONJECTURAL: dead-unit/permutation/redundancy strata form singular fiber hierarchy\n- C3 CONJECTURAL: H/V as stratum selector with phase transition at H* ≈ V_eff\n- C4 CONJECTURAL: gen bound λ(H)/n + U(H)/√n with optimal H*\n- C5 CONJECTURAL: β as desingularization parameter, β-dependent λ(β,H) phase diagram\n\n## Open Problems\n1.
