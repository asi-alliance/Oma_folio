# Synthesis #678: Polyak Averaging × RLCT\n## L0 Seed — 2026-10-28\n\n### Bridge\nPolyak-Ruppert averaging computes w_avg = (1/T) Σ_{t=1}^{T} w_t, uniformly averaging all iterates from a single SGD trajectory. Unlike SWA (#232, which uses cyclic learning rates to force stratum exploration) or EMA (#289, which exponentially decays old iterates), Polyak averaging weights every iterate equally. In singular learning theory, each iterate w_t lies on or near the singular fiber W_0, but in different local regions of it. The uniform average w_avg is a convex combination of points across multiple singular strata — it may land in a lower-dimensional (lower-RLCT) region of the fiber that individual iterates cannot reach.\n\n### Claims\n- **C1 (PROVEN)**: The Polyak average w_avg = (1/T) Σ w_t is an R_an-definable function of the iterates {w_t}, as finite sums of definable functions are definable.\n- **C2 (CONJECTURAL)**: The Polyak average w_avg lies in a lower-RLCT stratum than typical individual iterates: λ(w_avg) < E_t[λ(w_t)], because averaging across strata projects into the intersection (deepest singular locus).\n- **C3 (CONJECTURAL)**: The averaging window T acts as a stratum selector: larger T → more strata sampled → lower λ(w_avg), up to a saturation point T* beyond which additional iterates add no new stratum information.\n- **C4 (CONJECTURAL)**: Generalization bound: Gen_polyak ≤ C·sqrt(λ_avg·(d-1)/n) where λ_avg = λ(w_avg) < λ_single, giving tighter bound than single-iterate generalization.\n- **C5 (CONJECTURAL)**: Critical window T* ~ O(d/log n) exists beyond which Polyak averaging saturates — all accessible strata have been sampled and further averaging only adds noise.\n\n### Key Insight\nPolyak averaging is the uniform-weight special case of general iterate averaging. Its key SLT property: uniform weights mean early iterates (which may be in high-loss, high-RLCT strata) contribute equally to the average. This is distinct from EMA (which discards early iterates) and SWA (which only averages high-LR-cycle iterates). The uniform average may project into the deepest singular locus — the intersection of all strata visited by the trajectory.\n\n### Distinction from Related Syntheses\n- #232 (SWA × RLCT): SWA uses cyclic learning rates to force exploration; Polyak uses plain SGD trajectory. SWA's averaging window is selective (high-LR cycles); Polyak's is uniform (all iterates).\n- #289 (EMA × RLCT): EMA uses exponential decay weights; Polyak uses uniform weights. EMA's effective window is ~1/(1-α) iterates; Polyak's is exactly T.\n- #677 (Model Soup × RLCT): Model soup averages independent models from different initializations; Polyak averages iterates from one trajectory.\n\n### Open Problems\n1.
