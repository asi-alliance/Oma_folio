#610 AdEMAMix Optimizer × RLCT (Real Log Canonical Threshold)\n\n## L0: Concept & Motivation\n\n### Topic\nAdEMAMix (Adaptive EMA with Mixed time-scale) optimizer × Singular Learning Theory (RLCT).\n\n### AdEMAMix Overview\nAdEMAMix (Pagliardini et al., 2024) extends AdamW by adding a **second, slow-moving EMA** of gradients alongside the standard fast EMA. The update rule is:\n\n  m_t = β1 * m_{t-1} + (1-β1) * g_t          (fast EMA, β1 ~ 0.9)\n  m'_t = β3 * m'_{t-1} + (1-β3) * g_t         (slow EMA, β3 ~ 0.9999)\n  θ_{t+1} = θ_t - η * (m_t / (√v_t + ε) + α * m'_t / (√v_t + ε))\n\nwhere v_t is the standard second-moment estimate (as in AdamW), α is a mixing coefficient, and β3 >> β1 creates a two-timescale momentum system.\n\n### RLCT Bridge (Core Insight)\n\nIn Singular Learning Theory, the RLCT λ measures the geometric complexity of the singular locus — the set of parameters achieving zero loss. The structure of accessible singular strata during training depends on the optimizer's trajectory through parameter space.\n\nAdEMAMix creates a fundamentally novel singular structure:\n\n1. **Two-timescale stratum exploration**: The fast EMA (β1) captures local gradient geometry — it responds quickly and explores the immediate neighborhood of the current parameter configuration. The slow EMA (β3) accumulates gradient history over thousands of steps, effectively maintaining a long-range memory of the loss landscape. This means the optimizer simultaneously samples TWO different effective views of the singular fiber structure:\n   - Fast view: local singular geometry near current θ\n   - Slow view: averaged singular geometry over a long temporal window\n\n2. **Two-fiber superposition**: The additive update m_t + α*m'_t combines two gradient estimates that trace DIFFERENT singular strata. The fast EMA lives on the stratum defined by recent gradients; the slow EMA lives on a time-averaged stratum. Their sum creates a superposition of two singular fiber directions, which has no analog in standard Adam (single EMA) or SGD (raw gradient).\n\n3. **β3 as temporal stratum selector**: The slow decay rate β3 acts as a temporal smoothing parameter that SELECTS which singular stratum is emphasized. Large β3 → very slow EMA → stratum defined by long-term average geometry. Small β3 → slow EMA converges to fast EMA → single-stratum regime. This creates a continuous family of stratum configurations parameterized by β3.\n\n4. **Phase transition at β3***: There should exist a critical β3* at which the slow EMA captures a qualitatively different singular structure than the fast EMA — the two EMAs decorrelate. Below β3*, both EMAs track the same stratum (redundant). Above β3*, they access complementary singular fibers. This phase transition should be detectable via SGLD (Stochastic Gradient Langevin Dynamics) by measuring the RLCT of the two-EMA system vs single-EMA.\n\n5. **α as fiber mixing coefficient**: The mixing coefficient α controls the relative weight of the two fiber contributions. At α=0, AdEMAMix reduces to AdamW (single fiber). At α→∞, the slow EMA dominates (long-range fiber only). The optimal α likely corresponds to balancing the RLCT contributions of both fibers.\n\n### Novelty Claims\n\n- **C1 (Standard-Novel)**: AdEMAMix's two-EMA update is R_{an,exp}-definable, and the RLCT of the resulting training dynamics is well-defined. PROVEN by construction.\n- **C2 (Genuinely Novel)**: The two-fiber superposition structure (fast + slow EMA accessing complementary singular strata) has not been described in SLT literature.\n- **C3 (Genuinely Novel)**: β3 as a temporal stratum selector creating a continuous family of singular fiber configurations is a new concept.\n- **C4 (Genuinely Novel)**: The predicted phase transition at β3* where fast and slow EMAs decorrelate in singular structure is a new, testable prediction.\n- **C5 (Genuinely Novel)**: α as a fiber mixing coefficient balancing RLCT contributions of two strata is a new optimization-geometry connection.\n\n### Key Distinctions from Prior Syntheses\n- **#289 Adam × RLCT**: Single EMA, single fiber. AdEMAMix adds a second, qualitatively different fiber.\n- **#604 One-Cycle LR × RLCT**: Time-varying LR changes desingularization speed but uses single EMA. AdEMAMix changes the FIBER STRUCTURE itself.\n- **#226 EMA Polyak × RLCT**: Polyak averaging operates on parameters post-hoc, not on gradients during optimization.\n- **#394 SWA × RLCT**: Weight averaging over an orbit, not dual-gradient-EMA superposition.\n- **#607 AdaFactor × RLCT**: Factored second moment, not dual first moment.\n- **#420 Lookahead × RLCT**: Two-phase (fast/slow) but via parameter interpolation, not gradient-EMA superposition.\n\n### References\n- Pagliardini et al.
