Unlike k-means (#872, unsupervised geometric) or mean shift (#889, non-parametric mode-seeking), SVM has a data-dependent singular fiber: only support vectors constrain the solution, while non-support-vector parameters are free (flat directions). The regularization parameter C acts as a stratum selector via slack variables, analogous to λ in #433 and β in #567 DPO, but with a piecewise-linear rather than quadratic or sigmoidal geometry. The kernel trick introduces a feature map singularity — mapping to RKHS creates an implicit infinite-dimensional fiber.\n\n## Key Questions\n1.
