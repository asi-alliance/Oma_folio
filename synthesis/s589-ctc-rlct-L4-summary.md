CTC marginalizes over all valid alignments between input and output sequences using a forward-backward algorithm (logsumexp recurrence). Key insight: alignment marginalization creates a combinatorial fiber structure — a union of alignment-indexed singular strata — absent in standard cross-entropy. The blank token probability serves as a continuous stratum selector on this discrete combinatorial fiber, with a predicted phase transition at critical blank probability p*_blank.\n\n## Claim Status\n\n| Claim | Description | Status | STV |\n|-------|-------------|--------|-----|\n| C1 | R_{an,exp}-definability of CTC loss via softmax + logsumexp + T-fold composition | PROVEN | (1.0, 0.95) |\n| C2 | Alignment-indexed singular strata: CTC fiber = union of per-alignment strata V_pi* | CONJECTURAL | (0.6, 0.7) |\n| C3 | T/U ratio as discrete stratum selector; lambda_CTC monotone in alignment multiplicity | CONJECTURAL | (0.6, 0.7) |\n| C4 | Gen bound: lambda_CTC·log(n)/(2n) + U_CTC/sqrt(n) with lambda_CTC <= lambda_CE | CONJECTURAL | (0.6, 0.7) |\n| C5 | Phase transition at p*_blank: discontinuous LLC when dominant stratum switches | CONJECTURAL | (0.6, 0.7) |\n\n## Novelty Highlights\n\n1. **C2 — Alignment-indexed strata**: CTC fiber is a union over alignment paths pi* of per-frame certainty sets. Each alignment defines a singular stratum V_pi* where softmax probability equals 1 at each frame. Union min-dominates: lambda_CTC = min over alignments of lambda_pi*. First combinatorial-fiber RLCT decomposition.\n2. **C3 — T/U as discrete selector**: Ratio of input length T to output length U controls alignment multiplicity binom(T, floor((T-U)/2)). At T=U, CTC reduces to CE with single alignment. As T/U grows, more strata become accessible.\n3. **C4 — Marginalization tightens bound**: Alignment marginalization reduces effective RLCT below frame-level CE RLCT, giving a generalization advantage. First theoretical basis for CTC's generalization benefit over per-frame CE.\n4. **C5 — Blank probability phase transition**: Blank token probability acts as continuous selector on discrete alignment strata. At p*_blank, dominant stratum switches from blank-heavy (low RLCT) to label-dense (higher RLCT), causing discontinuous LLC.\n\n## Cross-Reference Map\n\n- **#8 (RLCT basics):** Definability framework foundation\n- **#100 (gradient descent):** CTC optimization dynamics\n- **#169 (dropout):** Shared stochastic marginalization — dropout is stochastic, CTC is deterministic\n- **#515 (VAE):** Continuous latent marginalization contrast — CTC is discrete combinatorial\n- **#167 (score matching):** Shared logsumexp/partition function structure\n- **#359 (nucleus sampling):** Softmax sequence probability connection\n- **#388 (beam search/Mirostat):** CTC decoding interface\n- **#372 (deep supervision):** Auxiliary loss interaction\n- **#582 (curriculum learning):** Sequence length curriculum connection\n- **#234 (temperature scaling):** Continuous stratum selector parallel\n- **#362 (label smoothing):** Soft target modification contrast\n- **#485 (RLT):** Recurrent weight sharing in sequence models\n- **#396 (GLA):** Gated attention sequence model\n- **#468 (PPO):** Piecewise non-analytic loss comparison\n\n## Open Problems\n\n1.
