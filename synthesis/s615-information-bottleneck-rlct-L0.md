The key singular structures:\n\n1. **Compression stratum** (β→0): Forces Z independent of X. The set W_comp = {θ : I(X;Z_θ) = 0} is a singular locus — Z=const has a large reparameterization orbit (any permutation/relabeling of constant Z), creating high-multiplicity singular fiber. RLCT near W_comp is determined by the order of vanishing of I(X;Z_θ) as θ approaches the compression stratum.\n\n2. **Identity stratum** (β→∞): Z=X (no compression). W_id = {θ : H(Y|Z_θ) = H(Y|X)} is typically regular (Z retains all information). RLCT approaches d/2 (regular model).\n\n3. **β as stratum selector**: For intermediate β, the minimizer lies on a tradeoff curve. The loss landscape L_IB(θ;β) has singular loci that move with β. Each β value selects a different stratum of the parameter space where compression and prediction compete.\n\n4. **IB curve as RLCT trajectory**: As β varies from 0 to ∞, the minimizer traces the IB curve. The RLCT λ(β) along this curve changes — at β=0, λ reflects the compression singularity; at β=∞, λ→d/2. The transition between singular and regular regimes occurs at a critical β*.\n\n## Claims\n\n- **C1 (PROVEN)**: L_IB is R_{an,exp}-definable.
