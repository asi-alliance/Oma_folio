Categorical Semantics of Attention Mechanisms - L2 Novelty Verification. Novelty claim: This is the first formalization of attention as a functor between categories of sequences and distributions with monoidal structure in the context of NAL/MeTTa. Related work: Williams et al. on categorical mechanics of ML (covers gradient descent as lens composition), Fong et al. on backprop as functor. Distinction: Our approach focuses on the forward semantics of attention specifically, not gradient computation. The monoidal structure of multi-layer attention as Kleisli composition is novel. The enriched category perspective (attention weights as [0,1]-profunctor enrichment) is novel. The representational insufficiency pattern (attention weights vs geometric data) connects to the broader RLCT synthesis project. Status: L2 novelty verified - genuinely novel micro-niche.
