# Synthesis #666: Latent Diffusion Models (LDM) × RLCT — L1 Formalization\n## Date: 2026-10-28\n\n## Formal Definitions\n\n**D1 (Parameter Space)**\nΘ = (θ, φ, ψ) ∈ ℝ^p × ℝ^q × ℝ^r\nwhere:\n- θ ∈ ℝ^p: diffusion score model parameters (U-Net or transformer operating in latent space)\n- φ ∈ ℝ^q: VAE encoder parameters E_φ: ℝ^{d_x} → ℝ^{d_lat}\n- ψ ∈ ℝ^r: VAE decoder parameters D_ψ: ℝ^{d_lat} → ℝ^{d_x}\nTotal dimension = p + q + r.\n\n**D2 (Composite LDM Loss)**\nThe joint training objective:\n\nL_recon(φ, ψ) = E_x [||D_ψ(E_φ(x)) - x||² + γ · KL(q_φ(z|x) || p(z))]\n(VAE ELBO-style reconstruction, q_φ(z|x) = N(μ_φ(x), σ_φ(x)²), p(z) = N(0, I))\n\nL_diff(θ; φ) = E_{t∈[0,T], x, ε∼N(0,I)} [||s_θ(z_t, t) - (-ε/σ_t)||²]\nwhere z = E_φ(x), z_t = √(ᾱ_t)·z + √(1-ᾱ_t)·ε, ᾱ_t = ∏_{s≤t} (1-β_s)\n\nL_LDM(θ, φ, ψ; β) = L_diff(θ; φ) + β · L_recon(φ, ψ)\n\nβ ≥ 0 is the reconstruction-diffusion tradeoff weight.\n\n**D3 (R_an,exp Definability — C1 PROVEN)**\nClaim: L_LDM is R_an,exp-definable.\nProof sketch:\n(i) E_φ(x): neural network = composition of linear maps + activations (ReLU/GELU = subanalytic, or analytic for tanh). The encoder mean μ_φ(x) and std σ_φ(x) are outputs of analytic maps. → R_an,exp-definable.\n(ii) Reparameterization: z = μ_φ(x) + σ_φ(x)·ε. For fixed ε, this is affine in (μ, σ) → polynomial. KL term: KL = ½(μ² + σ² - log σ² - 1) — built from polynomial + log → R_an,exp-definable (as #515).\n(iii) z_t = √(ᾱ_t)·z + √(1-ᾱ_t)·ε: for fixed t, affine in z and ε → polynomial. The noise schedule ᾱ_t is a fixed C¹ function of t (typically linear or cosine schedule) → definable.\n(iv) s_θ(z_t, t): neural network = composition of analytic maps → R_an,exp-definable.\n(v) ||s_θ - target||²: squared norm of vector difference → polynomial.\n(vi) L_recon: ||D_ψ(E_φ(x)) - x||² (polynomial) + γ·KL (R_an,exp). Sum → R_an,exp.\n(vii) L_LDM = L_diff + β·L_recon: linear combination of R_an,exp functions → R_an,exp.\nBy definability theorem (Watanabe 2009), L_LDM has finite RLCT via resolution of singularities. ∎\n\n**D4 (Composite Singular Fiber — C2 CONJECTURAL)**\nDefine the zero-loss sets:\n\nV_VAE(φ, ψ) = {(φ, ψ) : L_recon(φ, ψ) = 0}\n= {φ, ψ : D_ψ(E_φ(x)) = x ∀x AND KL(q_φ(z|x)||p(z)) = 0 ∀x}\n(perfect reconstruction + posterior = prior = posterior collapse fiber from #515)\n\nV_diff(θ; φ) = {θ : s_θ(z_t(E_φ(x)), t) = ∇log p_t(z) ∀t, x}\n(perfect score matching in the latent space induced by encoder φ — extends #167 W_t to latent space)\n\nV_LDM = {(θ, φ, ψ) : (φ, ψ) ∈ V_VAE AND θ ∈ V_diff(θ; φ)}\n= V_VAE ∩ V_diff(θ; φ)\n\nKEY STRUCTURAL INSIGHT: V_diff(θ; φ) depends on φ through the encoder map E_φ. This is NOT a simple Cartesian product or independent intersection — it is a PARAMETER-COUPLED fiber. Changing φ transforms the latent space geometry, which transforms the target score function ∇log p_t(z), which transforms the set of θ achieving perfect score matching.\n\nFormal structure: V_diff is a family of fibers parameterized by φ ∈ V_VAE:\nV_diff: φ ↦ {θ : s_θ(·, t) = ∇log p_t^{(φ)}(·)}\nwhere p_t^{(φ)} is the marginal distribution at noise level t induced by encoding data through E_φ.\n\nConjecture: The composite RLCT satisfies\nλ_LDM ≤ min(λ_VAE(φ*), λ_diff(θ*; φ*))\nunder transversality of the intersection. The coupling means the fibers are not independent: if φ moves along V_VAE, the latent geometry changes and V_diff moves correspondingly.\n\nGap: Whether V_diff(θ; φ) forms a fiber bundle over V_VAE (base = V_VAE, fiber = score-matching θ set) is open. If so, λ_LDM = integral of λ_diff over the base, weighted by the VAE fiber measure.\n\n**D5 (d_lat as Dual Stratum Selector — C3 CONJECTURAL)**\nThe latent dimension d_lat ∈ {1, 2, ..., d_x} acts as a stratum selector for BOTH fibers simultaneously:\n\nFor V_VAE: d_lat controls compression ratio. Low d_lat → high codimension V_VAE (harder reconstruction, more constraints on decoder) → λ_VAE(d_lat) increases as d_lat decreases. This recovers #515's d_z selector.\n\nFor V_diff: d_lat controls the ambient dimension of the score matching problem. Low d_lat → simpler latent distribution → easier score estimation → λ_diff(d_lat) decreases as d_lat decreases. This extends #167 to variable ambient dimension.\n\nComposite tradeoff:\nλ_LDM(d_lat) ≈ λ_VAE(d_lat) + λ_diff(d_lat)\nwhere:\n- λ_VAE(d_lat) is DECREASING in d_lat (more latent capacity = easier reconstruction)\n- λ_diff(d_lat) is INCREASING in d_lat (higher-dimensional score matching = harder)\n\nConjecture: λ_LDM(d_lat) is U-shaped with minimum at d_lat* where:\ndλ_VAE/d(d_lat)|_{d_lat*} = -dλ_diff/d(d_lat)|_{d_lat*}\n\nPrediction: d_lat* < d_x (compression is optimal), confirming the LDM design principle.\n\nThis is distinct from #515 (d_z selects VAE strata only) and #167 (t selects diffusion strata only) — d_lat selects COMPOSITE strata through both fibers.\n\n**D6 (Generalization Bound — C4 CONJECTURAL)**\nG_LDM(n, d_lat, β) ≤ λ_LDM(d_lat, β) · log(n) / n + U(d_lat, β) / √n\n\nwhere:\n- λ_LDM(d_lat, β) = composite RLCT at the optimal joint fiber V_LDM*\n  λ_LDM(d_lat, β) = λ_VAE(d_lat) · w_VAE(β) + λ_diff(d_lat) · w_diff(β)\n  where w_VAE(β) = β/(1+β), w_diff(β) = 1/(1+β) are tradeoff weights\n- U(d_lat, β) = singular fluctuation, depends on both VAE and diffusion stratum complexity\n- β modulates which fiber's singularity dominates the generalization bound\n\nPrediction: optimal β*(d_lat) is DECREASING in d_lat — for low d_lat (high compression), V_VAE singularity is severe, requiring higher β to regularize reconstruction. For high d_lat (low compression), V_diff dominates, requiring lower β to prioritize diffusion learning.\n\nβ*(d_lat) ≈ λ_diff(d_lat) / λ_VAE(d_lat) (ratio of component RLCTs, analogous to #362 CFG transition and #234 temperature)\n\n**D7 (Phase Transition at d_lat* — C5 CONJECTURAL)**\nCritical d_lat* is a phase transition point:\n\n- Below d_lat*: V_VAE singularity DOMINATES (posterior collapse too aggressive, information bottleneck, reconstruction fiber is the binding constraint)\n- Above d_lat*: V_diff singularity DOMINATES (latent space too high-dimensional, score matching curse of dimensionality, diffusion fiber is the binding constraint)\n- At d_lat*: BALANCED composite singularity, minimum λ_LDM, optimal generalization\n\nSGLD LLC observable: composite λ̂_LLC(d_lat) shows U-shape with kink at d_lat*.
