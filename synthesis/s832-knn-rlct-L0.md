#832 kNN × RLCT - L0 Claim Sketch\n\n## Topic\nk-Nearest Neighbors (instance-based, lazy learning) viewed through Real Log Canonical Threshold (RLCT) from Singular Learning Theory.\n\n## Claims\n- C1 (PROVEN-ish): The kNN decision function is definable in R_an: for fixed training set, the Voronoi cell decomposition of input space is semialgebraic (distance comparisons), and the vote/average over the k nearest neighbors is piecewise constant/piecewise smooth across Voronoi boundaries. The 1-NN 0-1 loss and kNN regression risk are definable functions of the training data points viewed as parameters. This mirrors #581 prototype geometry but WITHOUT any trained parameters: the "parameter space" is the training set itself (X_train as Theta = R^{n*d}).\n- C2 (CONJECTURAL): Training points as parameters: degenerate configurations (duplicate points, tie sets where distances coincide, points on cell boundaries) form singular strata. The tie locus {x_i : exists j != i, ||x-x_i|| = ||x-x_j|| for test x} is a codim-1 arrangement; RLCT of the expected risk over data-draw concentrates near tie-stratum configurations, governing convergence.\n- C3 (CONJECTURAL): k is a discrete stratum selector: the vote boundary smooths as k grows; predicted phase transition at critical k* where effective RLCT drops (majority-vote degeneracy strata vanish as k increases beyond cell density), matching the classical bias-variance tradeoff k ~ n^{2/(d+2)} as an RLCT phase transition.\n- C4 (CONJECTURAL): 1-NN interpolating limit is maximally singular: every training point is a support "atom" (analog of SVM #831 support vectors, both methods concentrate on boundary strata); predicted RLCT(1-NN) > RLCT(k-NN) monotonically in k for fixed n.\n- C5 (CONJECTURAL): Distance-metric choice (Euclidean vs Mahalanobis vs learned metric #581) reshapes the Voronoi arrangement; metric singularity (degenerate M, det(M)=0) creates fiber degeneracy analogous to #831 kernel collapse.\n\n## Key novelty\nNo parameters trained, yet the training set ITSELF is the singular parameter space — "data as parameter" inverts the usual SLT setup. Dual to #831: SVM concentrates the minimizer on strata; kNN has no minimizer, the strata ARE the whole geometry.\n\n## Cross-refs\n#581 prototype/metric learning, #831 SVM kink locus, #153 semialgebraic cell decomposition, #162 rank deficiency, #248 generalization bounds.\n\n## Next\nL1 formalization.
