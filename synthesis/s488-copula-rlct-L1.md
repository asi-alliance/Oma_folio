# Synthesis #488: Copula Models × RLCT\n## L1: Formalization\n\n**Date:** 2026-10-21\n\n### Formal Definitions\n\n**D1 (Sklar Decomposition):** Let $F_{X,Y}(x,y)$ be a bivariate joint CDF with continuous marginals $F_X, F_Y$. By Sklar's theorem, $F_{X,Y}(x,y) = C(F_X(x), F_Y(y); \theta)$ where $C: [0,1]^2 \to [0,1]$ is a copula parameterized by $\theta$. The copula density $c(u,v;\theta) = \partial^2 C / \partial u \partial v$. The negative log-likelihood loss $K(\theta) = -\frac{1}{n}\sum_i \log c(F_X(x_i), F_Y(y_i); \theta)$. The parameter space splits $\Theta = \Theta_{\text{marg}} \times \Theta_{\text{cop}}$.\n\n**D2 (Product Fiber):** The true parameter set $W = \{\theta : K(\theta) = K^*\}$ decomposes as $W = W_{\text{marg}} \times W_{\text{cop}}$ where $W_{\text{marg}} = \{(\theta_X, \theta_Y) : F_X(\cdot;\theta_X) = F_X^*, F_Y(\cdot;\theta_Y) = F_Y^*\}$ and $W_{\text{cop}} = \{\theta_{\text{cop}} : c(\cdot;\theta_{\text{cop}}) = c^*\}$. This holds because Sklar decomposition makes marginals and copula independently estimable.\n\n**D3 (Tail Dependence Singularity):** The upper tail dependence coefficient $\lambda_U(\theta) = \lim_{t \to 1^-} P(V > t | U > t) = \lim_{t \to 1^-} \frac{1 - 2t + C(t,t;\theta)}{1 - t}$. For Clayton copula: $\lambda_L = (2^{-1/\theta})$ for $\theta > 0$, $\lambda_L = 0$ for $\theta = 0$ (independence). The map $\theta \mapsto \lambda_L(\theta)$ is non-analytic at $\theta = 0$ (the independence boundary).\n\n**D4 (Copula Family Stratum):** Each copula family $\mathcal{F}$ (Gaussian, Clayton, Gumbel, Frank) defines an algebraic variety $V_\mathcal{F} \subset \Theta_{\text{cop}}$ via its parameterization. Mixtures $\sum_k \pi_k C_k(\cdot;\theta_k)$ create union strata $\bigcup_k V_{C_k}$.\n\n**D5 (Kendall τ Stratum Selector):** Kendall's $\tau = 4\int C(u,v) dC(u,v) - 1$ maps copula parameter $\theta$ to $[-1,1]$. The function $\tau(\theta)$ is a smooth stratum selector: different $\tau$ values select different regions of the copula fiber with different singularity structure.\n\n**D6 (Generalization Functional):** $G(\theta) = \frac{\lambda_{\text{marg}} + \lambda_{\text{cop}}(\theta)}{n} + U(\theta)/\sqrt{n}$ where $\lambda_{\text{cop}}(\theta)$ is the RLCT of the copula fiber at parameter $\theta$ and $U$ is the fluctuation functional.\n\n**D7 (Tail Phase Transition):** Define $\theta^*$ as the critical value where tail dependence transitions from zero to positive. For Clayton: $\theta^* = 0$ (tail dependence for $\theta > 0$, independence at $\theta = 0$). The RLCT $\lambda_{\text{cop}}(\theta)$ satisfies $\lambda_{\text{cop}}(\theta^+) > \lambda_{\text{cop}}(0)$ due to the non-analytic tail boundary.\n\n### Claim Formalizations\n\n**C1 (Proven):** The copula densities for Gaussian ($c = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\rho^2}} \exp(\ldots)$), Clayton ($c = (1+\theta)(uv)^{-1-\theta}(u^{-\theta}+v^{-\theta}-1)^{-1/\theta-2}$), and Gumbel families are compositions of polynomial and exponential functions of $\theta$, hence definable in $R_{\text{an,exp}}$. The NLL $K(\theta)$ is a finite sum of $\log c$ evaluations, also definable.\n\n**C2 (Conjectural):** The product fiber decomposition $W = W_{\text{marg}} \times W_{\text{cop}}$ implies $\lambda_{\text{total}} = \lambda_{\text{marg}} + \lambda_{\text{cop}}$ by the RLCT addition rule for product varieties. **Gap:** The decomposition assumes marginals are estimated without error; finite-sample marginal estimation creates coupling between $W_{\text{marg}}$ and $W_{\text{cop}}$.\n\n**C3 (Conjectural):** Kendall $\tau$ as stratum selector: $\lambda_{\text{cop}}(\tau)$ varies with $\tau$, with critical $\tau^*$ corresponding to the tail dependence onset. **Gap:** No rigorous proof that $\tau$ stratifies the fiber into smooth strata; the relationship between $\tau$ and algebraic geometry of $W_{\text{cop}}$ is heuristic.\n\n**C4 (Conjectural):** $G \sim (\lambda_{\text{marg}} + \lambda_{\text{cop}})/n + U/\sqrt{n}$. Since copula separates dependence from marginals, effective dimensionality is reduced: $\lambda_{\text{cop}} < \lambda_{\text{joint}}$ (modeling dependence structure alone is less singular than modeling the full joint). **Gap:** The inequality $\lambda_{\text{cop}} < \lambda_{\text{joint}}$ is intuitive but not proven; the joint model may have cancellations not present in the copula decomposition.\n\n**C5 (Conjectural):** Phase transition at $\theta^*$: for $\theta < \theta^*$ (weak/no dependence), $W_{\text{cop}}$ is smooth ($\lambda_{\text{cop}} = d_{\text{cop}}/2$, trivial). For $\theta > \theta^*$ (tail dependence active), $W_{\text{cop}}$ has boundary singularity at $(0,0)$ or $(1,1)$, $\lambda_{\text{cop}} > d_{\text{cop}}/2$. **Gap:** The exact RLCT at the tail boundary is not computed; the transition may be continuous rather than sharp.\n\n### Open Problems\n\n1.
