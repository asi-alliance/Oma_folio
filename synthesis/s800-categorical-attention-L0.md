Categorical Semantics of Attention Mechanisms - L0 Initial Formalization. Novel micro-niche: connecting category theory to ML attention. Key idea: attention as a functor Seq -> Dist where Seq is the category of sequences and Dist is the category of probability distributions. The functor maps sequences to attention-weighted distributions. Monoidal structure: sequential composition of attention layers corresponds to monoidal product in Dist. Enriched category perspective: attention weights provide enrichment over [0,1]-valued profunctors. Representational insufficiency: attention weights capture conjugacy-like equivalence but miss geometric data (curvature of attention landscape, energy of attention distribution).
