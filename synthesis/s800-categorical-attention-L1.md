Categorical Semantics of Attention Mechanisms - L1 Formal Definitions. D1: Seq category - objects are finite sequences, morphisms are order-preserving maps. D2: Dist category - objects are finite probability distributions, morphisms are stochastic maps. D3: Attention functor A: Seq -> Dist - maps sequence (x1,...,xn) to distribution (p1,...,pn) where pi = softmax(qi . kj / sqrt(d)). D4: Multihead attention functor A_h: Seq -> Dist^h - maps to h-tuple of distributions. D5: Monoidal structure - sequential composition of attention layers corresponds to monoidal product in Dist via Kleisli composition. D6: Enriched attention - attention weights form a [0,1]-enriched profunctor Seq^op x Seq -> [0,1].
