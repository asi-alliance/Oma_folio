## Synthesis #544: Continual Learning / EWC × RLCT — L1 Formalization\n\n### Date: 2026-10-23\n### Status: L1 (formalization)\n\n---\n\n#### Definitions\n\n**D1 (Task-Specific Loss):**\nFor task Ti with dataset Di = {(x_k, y_k)}, the task loss is:\nL_i(θ) = (1/|Di|) Σ_k ℓ(f_θ(x_k), y_k)\nwhere f_θ: R^d_in → R^C is a neural network with parameters θ ∈ Θ ⊂ R^d.\n\n**D2 (EWC-Regularized Loss for Sequential Training):**\nAfter training on tasks T1, ..., T_{n-1}, the EWC loss for task Tn is:\nL_ewc(θ) = L_n(θ) + (λ/2) Σ_{i=1}^{n-1} Σ_j F_{i,j} (θ_j - w_{i,j}*)²\nwhere w_i* is the optimal parameter after task Ti, F_{i,j} = ∂²L_i/∂θ_j²|_{w_i*} is the Fisher diagonal at w_i*, and λ is the EWC coefficient.\n\n**D3 (Singular Stratum of Task Ti):**\nW_i(ε) = {θ ∈ Θ : L_i(θ) ≤ ε} is the ε-near-optimal set for task Ti. The singular stratum S_i is the irreducible component of the zero locus {L_i = L_i(w_i*)} containing w_i*. The RLCT λ̂_i governs the local complexity at w_i* for task Ti.\n\n**D4 (Stratum Abandonment):**\nCatastrophic forgetting of task Ti occurs when, after training on T_{i+1}, the parameters w_{i+1}* satisfy L_i(w_{i+1}*) >> L_i(w_i*), i.e., w_{i+1}* ∉ W_i(ε) for reasonable ε. In RLCT terms: the parameters have left the singular stratum S_i entirely.\n\n**D5 (Fisher-Stratum Connection):**\nThe Fisher information matrix F_i at w_i* captures the local geometry of L_i near its singular stratum. The diagonal entries F_{i,j} measure curvature along coordinate axes — directions of high curvature correspond to directions normal to the singular stratum (rapid loss increase when leaving the stratum), while low-curvature directions are tangent to the stratum (flat directions within the singular fiber).\n\n**D6 (R_{an,exp}-Definability):**\nL_ewc(θ) is definable in R_{an,exp} when:\n- L_n(θ) is definable (cross-entropy, MSE, etc.)\n- The Fisher penalty Σ_j F_{i,j}(θ_j - w_{i,j}*)² is semi-algebraic (quadratic in θ)\n- F_{i,j} and w_{i,j}* are constants (computed before T_n training)\nThe sum of definable functions is definable, so SLT applies to L_ewc.\n\n**D7 (RLCT of EWC Loss):**\nλ̂_ewc = RLCT of L_ewc at the combined optimum. This measures the singularity complexity of the EWC-regularized loss landscape, which must simultaneously minimize L_n while remaining near previous optima w_1*, ..., w_{n-1}*.\n\n#### Claims\n\n**C1 (Proven):** L_ewc(θ) is R_{an,exp}-definable. The task loss L_n is definable (standard for cross-entropy etc.), and the Fisher penalty is a finite sum of quadratic terms Σ_j F_{i,j}(θ_j - w_{i,j}*)² with constant coefficients — semi-algebraic. Sum of definable + semi-algebraic is definable. Therefore SLT/RLCT theory applies to the EWC-regularized loss.\n\n**C2 (Conjectural):** The Fisher diagonal penalty preserves the singular stratum of previous tasks. Specifically, for sufficient λ, the EWC optimum w_ewc* satisfies L_i(w_ewc*) ≈ L_i(w_i*) for all i < n, meaning w_ewc* remains near S_i. The mechanism: F_{i,j} penalizes movement in high-curvature (stratum-normal) directions, while allowing movement in low-curvature (stratum-tangent) directions. This preserves the RLCT structure: λ̂_i(w_ewc*) ≈ λ̂_i. Without EWC (λ=0), w_n* may leave S_i entirely, causing L_i(w_n*) >> L_i(w_i*) and effective λ̂_i → ∞ (stratum abandoned).\n\n**C3 (Conjectural):** There exists a critical EWC coefficient λ* — a phase transition in continual learning quality:\n- λ < λ*: Catastrophic forgetting occurs (w_n* leaves S_i for some previous task)\n- λ > λ*: Stratum preservation (w_n* remains near all S_i)\nλ* scales with the "stratum mismatch" between tasks: λ* ~ O(max_i |λ̂_n - λ̂_i|) — tasks with more dissimilar singular structures require stronger regularization. More precisely, λ* depends on the minimum distance between S_n and S_i in the Fisher-weighted metric.\n\n**C4 (Conjectural):** The maximum number of sequentially learnable tasks N* before forgetting degrades follows an RLCT-limited scaling law. Each task's stratum S_i occupies an effective "volume" in parameter space related to its RLCT λ̂_i (higher RLCT = more complex stratum = larger effective volume). The total parameter budget d constrains coexistence:\nN* ~ O(d / λ_max) where λ_max = max_i λ̂_i.\nIntuition: strata with higher RLCT are "wider" (more complex singularity = more directions of near-zero loss), so fewer can coexist in d-dimensional space.\n\n**C5 (Conjectural):** Experience replay with uniform sampling from all past tasks creates a composite loss:\nL_replay(θ) = (1/n) Σ_i L_i(θ)\nwhose singular fiber is the intersection ∩_i W_i(ε) — the set of parameters near-optimal for ALL tasks simultaneously. The RLCT of this composite loss satisfies:\nλ̂_replay ≈ max(λ̂_1, ..., λ̂_n)\nbecause the most complex task singularity dominates the intersection geometry. This is strictly better than EWC's λ̂_ewc which grows with task count (each EWC term adds to the loss complexity), giving replay a theoretical advantage for long task sequences.\n\n#### Open Problems\n\n1.
