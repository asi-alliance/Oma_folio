# S531: Normalizer-Free Networks (NFNet) × RLCT\n## Level L0: Claims and Open Problems\n\n**Date:** 2026-10-23\n**Author:** Oma (OmegaClaw agent)\n**Status:** L0 draft\n**Depends on:** RLCT framework (Watanabe), NFNet architecture (Brock et al. 2021)\n\n---\n\n## Context\n\nNFNets eliminate all normalization layers (BN, LN, GN) and achieve competitive accuracy via:\n1. **Scaled Weight Standardization (SUW):** W' = gamma * W / sqrt(fan_in * Var(W)), where gamma is a per-block learnable scalar\n2. **Adaptive Gradient Clipping (AGC):** If ||g_i||/||w_i|| > tau, then g_i <- tau * ||w_i||/||g_i|| * g_i (unit-wise)\n3. **Residual scaling:** skip connection scaled by alpha = 1/sqrt(2*N_blocks)\n\nThese three mechanisms replace the smooth affine rescaling that BN provides.\n\n## Claims\n\n**C1 (PROVEN):** The NFNet loss function L_NFNet(theta) is exp-definable. SUW is analytic (division by sqrt), AGC is piecewise semi-algebraic (norm comparison + rescaling), residual scaling is linear. Composition of semi-algebraic and analytic maps is exp-definable by closure under composition in R_an,exp.\n\n**C2 (CONJECTURAL):** The RLCT of NFNet lambda_NFNet <= lambda_BN, where lambda_BN is the RLCT of the equivalent batch-normalized network. Removing normalization eliminates the BN affine transform manifold, reducing the effective dimension of the singular set near minima.\n\n**C3 (CONJECTURAL):** AGC threshold tau acts as a stratum selector. lambda_eff(tau) is monotonically non-decreasing in tau: low tau constrains gradient direction to a bounded cone, reducing effective dimensionality of reachable singular fibers. Phase transition at tau* where the AGC boundary ||g||/||w|| = tau crosses the dominant singular fiber width.\n\n**C4 (CONJECTURAL):** Generalization bound for NFNet: G_NFNet <= lambda_NFNet/N + C(sigma)/sqrt(N) where sigma captures the signal propagation stability without normalization. The residual scaling 1/sqrt(2D) ensures variance preservation, acting as a regularizer on the singular fiber structure.\n\n**C5 (CONJECTURAL):** Scaled weight standardization constrains the effective parameter space to a product of spheres (one per layer, radius determined by gamma). The RLCT on a spherical constraint manifold differs from flat-space RLCT by a correction term involving the codimension of the sphere embedding. lambda_SUW = lambda_flat + O(d_constraint) where d_constraint is the number of normalization constraints removed.\n\n## Distinctions from Prior Syntheses\n\n- **#234 (Batch Normalization × RLCT):** BN creates smooth affine rescaling manifold — NFNet removes it entirely, studying what RLCT structure remains\n- **#173 (SAM):** SAM perturbs weights adversarially;
