## Synthesis #676: Group Convolution × RLCT -- L1 Formalization\n**Date:** 2026-10-28\n**Status:** L1 FORMALIZATION\n\n### Definitions\n\n**D1 (Group Convolution Operator):** Given input X ∈ R^{C_in × H × W}, group convolution with g groups partitions channels into g disjoint subsets. For group i (i=1,...,g), the output is:\n  Y_i = W_i * X_i + b_i, where W_i ∈ R^{(C_out/g) × (C_in/g) × k × k}, X_i ∈ R^{(C_in/g) × H × W}, Y_i ∈ R^{(C_out/g) × H × W}.\nThe full output is the concatenation Y = [Y_1; Y_2; ...; Y_g] ∈ R^{C_out × H × W}.\n\n**D2 (Block-Diagonal Weight Space):** The effective weight tensor W_g lives in the constrained parameter space:\n  Θ_g = {W ∈ R^{C_out × C_in × k × k} : W = ⊕_{i=1}^g W_i, W_i ∈ R^{(C_out/g) × (C_in/g) × k × k}}\nThis is a linear subspace of dimension d_g = g · (C_out/g) · (C_in/g) · k² = (C_out · C_in · k²) / g.\nNote: d_g = d_1 / g, so increasing g reduces parameter count linearly.\n\n**D3 (Per-Group Determinantal Variety):** For group i, the singular fiber at rank threshold r is:\n  V_i(r) = {W_i ∈ R^{(C_out/g) × (C_in/g) × k × k} : rank(W_i^{flat}) < r}\nwhere W_i^{flat} ∈ R^{(C_out/g) × (C_in/g · k²)} is the flattened weight matrix (channels × receptive field). V_i(r) is a determinantal variety of codimension (m-r+1)(n-r+1) where m = C_out/g, n = C_in/g · k².\n\n**D4 (Union Fiber for Group Conv):** The full singular fiber for group convolution at rank threshold r is:\n  V_g(r) = ⋃_{i=1}^g V_i(r) = {W ∈ Θ_g : ∃i, rank(W_i^{flat}) < r}\nThis is a union of g determinantal varieties, each living in a separate block. The singular locus is the union of per-group singular loci.\n\n**D5 (Stratification by Group Count):** The parameter space is stratified by g:\n  Θ = ⊔_{g | gcd(C_in,C_out)} Θ_g\nEach stratum Θ_g has dimension d_g = d_1/g. The strata are NOT nested — Θ_g ∩ Θ_h = ∅ for g ≠ h (different block structures). Transition between strata is discrete (architecture choice, not continuous parameter).\n\n**D6 (RLCT Decomposition):** For the group conv model with g groups, the RLCT λ_GC(g) satisfies:\n  λ_GC(g) ≤ Σ_{i=1}^g λ_i(r)\nwhere λ_i(r) is the RLCT of the i-th group's determinantal variety V_i(r). For identical groups (equal partition), λ_i = λ_1 for all i, giving:\n  λ_GC(g) ≤ g · λ_single(r)\nwhere λ_single(r) is the RLCT of a single group's determinantal variety of size (C_out/g) × (C_in/g · k²) at rank threshold r.\nBy subadditivity of RLCT over product singularities: if the loss decomposes as L(θ) = Σ_i L_i(θ_i) + L_inter, then λ_total ≤ Σ_i λ_i + λ_inter.\n\n**D7 (Generalization Bound):** The generalization bound for group convolution with g groups, n training samples:\n  G_GC(g, n) ~ λ_GC(g) / n + U_GC(g) / √n\nwhere U_GC(g) is the complexity functional (analogous to Rao-Blackwellized complexity). The U-shaped behavior arises because:\n  - Small g: λ_GC(g) is large (full coupling, high-dimensional determinantal variety)\n  - Large g: λ_GC(g) is small (small blocks, low per-group RLCT) but U_GC(g) grows (reduced expressivity → higher bias)\n  - Optimal g* minimizes the sum G_GC(g*, n) = min_g [λ_GC(g)/n + U_GC(g)/√n]\n\n### Loss Landscape Properties\n- L(θ) = (1/n) Σ_j ||y_j - f_GC(X_j; W)||² where f_GC applies group conv followed by ReLU and subsequent layers\n- Convolution is polynomial in weights → definable\n- ReLU is subanalytic → definable in R_{an,exp}\n- Squared loss is analytic → definable\n- By closure: L(θ) ∈ R_{an,exp} ✓ (C1 proven)\n\n### Key Structural Insight\nThe block-diagonal constraint means perturbations in one group's weights do not affect other groups' outputs. This decouples the Fisher information matrix into block-diagonal form, and the Hessian splits into g independent blocks. The nullspace of the full Hessian is the direct sum of per-group nullspaces — unlike dense conv where the nullspace can mix across all channels.
