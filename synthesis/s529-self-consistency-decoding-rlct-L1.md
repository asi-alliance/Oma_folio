#529 L1 FORMAL SKETCH (2026-10-23) Self-Consistency Decoding × RLCT
D1 (Setup): Language model with parameters w in Theta subset R^d; conditional distribution p_w(y|x) via softmax over tokens at temperature T; N independent reasoning paths y_1,...,y_N ~ p_{w/T}(y|x); deterministic answer extraction a_i = parse(y_i); answer space A = finite set of candidate answers.
D2 (Self-consistency functional): SC(w) = argmax_{a in A} sum_{i=1}^N 1[parse(y_i) = a], where y_i ~ softmax(w.h/T). Sampling from softmax is exp-definable; indicator 1[a_i = a] is semi-algebraic (finite equality on discrete set); argmax over finite counts is definable via finite max. Composite is R_an,exp-definable.
D3 (Fiber neighborhood): B_tau(w*) = {w in Theta : KL(p_w(.|x) || p_{w*}(.|x)) < epsilon(tau)} — the KL-ball of radius controlled by temperature tau; reachable strata S_k(tau) = B_tau(w*) intersect Theta_k where Theta_k are answer-specific strata.
D4 (Effective RLCT): lambda_eff(tau, N) = RLCT of the dominant stratum identified by N samples at temperature tau; increases with tau (more strata accessible) and decreases with N (better stratum identification reduces variance).
D5 (Stratum consensus): The answer cluster a* = argmax_a count(a_i = a) corresponds to the dominant singular stratum Theta_{a*}; agreement fraction f_agree = count(a*)/N estimates posterior mass P(Theta_{a*}) concentrated on dominant stratum.
D6 (Tie boundary): Tie manifold T_{a,b} = {w : count_a(w) = count_b(w)} = {w : sum 1[a_i=a] = sum 1[a_i=b]}; semi-algebraic hypersurface in sample space; carries its own RLCT lambda_tie.
D7 (Temperature as stratum selector): tau maps to exploration radius epsilon(tau) = -tau * log(p_min); as tau increases from 0 to infinity, B_tau grows from {w*} to all of Theta, exposing strata Theta_1, Theta_2, ... sequentially by posterior mass.
C1 (PROVEN, definability): SC(w) is a finite composition of exp-definable (softmax sampling) and semi-algebraic (count indicators, argmax) operations, hence definable in R_an,exp. The induced fiber admits finite RLCT by resolution of singularities. Proof: softmax(z/T) = exp(z_i/T) / sum_j exp(z_j/T) is R_an,exp-definable; finite sampling with fixed N gives a product distribution, still definable; indicator 1[a_i = a] is a polynomial equality constraint (semi-algebraic); argmax of N finite counts is a finite max, definable. Closure under composition gives R_an,exp-definability of SC.
C2 (CONJECTURE): tau acts as fiber neighborhood stratum selector: B_tau(w*) grows monotonically with tau, exposing strata of increasing RLCT. Gap: no proof that strata are exposed in RLCT order (P1, P4).
C3 (CONJECTURE): Majority vote identifies the dominant stratum: a* = argmax count corresponds to Theta_{a*} with highest posterior mass; f_agree estimates P(Theta_{a*}). Gap: requires stratum-answer bijection (P2); correlation between paths may bias the estimate (P7).
C4 (CONJECTURE): Gen bound G_SC <= lambda_eff(tau,N)/N + U(tau)/sqrt(N) where lambda_eff is effective RLCT of dominant stratum and U(tau) captures non-dominant stratum variance. Bound improves with N, degrades with tau too high. Distinct from single-sample bounds #8/#16. Gap: lambda_eff(tau,N) not formally derived (P1); U(tau) form unknown (P2).
C5 (CONJECTURE): Phase transition at critical tau*: U-shaped lambda_eff(tau) with minimum at tau*; agreement fraction drops sharply at tau* (order-disorder transition); SGLD LLC at tau < tau* single-basin, tau > tau* multi-basin. Gap: tau* existence not proven (P4); SGLD prediction unverified (P5).
