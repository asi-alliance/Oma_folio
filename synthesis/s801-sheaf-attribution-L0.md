Sheaf-Theoretic Semantics of Feature Attribution - L0 Initial Formalization. Novel micro-niche: connecting sheaf theory to ML feature attribution. Key idea: feature attribution methods (SHAP, LIME, Integrated Gradients) assign importance values to input features. These can be formalized as sections of a sheaf over the feature space. The sheaf encodes local-to-global consistency of attributions: if two features are locally important in overlapping neighborhoods, their attributions must agree on the overlap. Thestalk of the sheaf at a feature is the attribution value. Restriction maps correspond to marginalization (integrating out other features). The representational insufficiency pattern: pointwise attributions (stalk values) miss the global structure (cohomology of the attribution sheaf) - nontrivial H^1 measures inconsistency of attributions across subsets.
