Synthesis #493 L4: Sandwich Norm x RLCT - Capstone Summary\n\nDate: 2026-10-25\nStatus: L0-L4 COMPLETE\n\nAbstract:\nSandwich normalization (Norm2 o F o Norm1) creates a fiber product V1 x_F V2 of two normalization singular loci coupled through sublayer map F. Unlike pre-norm (union, RLCT=min) or post-norm (intersection, codim adds), sandwich norm produces a genuine fiber product whose codim and RLCT depend on transversality of F with V2. This is the first SLT treatment of normalization composition as fiber product geometry.\n\nClaims:\nC1 PROVEN: Sandwich norm Norm2(F(Norm1(x))) is definable in R_an,exp by closure under composition.\nC2 CONJECTURAL: Fiber product codim <= min(codim V1, codim V2), with equality under transversality.\nC3 CONJECTURAL: Under transversality, lambda_sandwich <= max(lambda_1, lambda_2). Under tangency, lambda_sandwich > max(lambda_1, lambda_2).\nC4 CONJECTURAL: Gen bound G_sandwich < G_single when lambda_sandwich < lambda_single and U_sandwich <= U_single.\nC5 CONJECTURAL: Phase transition at critical depth d_crit where sandwich placement becomes optimal vs pre-norm.\n\nPLN Chains:\n- C1 to C2: definability enables fiber product analysis\n- C2 to C3: transversality controls RLCT direction (lower bound vs upper bound)\n- C3 to C4: RLCT inequality implies gen bound inequality\n- C4 to C5: gen bound advantage emerges at critical depth\n- C2 diagnostic: transversality implies codim(fiber product) < codim(V1)\n\nOpen Problems:\n1.
