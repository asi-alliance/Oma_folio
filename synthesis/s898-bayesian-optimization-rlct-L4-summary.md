# Synthesis #898: Bayesian Optimization x RLCT (L4 Executive Summary)\n\n**Date:** 2026-11-12 | **Author:** Oma | **Status:** L4 SUMMARY — Pipeline L0-L1-L2-L3-L4 COMPLETE\n\n---\n\n## Plain-Language Summary\n\n**C1 (PROVEN): The math of BO loss is definable in the right language.** Bayesian optimization builds a surrogate model (Gaussian process) and an acquisition function (EI, PI, or UCB). Every ingredient — exp, matrix inverse, determinant, sqrt, the normal CDF Phi, the normal PDF phi, KL divergence — belongs to the class R_{an,exp} of definable functions. Since this class is closed under composition, the entire BO loss landscape is R_{an,exp}-definable. This is the entry ticket for singular learning theory.\n\n**C2 (CONJECTURAL): BO creates a singular fiber that depends on its own history.** At each step, the GP posterior updates with new data, and the acquisition landscape shifts. The zero-loss set V_t (where the acquisition vanishes) depends on ALL prior queries, not just the latest point. Unlike every prior synthesis where the singular fiber lives in parameter space (network weights) or is input-conditioned, this fiber lives in function space and evolves sequentially. This is the first path-dependent singular fiber in function space in the entire catalog.\n\n**C3 (CONJECTURAL): Kappa is a continuous dial from regular to singular.** In UCB, kappa controls exploration vs exploitation. Large kappa = exploration dominates, the loss landscape is smooth and regular (non-degenerate Fisher, lambda ~ d/2). Small kappa = exploitation dominates, the landscape concentrates near the current best, creating degeneracy (lambda < d/2). Unlike discrete stratum selectors in prior syntheses (#846 MoE routing, #877 depth), kappa interpolates smoothly — a genuinely new type of stratum selector.\n\n**C4 (CONJECTURAL): Regret is governed by the effective RLCT.** The standard BO regret bound involves information gain gamma_N. The conjecture is that gamma_N and the effective RLCT lambda_eff are connected: G_BO(N) ~ lambda_eff(kappa,t)/N + U(kappa)/sqrt(N). If true, SLT provides a principled generalization theory for BO that reduces regret analysis to singularity analysis.\n\n**C5 (CONJECTURAL): There is a phase transition at kappa-star.** As kappa decreases, the LLC jumps from regular to singular at a critical value kappa*. This depends on the objective function structure (Lipschitz constant, number of minima, kernel scale). It is SGLD-testable: sample from the GP posterior at varying kappa and measure the LLC.\n\n## Star Contribution\n\n**C2 + C3 combined:** The path-dependent function-space fiber (C2) coupled with kappa as a continuous stratum selector (C3) is the standout. No prior synthesis in the 898-entry catalog has a singular fiber that (a) lives in function space, (b) depends on sequential query history, and (c) is continuously tunable via a single real parameter. This triple combination is unique to BO.\n\n## Open Problems\n\n1. Dynamic SLT for sequentially conditioned posteriors (standard SLT assumes fixed loss)\n2.
