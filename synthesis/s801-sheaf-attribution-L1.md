Sheaf-Theoretic Semantics of Feature Attribution - L1 Formal Definitions. D1 Feature space X topological space whose open sets are subsets of input features. D2 Attribution sheaf A a sheaf over X where A(U) is the set of attribution functions on subset U of features. D3 Stalk A_x the attribution value at feature x defined as colimit over neighborhoods of x. D4 Restriction maps for U contained in V the map r_VU from A(V) to A(U) marginalizes attributions from V down to U. D5 Gluing axiom if local attributions agree on pairwise overlaps they extend to a global section. D6 Cohomology H1(X,A) measures obstruction to gluing local attributions into a consistent global attribution. D7 Sheafification converts a pre-attribution functor violating gluing into a proper attribution sheaf. Status L1 formal definitions written next L2 novelty verification.
