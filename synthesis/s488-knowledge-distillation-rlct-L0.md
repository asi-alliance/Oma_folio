The student minimizes a weighted combination of hard-label CE loss and KL divergence to the softened teacher distribution.\n\n### Key RLCT Bridge\n\nThe teacher's soft targets define a *target distribution* q_T(y|x) = softmax(z_teacher / T). The student learns to match this distribution. The RLCT bridge:\n\n1. **Soft target as desingularizer:** The hard one-hot target creates a singular loss landscape (the student must place all probability mass on one class — a singular constraint). The softened teacher distribution q_T relaxes this to a smooth target, potentially desingularizing the loss landscape and reducing the effective RLCT.\n\n2. **Temperature as stratum selector:** Temperature T controls the entropy of the target distribution. T→∞ gives uniform (maximally smooth, lowest RLCT), T→0 gives one-hot (maximally singular, highest RLCT). T acts as a continuous stratum selector between the smooth and singular regimes.\n\n3. **Teacher-student fiber projection:** The student parameter space is a projection of the teacher's (fewer params). The teacher's singular structure induces a *projected* singular structure on the student. The student's RLCT λ_student is bounded by the teacher's RLCT λ_teacher projected through the student's architecture.\n\n4. **Generalization bound:** The student's generalization gap G_student ~ λ_student / n + U_student / √n where λ_student depends on T. An optimal T* minimizes this bound — too low T makes the target too singular (high λ), too high T makes it too uniform (loses information, high U).\n\n### Five Claims\n\n- **C1 (Provable):** The KD loss L_KD = (1-α)·CE(y, p_student) + α·T²·KL(q_T, p_student/T) is definable in R_{an,exp} since softmax, CE, and KL are compositions of exp/log/polynomial operations.\n- **C2 (Conjectural):** The soft target q_T desingularizes the loss landscape relative to one-hot targets. The RLCT of KD loss λ_KD(T) < λ_CE for T > 0, with λ_KD(T) monotonically decreasing in T.\n- **C3 (Conjectural):** Temperature T acts as a continuous stratum selector. A phase transition at T* separates a low-T regime (target-dominated singular structure, λ ≈ λ_CE) from a high-T regime (uniform-target smooth structure, λ → λ_uniform).\n- **C4 (Conjectural):** The generalization bound for the distilled student is G_KD ~ λ_KD(T) / n + U_KD(T) / √n, with optimal T* balancing desingularization (lower λ) against information loss (higher U).\n- **C5 (Conjectural):** The student's RLCT λ_student ≤ λ_teacher when the student architecture is a smooth projection of the teacher's, because the projection collapses singular fibers (fewer redundant symmetries).\n\n### Cross-References\n\n- #355 (Perceptual Loss × RLCT) — frozen teacher as external feature extractor\n- #224 (Label Smoothing × RLCT) — soft targets as desingularization (KD generalizes this)\n- #66 (Test-Time Training × RLCT) — inference-time modification\n- #169 (Dropout × RLCT) — stochastic regularization as stratum selection\n- #302 (Linear Attention × RLCT) — architecture projection\n\n### Open Questions\n\n1.
