# Synthesis #853: DARTS (Differentiable Architecture Search) × RLCT\n## L0: Exploration\n## Date: 2026-11-06\n\n### Context\nDARTS (Liu et al., 2019) formulates neural architecture search as a bilevel optimization problem. The discrete choice of operations on each edge is relaxed via softmax over a continuous architecture parameter α. Inner loop optimizes weights w; outer loop optimizes architecture α. This synthesis bridges DARTS with Watanabe's RLCT framework.\n\n### Key RLCT Bridge\nThe softmax-over-operations relaxation creates a probability simplex in architecture space. Each edge (i,j) has K operations with mixing weights softmax(α^(i,j)). The simplex vertices (one-hot selections = discrete architectures) are singular points where Fisher information degenerates: non-selected operations have zero gradient contribution, creating flat directions. The bilevel optimization compounds this with inner-loop weight singularities.\n\n### Claims\n\n**C1 (PROVEN): R_an,exp Definability**\nThe mixed operation o^(i,j)(x) = Σ_k softmax(α_k) · o_k(x; w_k) is analytic: softmax is analytic (exp/sum), each o_k is polynomial/analytic in w, finite sum preserves analyticity. Bilevel finite-difference gradient: ∇_α L_val(w', α) ≈ (L_val(w + ε∇_w L_train(w,α), α) - L_val(w,α))/ε is composition of analytic maps. Standard but foundational.\n\n**C2 (CONJECTURAL): Simplex Vertex Singular Fiber**\nV_DARTS = {θ = (w, α) : α^(i,j) → vertex e_k for some edge (i,j)}. At a simplex vertex, softmax concentrates mass on one operation; gradients w.r.t. other operations' weights vanish → Fisher information has zero eigenvalues in those directions → flat degenerate directions. The singular fiber is the union of simplex boundaries across all edges. This is a NOVEL singularity type: simplex-constrained vertex degeneracy, distinct from weight-space singularities (standard RLCT) and from MAML's meta-fiber composition (#530).\n\n**C3 (CONJECTURAL): Architecture Mixing as Stratum Selector**\nThe effective temperature T_eff = 1/||α|| controls proximity to simplex vertices. At T_eff → ∞ (α ≈ 0): uniform mixing, full smooth optimization, no vertex singularity (interior of simplex). At T_eff → 0 (||α|| → ∞): vertex approach, singular fiber activation. The stratum selector is the inverse temperature β_arch = ||α||. Cross-temperature annealing (TA-DARTS) explicitly sweeps this selector.\n\n**C4 (CONJECTURAL): Bilevel Generalization Bound**\nG_DARTS ~ λ_arch·log(n_outer)/(2n_outer) + λ_w·log(n_inner)/(2n_inner) + U(α,w)/√n_outer\nwhere λ_arch is the RLCT of the architecture simplex fiber, λ_w is the inner-loop weight RLCT. The bilevel structure creates a compound bound: the architecture RLCT governs architecture generalization, the weight RLCT governs within-architecture generalization. Discretization gap = L(discrete) - L(continuous) contributes bias U that vanishes as α → vertex.\n\n**C5 (CONJECTURAL): Discretization Gap Phase Transition**\nAt critical training step t* ~ log(n), architecture parameters α begin collapsing toward simplex vertices (the well-known DARTS "performance collapse" phenomenon). SGLD should detect: (a) LLC increase as α approaches boundary (singular fiber activation), (b) Fisher rank decrease in non-selected operation directions, (c) potential instability at vertex crossing. The discretization gap δ(t) = max_edge ||softmax(α) - onehot(argmax(α))|| transitions from O(1) to O(0) at t*.\n\n### Star Contribution\n**Simplex vertex singular fiber** — the architecture parameters live on a product of probability simplexes (one per edge), and the vertices (discrete architectures) are singular points with Fisher degeneracy. This is the first RLCT analysis of NAS methods, and the first formalization of simplex-boundary singularity in the synthesis folio. The bilevel optimization adds a compound RLCT structure λ_arch × λ_w unique to NAS.\n\n### Distinction from Prior Bilevel Syntheses\n- #530 MAML: Bilevel = task-inner/meta-outer. No simplex structure. Meta-fiber is compositional.\n- #600 Domain Adaptation: Bilevel = domain-space adversarial. No architecture search simplex.\n- #179 TTT: Single inner-loop, no outer architecture optimization.\n- DARTS uniquely has: (a) simplex-constrained architecture parameters, (b) vertex singularities from softmax concentration, (c) bilevel = weight-inner/architecture-outer with distinct RLCT for each level.\n\n### Open Problems\n1.
