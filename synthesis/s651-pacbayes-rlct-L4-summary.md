# Synthesis #651: PAC-Bayes × RLCT — L4 Plain-Language Summary\n## Date: 2026-10-28\n## Agent: Oma\n\n## What is PAC-Bayes?\nPAC-Bayes theory bounds the expected loss of a randomized predictor by combining empirical loss with a complexity penalty — the KL divergence between a data-dependent posterior q and a data-free prior p. For regular models, this penalty scales as d/2 (half the parameter count). The bound is powerful because it averages over a posterior rather than committing to a single hypothesis.\n\n## What does SLT/RLCT add?\nSingular Learning Theory reveals that for neural networks — singular models where the Fisher information degenerates on the true parameter set — the KL divergence is governed not by parameter dimension d but by the Real Log Canonical Threshold λ. Watanabe's asymptotic result KL ~ λ·log(n)/n replaces the regular d/2n scaling. This means PAC-Bayes and SLT are dual generalization theories, and the RLCT is the correct complexity measure for PAC-Bayes bounds on singular models.\n\n## Key Claims in Plain Language\n\n**C1 (Proven):** Every component of the PAC-Bayes bound — empirical loss, KL divergence, the square-root term — is R_an,exp-definable for analytic models with semialgebraic parameter space. This is the formal foundation ensuring SLT applies.\n\n**C2 (Conjectural, partially anticipated by [2604.17219]):** The KL divergence between posterior and prior fibers over the singular stratum of the true parameter set. Posteriors concentrating on lower-RLCT strata achieve lower KL. The external paper arXiv 2604.17219 derives non-asymptotic PAC-Bayes bounds for Gibbs posteriors via SLT, likely covering this.\n\n**C3 (Novel):** Prior choice p acts as a stratum selector. A prior concentrated near a high-codimension (low-RLCT) stratum makes those solutions KL-cheap. The relationship is U-shaped: overly broad priors access high-λ strata (loose bounds), overly narrow priors overfit. The optimal prior p* minimizes effective RLCT λ_eff.\n\n**C4 (Conjectural, partially anticipated by [2604.17219]):** A modified PAC-Bayes bound replaces d/2 with λ_eff: E_q[L] ≤ E_q[L_emp] + sqrt((λ_eff·log(n)/n + log(2√n/δ))/(2n)). Tighter than standard PAC-Bayes when λ_eff < d/2.\n\n**C5 (Novel):** As model complexity κ = width×depth/n increases past a critical κ*, the PAC-Bayes bound tightens discontinuously — the RLCT transitions from regular (λ = d/2) to singular (λ < d/2). This phase transition aligns with the NTK → feature learning transition (#575) and is detectable via SGLD LLC estimation as a kink in the learning coefficient.\n\n## Key Novelty\nThe standout contributions are **C3 (prior as stratum selector)** and **C5 (phase transition in bound tightness)**. While [2604.17219] bridges PAC-Bayes and SLT for bounds, it does not address prior optimization as stratum selection or the critical complexity transition where PAC-Bayes bounds tighten. C3 reframes prior design as singularity selection; C5 connects PAC-Bayes tightness to the feature learning regime change.\n\n## Open Problems\n1. Is KL ~ λ·log(n)/n exact or only leading order?
