#587: Barlow Twins × RLCT — L1 Formalization\n\nDate: 2026-10-24\n\n## Definitions\n\nD1 (Network Architecture): Two identical networks f_θ (shared weights θ) process augmented views (A, B) of input x, producing batch-normalized embeddings z_A = BN(f_θ(A)) ∈ R^{N×d}, z_B = BN(f_θ(B)) ∈ R^{N×d}. The batch normalization ensures ||z_{A,n}||_2 = ||z_{B,n}||_2 = 1 per sample n. Embedding dimension d, batch size N.\n\nD2 (Empirical Cross-Correlation): C_θ ∈ R^{d×d} with entries:\n  C_{ij}(θ) = (1/N) Σ_{n=1}^{N} z_{A,n,i} · z_{B,n,j}\n\nThis is a polynomial map from θ → C_θ (analytic, since BN and f_θ are analytic and the sum is finite). C_θ is the empirical cross-correlation matrix.\n\nD3 (Barlow Twins Loss): \n  L_BT(θ) = Σ_{i=1}^{d} (C_{ii}(θ) - 1)² + λ · Σ_{i≠j} C_{ij}(θ)²\n\nwhere λ > 0 is the redundancy penalty coefficient. The first term (invariance) drives diagonal toward 1; the second (redundancy) drives off-diagonal toward 0. L_BT is a polynomial in C_θ, hence R_an-definable (C1 PROVEN: no exp/log/max required; purely semi-algebraic).\n\nD4 (Identity Fiber): The singular fiber (global minimum set) is:\n  V_id = { θ ∈ Θ : C_θ = I_d }\n\nThis imposes d constraints C_{ii}=1 (diagonal) and d(d-1)/2 constraints C_{ij}=0 (off-diagonal, symmetric in i,j since C is not necessarily symmetric but the penalty treats C_{ij}² for all i≠j). Total: d + d(d-1) = d² constraints. The fiber is the preimage of the identity under the polynomial map θ → C_θ. By algebraic geometry, V_id is a semi-algebraic variety. Its dimension = dim(Θ) - rank(J_C-I), where J_C-I is the Jacobian of the constraint map. Under generic transversality (Bertini), rank = d² and dim(V_id) = dim(Θ) - d².\n\nD5 (Lambda-Parameterized Loss Family): For λ > 0, define the 1-parameter family:\n  L_BT^{(λ)}(θ) = Σ_i (C_{ii} - 1)² + λ · Σ_{i≠j} C_{ij}²\n\nAt λ=0: V_0 = {θ : C_{ii}=1 ∀i} — invariance-only fiber (d constraints, dim = dim(Θ) - d).\nAt λ→∞: V_∞ = {θ : C_{ij}=0 ∀i≠j} — redundancy-only fiber (d(d-1) constraints, dim = dim(Θ) - d(d-1)).\nV_id = V_0 ∩ V_∞ (all d² constraints active).\n\nLambda selects which stratum is dominant: for small λ, the invariance stratum dominates (lower RLCT region); for large λ, the redundancy stratum dominates. The critical λ* marks the transition where both strata contribute equally.\n\nD6 (Generalization Bound): The RLCT-based generalization gap:\n  G_BT(n) ≤ λ_BT / n + U_BT / √n\n\nwhere λ_BT is the RLCT of the Barlow Twins loss landscape at the identity fiber V_id, and U_BT captures the non-singular contribution. Key conjecture: λ_BT < λ_SSL (unconstrained SSL) because the identity constraint variety V_id has lower complexity than the full parameter space. The constraint acts as implicit regularization, reducing effective dimension.\n\nD7 (Phase Transition at λ*): Define λ* as the critical value where the dominant singular stratum transitions:\n  - For λ < λ*: invariance stratum V_0 dominates, λ_BT ≈ d/2 (Morse-Bott at d constraints)\n  - For λ > λ*: redundancy stratum V_∞ dominates, λ_BT ≈ d(d-1)/2 (Morse-Bott at d(d-1) constraints)\n  - At λ = λ*: both strata are equally singular, λ_BT achieves minimum (balanced desingularization)\n\nThe phase transition is detectable via SGLD: the local RLCT estimate \hat{λ}(λ) shows a crossover at λ*. This is analogous to #541 VICReg's (λ,μ,ν) phase diagram but in a UNIFIED 1-parameter framework.\n\n## Claims (refined from L0)\n\nC1 (PROVEN): L_BT is R_an-definable.
