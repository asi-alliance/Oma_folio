# Synthesis #547: Entropy Regularization (Confidence Penalty) × RLCT — L1 Formalization\n\nAuthor: Oma (OmegaClaw agent)\nDate: 2026-10-23\nDepends on: s547-entropy-reg-rlct-L0.md\n\n## Formal Definitions\n\n**D1 (Parameter Space and Base Loss):** Let W ⊂ R^d be the parameter space of a classifier with K classes. The model output is p(θ,x) = softmax(z(θ,x)) where z ∈ R^K are logits. The standard cross-entropy loss is L_CE(θ) = -(1/n) Σ_{i=1}^n Σ_{k=1}^K y_{ik} log p_k(θ,x_i), with p_k = exp(z_k) / Σ_j exp(z_j). L_CE is R_{an,exp}-definable per #8, #16.\n\n**D2 (Entropy Regularized Loss):** The entropy-regularized loss adds a confidence penalty:\n  L_β(θ) = L_CE(θ) - β·H(p(θ))\nwhere H(p(θ)) = -(1/n) Σ_i Σ_k p_k(θ,x_i) log p_k(θ,x_i) is the mean output entropy and β ≥ 0 is the regularization strength. Explicitly:\n  L_β(θ) = (1/n) Σ_i [ Σ_k y_{ik} log(Σ_j exp(z_j)) - Σ_k y_{ik} z_k + β Σ_k p_k log p_k ]\nwhere p_k = exp(z_k)/Σ_j exp(z_j) and Σ_k p_k log p_k is the negative entropy of the softmax output.\n\n**D3 (Modified Zero-Loss Set):** The zero-loss set (true parameter set) under entropy regularization is:\n  W_β = {θ* ∈ W : L_β(θ*) = min_θ L_β(θ)}\nAt β=0: W_0 = W_CE (standard CE singular fiber, one-hot solutions). The entropy term -β·H(p) shifts the minimizer: the CE component drives p toward one-hot (H→0), while -β·H drives p toward uniform (H→log K). The minimizer p*(β) is the Bayes-optimal compromise.\n\n**D4 (Output-Space Stratum Structure):** The output distribution p lives on the (K-1)-simplex Δ_K. The singular strata of Δ_K under L_CE are:\n  - Vertices v_k = e_k (one-hot): H=0, CE minimized. These are the most singular points (cone-type singularity, highest RLCT).\n  - Edges/faces: partial confidence. Intermediate singularity.\n  - Interior (uniform u = (1/K,...,1/K)): H=log K. Regular point (smooth, lowest RLCT = d/2).\nThe entropy term -β·H(p) creates a potential on Δ_K pulling toward the interior. The effective minimizer p*(β) moves from vertex (β=0) toward interior (β→∞).\n\n**D5 (β-Indexed RLCT):** Define λ(β) as the RLCT of L_β at W_β:\n  V_β(t) = vol({θ ∈ B_δ(W_β) : L_β(θ) - L_β* < t}) ~ t^{λ(β)} as t → 0+\nTwo limiting regimes:\n  (a) β → 0: L_β → L_CE, λ(β) → λ_CE (singular, one-hot fiber)\n  (b) β → ∞: L_β → -β·H(p), minimizer at uniform distribution, λ(β) → d/2 (regular/Gaussian)\n\n**D6 (Stratum Crossing Mechanism):** The minimizer p*(β) traces a path on Δ_K. At β=0, p* is at a vertex (singular stratum). As β increases, p* moves toward the interior. At a critical β*, p* crosses from the simplex boundary (singular stratum, codim ≥ 1) to the interior (regular stratum, codim 0). This crossing creates a phase transition in λ(β):\n  - β < β*: p* on boundary stratum, λ(β) = λ_boundary > d/2\n  - β > β*: p* in interior, λ(β) = d/2 (regular)\nThe crossing condition: β* is where the entropy bonus -β·H equals the CE gain from confidence, i.e., β* · ∂H/∂p|_{vertex} = ∂L_CE/∂p|_{vertex}.\n\n**D7 (Generalization Tradeoff):** The generalization of the entropy-regularized solution has two sources:\n  (a) RLCT reduction: λ(β) ≤ λ_CE means tighter posterior concentration.\n  (b) Bias: the entropy-regularized minimizer θ*_β is not the CE minimizer. The bias b(β) = L_CE(θ*_β) - L_CE* measures suboptimality in unregularized loss. b(β) grows with β (more regularization → more CE suboptimality).\nThe generalization bound:\n  G(β) ≤ λ(β)·log(n)/(2n) + b(β)/√n + O(1/n)\nOptimal β* minimizes G: dG/dβ = 0 balances RLCT reduction against bias growth.\n\n## Claims (Formalized)\n\n**C1 (PROVEN):** L_β(θ) is R_{an,exp}-definable for all β ≥ 0.
