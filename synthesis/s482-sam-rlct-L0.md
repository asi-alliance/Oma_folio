#482 Sharpness-Aware Minimization (SAM) × RLCT — L0 EXPLORATION\nDate: 2026-10-21\nStatus: EXPLORATION\n\n## Motivation\nSAM (Sharpness-Aware Minimization, Foret et al. 2021) minimizes L_SAM(w) = max_{||ε||≤ρ} L(w+ε). Unlike standard SGD which minimizes L(w) directly, SAM first ascends to find the worst-case perturbation within radius ρ, then descends. This explicitly targets flat minima.\n\n## Why RLCT?\n- SAM loss L_SAM is a max over analytic functions → L_SAM ∈ R_an (max of analytic is lower semianalytic, definable in R_an)\n- RLCT λ_SAM(ρ) of the SAM loss landscape depends on perturbation radius ρ:\n  - ρ=0: SAM degenerates to standard ERM, λ_SAM(0) = λ_standard\n  - ρ>0: SAM "smooths" the loss landscape by taking max over ball, potentially raising λ at the true parameter (harder to achieve zero loss)\n  - As ρ increases, SAM penalizes sharper minima more → pushes trajectory toward flatter regions = lower λ regions\n- Key insight: SAM doesn't change W* (true parameter set) but changes which local minima are favored. The SAM-modified loss has DIFFERENT singularity structure: the flat directions that SAM prefers correspond to HIGHER-dimensional singular fibers (lower λ).\n\n## Preliminary Claims\n- C1 (Definability): L_SAM is definable in R_an → RLCT exists\n- C2 (Radius-RLCT): λ_SAM(ρ) ≥ λ_standard for ρ>0 at any given w (SAM loss is harder to minimize)\n- C3 (Flat-bias): SAM's implicit bias toward flat minima = bias toward LOW-λ singularities. The trajectory under SAM preferentially visits low-λ strata.\n- C4 (Generalization bound): G_SAM ~ λ_SAM(ρ*)/n + U/√n where ρ* is optimal perturbation radius. SAM's generalization advantage = λ_SAM(ρ*) < λ_standard.\n- C5 (Phase transition): At critical ρ*, SAM transitions from exploiting sharp minima (high-λ) to exploiting flat minima (low-λ). SGLD-testable: LLC under SAM should be measurably lower than under standard SGD.\n\n## Related Syntheses\n- #241 (Gradient Clipping × RLCT): clips gradient magnitude, different mechanism\n- #236 (Early Stopping × RLCT): stopping as stratum selector; SAM as stratum SELECTOR via landscape modification\n- #169 (Dropout × RLCT): stochastic regularization vs deterministic perturbation maximization\n- #222 (Weight Decay × RLCT): L2 regularization in parameter space vs sharpness penalty\n\n## Key Questions\n1.
