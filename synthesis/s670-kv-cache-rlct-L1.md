#670: KV Cache × RLCT\n\n## L1: Formalization\n\n### Definitions\n\n**D1 (KV Cache as Fixed-Point Evaluation Map)**\nLet $\mathcal{C}_n = \{(K_1,V_1),\ldots,(K_{n-1},V_{n-1})\}$ be the KV cache after $n-1$ autoregressive steps, where $(K_i,V_i) = (W_K x_i, W_V x_i)$ for input embeddings $x_i$ and projection weights $W_K, W_V$. During generation of token $n$, the forward pass computes:\n$$f_\theta(x_n; \mathcal{C}_n) = \text{Attn}(W_Q x_n, \mathcal{C}_n) \circ \text{FFN} \circ \cdots$$\nThe cache $\mathcal{C}_n$ is held at fixed values $\hat{\theta}_{frozen} = \{(W_K x_i, W_V x_i)\}_{i=1}^{n-1}$. The loss:\n$$L_n(\theta) = \ell(f_\theta(x_n; \mathcal{C}_n), y_n)$$\nsatisfies $\nabla_\theta \mathcal{C}_n = 0$ during backpropagation. Formally, $L_n(\theta) = L_n(\theta_{live}; \hat{\theta}_{frozen})$ where $\theta_{live}$ are parameters receiving gradients.\n\n**D2 (Mixed Singular Fiber Decomposition)**\nParameter space $\Theta \subset \mathbb{R}^d$ decomposes under caching as:\n- $\theta_{live} \in \Theta_{live}$: parameters with active gradient flow (query projection $W_Q$, output projection $W_O$, FFN weights, layer norms, current token embedding)\n- $\theta_{frozen}^{(n)} \in \Theta_{frozen}^{(n)}$: parameters frozen in cache (K/V projections evaluated at past tokens, $W_K x_i, W_V x_i$ for $i < n$)\n\nThe true loss function $L(\theta) = \mathbb{E}[L_n(\theta)]$ has singular fiber:\n$$\mathcal{F}_\theta = \{w \in \Theta : L(w) = L^*\} = \mathcal{F}_{live}(\theta_{live}) \times \{\hat{\theta}_{frozen}^{(n)}\}$$\nThe frozen coordinates are singletons (fixed values), so the effective fiber is $\mathcal{F}_{live}(\theta_{live}) \times \{pt\}$. The dimension of the exploring fiber is $k = \dim(\theta_{live}) < d$.\n\n**D3 (Cache Boundary as Semialgebraic Hypersurface)**\nDefine the effective cache length $b(n,B) = \min(n-1, B)$ where $B$ is the cache budget. The cache boundary $\{n = B+1\}$ is a semialgebraic condition in $(n, B)$-space. For $n \leq B+1$ (no eviction), the frozen set grows monotonically: $\Theta_{frozen}^{(n)} \supseteq \Theta_{frozen}^{(n-1)}$. For $n > B+1$ (eviction active), the frozen set is a sliding window: $\Theta_{frozen}^{(n)} = \{(W_K x_i, W_V x_i)\}_{i=n-B}^{n-1}$. The transition at $n = B+1$ creates a discontinuity in the frozen-fiber composition.\n\n**D4 (Effective Sequence Length as Discrete Stratum Selector)**\n$n_{eff} = b(n,B)$ controls the dimension of frozen-parameter space: $\dim(\Theta_{frozen}^{(n)}) \propto n_{eff} \cdot d_{model}$. As $n_{eff}$ increases from $0$ to $B$, the live-to-frozen ratio $k/(d) = (d - n_{eff} \cdot d_{model})/d$ decreases. Each increment of $n_{eff}$ activates new frozen coordinates, changing the effective singular fiber. The stratum structure is:\n$$\mathcal{F}_{live}^{(n_{eff})} \times \{pt\}^{n_{eff} \cdot d_{model}}$$\n\n**D5 (Bimodal Fisher Information)**\nThe Fisher information matrix decomposes block-diagonally under the live/frozen split:\n$$I(\theta) = \begin{pmatrix} I_{live}(\theta_{live}) & 0 \\ 0 & I_{frozen}(\theta_{frozen}^{(n)}) \end{pmatrix}$$\nwhere $I_{live}$ is the standard Fisher computed from gradient flow, and $I_{frozen}$ captures the curvature contribution of cached K/V (computed from the forward pass but with zero gradient contribution). The local learning coefficient:\n$$\lambda_{eff} = \frac{1}{2}\text{rank}(I_{live}) + \lambda_{sing}(\theta_{live})$$\nwhere $\lambda_{sing}$ captures the singular stratum contribution. The frozen block contributes to the Hessian structure but not to posterior exploration volume.\n\n**D6 (Frozen-Parameter Generalization Bound)**\nWith $k = \dim(\theta_{live})$ live parameters and $d - k$ frozen, the Watanabe generalization bound modifies to:\n$$G_n \leq \frac{\lambda_{eff}(n_{eff})}{n} + \frac{U}{\sqrt{n}} + C_{frozen}(n_{eff})$$\nwhere $C_{frozen}(n_{eff}) = \mathcal{O}\left(\frac{(d-k) \cdot \sigma_{frozen}^2}{n}\right)$ accounts for the bias of frozen parameters at their cached (non-optimal) values. As $n_{eff} \to 0$, $C_{frozen} \to 0$ (no caching, full gradient). As $n_{eff} \to \infty$, $C_{frozen}$ saturates at $\mathcal{O}(d \cdot \sigma^2 / n)$.\n\n**D7 (Eviction Policy as Fiber Surgery)**\nWhen eviction begins ($n > B+1$), each eviction step replaces a frozen coordinate $\hat{\theta}_{frozen}^{(i)}$ with a new one $\hat{\theta}_{frozen}^{(n)}$. This is a surgery on the frozen fiber: the singleton $\{pt_i\}$ is replaced by $\{pt_n\}$. The fiber $\mathcal{F}_{live}^{(n_{eff})} \times \{pt\}^{n_{eff} \cdot d_{model}}$ undergoes a discrete jump in its frozen coordinates. Under LRU eviction, the oldest frozen coordinate is removed, creating a FIFO queue structure. Under learned eviction, the selection is a function of attention scores, introducing an $R_{an}$-definable selection map.\n\n### Claims\n\n**C1 (PROVEN, stv 1.0 0.95): R_an-Definability of Cached Loss**\nThe cached loss $L_n(\theta_{live}; \hat{\theta}_{frozen}^{(n)})$ is $R_{an}$-definable. \n*Proof sketch:* The forward pass $f_\theta(x_n; \mathcal{C}_n)$ is a composition of: (1) analytic attention computation (softmax is real-analytic on $\mathbb{R}^d$ away from zero-temperature limit), (2) evaluation of $W_K, W_V$ at fixed inputs $x_1, \ldots, x_{n-1}$ (producing constants), (3) feed-forward layers with analytic activations (tanh) or piecewise-analytic activations (ReLU, GELU). For analytic activations, the composition is $R_{an}$-definable. For ReLU/GELU, piecewise-analyticity is preserved under finite composition (by Łojasiewicz structure theorem). The loss $\ell$ (cross-entropy) is $R_{an}$-definable. Hence $L_n \in R_{an}$. By resolution of singularities (Hironaka), finite RLCT exists. $\square$\n\n**C2 (CONJECTURAL, stv 0.5 0.9): Reduced Effective RLCT Under Caching**\nThe mixed singular fiber has $\lambda_{cached} \leq \lambda_{full}$. \n*Supporting argument:* In full-gradient mode, all $d$ parameters participate in posterior exploration, and the RLCT $\lambda_{full}$ is determined by the full singular fiber $\mathcal{F}_\theta \subset \mathbb{R}^d$. Under caching, only $k < d$ parameters explore, so the posterior volume scales as $\epsilon^{\lambda_{live}}$ where $\lambda_{live}$ is the RLCT of $\mathcal{F}_{live}$. Since $\dim(\mathcal{F}_{live}) < \dim(\mathcal{F}_\theta)$, we expect $\lambda_{live} \leq \lambda_{full}$. However, the frozen parameters at non-optimal values may introduce additional singularity through the $C_{frozen}$ bias, so the reduction is not guaranteed to be strict.\n\n**C3 (CONJECTURAL, stv 0.5 0.9): Effective Sequence Length as Stratum Selector**\nAs $n_{eff}$ increases, the dominant singular stratum changes. \n*Supporting argument:* Each increment of $n_{eff}$ freezes $d_{model}$ additional coordinates, reducing the live fiber dimension by $d_{model}$. This is analogous to increasing the codimension of the exploring fiber. The RLCT transitions when the frozen coordinates cross a stratum boundary in the full loss landscape. The critical $n_{eff}^*$ is where the frozen coordinates align with a singular stratum of the full loss.\n\n**C4 (CONJECTURAL, stv 0.5 0.9): Frozen-Parameter Generalization Bound**\nThe correction $C_{frozen}(n_{eff})$ is monotonically increasing in $n_{eff}$ and bounded. \n*Supporting argument:* As more parameters are frozen at cached values (which are generically non-optimal for the population loss), the bias accumulates. The correction is bounded because the frozen values are drawn from the same distribution as live parameters (both are realizations of the model), so $C_{frozen} \leq C_{max} \cdot n_{eff}/n$ for some architecture-dependent constant $C_{max}$.\n\n**C5 (CONJECTURAL, stv 0.5 0.9): Cache Saturation Phase Transition**\nAt $B^*$, eviction creates a topological change in the frozen fiber. \n*Supporting argument:* For $n \leq B$, the frozen set grows monotonically (no eviction), and the fiber dimension decreases smoothly. At $n = B+1$, eviction begins: frozen coordinates are replaced rather than added. This creates a discontinuity in the fiber composition function $n \mapsto \Theta_{frozen}^{(n)}$. The RLCT may jump because the replaced coordinates may belong to a different stratum than the evicted ones. The scaling $B^* \sim \alpha \cdot n^\beta$ is motivated by the analogy with #582 curriculum learning where data distribution changes create phase transitions at $n^* \sim n^{1/(\lambda+1)}$.\n\n### Open Problems\n1. **Additive vs multiplicative $\lambda_{eff}$**: Does the frozen-parameter RLCT decompose as $\lambda_{eff} = \lambda_{live} + \lambda_{frozen}$ (additive) or $\lambda_{eff} = \lambda_{live} \cdot \lambda_{frozen}/(\lambda_{live} + \lambda_{frozen})$ (harmonic)? SGLD experiments varying $n_{eff}$ can distinguish.\n2. **Eviction policy and stratum structure**: Does LRU eviction preserve the singular stratum (removing oldest = removing a stratum that was already explored) or destroy it (removing a stratum still needed)?\n3. **Closed form for $C_{frozen}$**: Is the correction expressible as a function of the KL divergence between cached and population-optimal K/V distributions?\n4. **Per-head caching**: Multi-head attention creates $H$ independent caches. Does each head contribute an independent stratum selector, or do heads interact through the concatenation in $W_O$?\n5. **Flash attention (#292) invariance**: Does tiling/fusing the attention computation change the computation graph topology, or is the RLCT invariant under memory-layout optimization?\n6. **Multi-turn accumulation**: In conversational settings, cache persists across turns. Does this create a hierarchical stratum structure (per-turn frozen layers stacked)?\n7. **Connection to #653 TTT**: TTT updates parameters at test time (dynamic fiber exploration), the opposite of caching (static fiber freezing).
