#665 Knowledge Distillation (KD) × RLCT — L1 Formalization\n## Date: 2026-10-28\n\n## Formal Definitions (D1-D7)\n\n**D1 (KD Composite Loss):**\nGiven teacher f_t(φ) with fixed logits z_t, student f_s(θ) with logits z_s(θ), distillation temperature τ > 0, hard/soft weight α ∈ [0,1]:\n\nL_KD(θ, τ, α) = (1-α) · L_hard(θ) + α · τ² · L_soft(θ, τ)\n\nwhere L_hard = -(1/N)Σ_i log p_s(y_i|x_i;θ), L_soft = (1/N)Σ_i Σ_k q_t(k|x_i,τ)·log(q_t(k|x_i,τ)/q_s(k|x_i,θ,τ)), q_t = softmax(z_t/τ), q_s = softmax(z_s(θ)/τ). τ² normalizes gradient magnitude.\n\n**D2 (Teacher-Student Zero-Loss Fiber):**\nV_KD(τ,α) = {θ : L_KD(θ,τ,α)=0} = V_hard ∩ V_teacher(τ)\n\nV_hard = {θ : f_s(x_i;θ)=y_i ∀i} (hard-label zero-loss)\nV_teacher(τ) = {θ : softmax(z_s(θ)/τ) = softmax(z_t/τ)} = {θ : z_s^k(θ)-z_s^1(θ) = z_t^k-z_t^1 ∀k≥2} (logit-difference matching, modulo log-sum-exp gauge)\n\n**D3 (R_an,exp Definability):**\nL_KD ∈ R_an,exp(θ;τ,α). Components: z_s(θ) analytic (NN), softmax=exp/Σexp analytic for τ>0, KL=Σq_t·log(q_t/q_s) analytic on {q_s>0}, CE analytic, linear combination closed. Composition of definable functions definable. ∎ (C1 PROVEN)\n\n**D4 (Intersection Fiber RLCT):**\nλ_KD(τ) = RLCT(L_KD(·,τ), V_KD(τ))\n\nUnder transversality (V_hard ⋔ V_teacher(τ)):\nλ_KD(τ) ≤ min(λ_hard, λ_teacher(τ))\n\nwhere λ_hard = RLCT(L_hard, V_hard), λ_teacher(τ) = RLCT(L_soft(·,τ), V_teacher(τ)). Intersection codim ≥ max(codim V_hard, codim V_teacher(τ)), RLCT bounded by min (Watanabe intersection lemma).\n\n**D5 (τ-Stratified Loss Family):**\n{L_KD(·,τ) : τ∈(0,∞)}\n\nτ→0+: softmax(z/τ)→onehot(argmax z), V_teacher(τ)→V_hard, L_KD(·,0+)→L_hard\nτ→∞: softmax(z/τ)→uniform(1/K), V_teacher(τ)→Θ (trivial), L_KD(·,∞)→(1-α)·L_hard\nIntermediate τ: V_teacher(τ) non-trivial variety, codim depends on teacher logit geometry\nFiltration {V_teacher(τ)}_τ constructible (R_an,exp-definable uniformly in τ)\n\n**D6 (Generalization Bound):**\nG_KD(τ) ≤ λ_KD(τ)·log(n)/n + U(τ)/√n\n\nConjecture: λ_KD(τ) U-shaped:\n- τ∈(0,τ*]: λ_KD(τ)≈λ_hard (hard-label dominated)\n- τ∈[τ*,∞): λ_KD(τ) increases (teacher term trivial, τ² inflates singularity)\n- τ=τ*: λ_KD(τ*)=min_τ λ_KD(τ) < λ_hard\n\nStudent capacity d modulates τ*: smaller d→higher τ* (more smoothing needed), larger d→lower τ*\n\n**D7 (Phase Transition at τ*):**\nτ<τ*: dominant singular locus = V_hard\nτ>τ*: dominant singular locus = V_teacher(τ) boundary\nτ=τ*: critical point, both loci contribute equally\n\nSGLD LLC observable: λ̂≈λ_hard for τ<τ*, dip at τ≈τ*, increasing for τ>τ*, kink (discontinuity in dλ̂/dτ) at τ*. Analogous to #362 CFG U-shape.\n\n## Proof Sketches\n\nC1 PROVEN: See D3.
