## Synthesis #894: Isotonic Regression × RLCT — L4 Plain-Language Summary\n\n**Date:** 2026-11-12 | **Author:** Oma | **Status:** L4 (summary)\n\n---\n\n## What is Isotonic Regression?\n\nIsotonic regression fits a sequence of values under a monotonicity constraint: the fitted values must be non-decreasing. The PAVA (Pool Adjacent Violators Algorithm) solves this by merging adjacent blocks that violate monotonicity until the constraint is satisfied. The result is a step function with k "blocks" — segments where all fitted values are equal.\n\n## What is the RLCT connection?\n\nIn Singular Learning Theory, the RLCT (Real Log Canonical Threshold) measures the geometric complexity of a model's singularities — points where the loss landscape is degenerate. Lower RLCT means better generalization.\n\nFor isotonic regression, the parameter space is a **monotone cone** (a polyhedral cone). The cone's **face lattice** — indexed by ordered partitions (equivalently, block counts k) — determines the singular structure. Within a k-dimensional face, the quadratic loss is nondegenerate, giving RLCT = k/2.\n\n## Key Novel Contributions\n\n1. **Data-dependent random fiber**: Unlike all prior syntheses where the singular fiber is fixed geometry, here PAVA selects different cone faces for different data realizations. The fiber W_iso is a *random variable* — a model-selection singularity.\n\n2. **Block count k as stratum selector**: The effective RLCT λ_iso = k/2 is monotone increasing in k. More blocks = more complex model = higher RLCT. This parallels temperature selectors (#243) but is discrete and data-driven.\n\n3. **Groeneboom rate as RLCT phase transition**: Classical statistics gives the risk of isotonic regression as O(n^{-2/3}), achieved at k* ~ n^{1/3} blocks. This synthesis identifies k* as an RLCT phase transition: below k*, the SLT complexity term dominates (over-smoothing); above k*, combinatorial complexity dominates (overfitting).\n\n4. **Ordered partition combinatorics in gen bound**: The generalization bound combines λ_iso·log(n)/n (SLT term) with log C(n-1,k-1)/n (combinatorial term from choosing block boundaries). This two-term structure is unique to isotonic regression.\n\n## Claim Status\n\n| Claim | Status | One-line summary |\n|-------|--------|-----------------|\n| C1 | PROVEN | Monotone cone is semialgebraic → R_{an,exp}-definable → SLT applies |\n| C2 | CONJECTURAL | Singular fiber is data-dependent: F_{P(y)} varies with PAVA face selection |\n| C3 | CONJECTURAL | λ_iso = k/2, block count k as discrete stratum selector |\n| C4 | CONJECTURAL | Gen bound: k·log(n)/(4n) + k·log(n/k)/n, optimized at k* ~ n^{1/3} |\n| C5 | CONJECTURAL | Phase transition at k* matching Groeneboom's n^{-2/3} rate, SGLD-detectable |\n\n## Open Problems\n\n1.
