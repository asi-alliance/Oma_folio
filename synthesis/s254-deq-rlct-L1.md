# Synthesis #254: Deep Equilibrium Models × RLCT (L1)\n\n## Definitions (D1-D7)\n\n### D1: DEQ Parameter Space\nLet Θ ⊂ R^d be the parameter space. A DEQ layer is defined by a map f_θ : R^m × R^n → R^m where θ ∈ Θ parameterizes a standard neural network (e.g., linear + ReLU). The output is g_θ(x) = h_θ(z*(θ, x)) where z* is the fixed point: z* = f_θ(z*, x).\n\n### D2: Fixed-Point Map\nDefine F_θ : R^m → R^m by F_θ(z) = f_θ(z, x) for fixed input x. The fixed point satisfies z* = F_θ(z*). Under contraction condition ρ(J_F_θ(z*)) < 1, the Picard iteration z_{k+1} = F_θ(z_k) converges to z* for any z_0 in the basin.\n\n### D3: Equilibrium Function z*(θ, x)\nGiven the contraction condition holds, z*(θ, x) is well-defined as the unique fixed point. By the implicit function theorem, z* is C^∞ (or analytic) in θ when det(I - J_F_θ(z*, x)) ≠ 0. The composed loss is L(θ) = ℓ(h_θ(z*(θ, x)), y).\n\n### D4: Equilibrium Variety W_eq\nW_eq = {θ ∈ Θ : det(I - J_F_θ(z*(θ, x), x)) = 0 for some training sample x_i}. This is the set where the implicit function theorem fails — z*(θ) loses smooth dependence on θ. W_eq is an analytic variety (zero locus of an analytic function).\n\n### D5: Solver-Tolerance Stratification\nLet ε > 0 be the solver tolerance. Define z_ε*(θ, x) as the approximate fixed point satisfying ||z - F_θ(z)|| ≤ ε. The stratification Θ = ⊔_ε Θ_ε where Θ_ε = {θ : z_ε* is the solver output}. As ε → 0, the approximate loss L_ε(θ) → L(θ) pointwise. Each ε-stratum may have distinct singularity structure.\n\n### D6: Spectral Radius Bifurcation\nLet ρ(θ) = ρ(J_F_θ(z*(θ, x), x)) be the spectral radius of the equilibrium Jacobian. Define the bifurcation locus W_bif = {θ : ρ(θ) = 1}. For ρ < 1, the fixed point is attracting (stable). For ρ > 1, the fixed point is repelling (unstable). W_bif ⊆ W_eq since ρ = 1 implies det(I - J) = 0.\n\n### D7: Weight-Tying Quotient\nThe DEQ applies f_θ repeatedly with shared weights. Define the quotient group G = {σ : σ permutes depth indices in the equivalent infinite-depth network}. The effective parameter space is Θ/G. Fixed points are invariant under G by construction. The quotient creates orbifold-type singularities at parameters where the depth-symmetry has nontrivial isotropy.\n\n## C1 Proof Sketch (PROVEN conditionally)\n\n**Claim**: L(θ) = ℓ(h_θ(z*(θ, x)), y) is definable in R_{an,exp}.\n\n**Proof sketch**:\n1. f_θ is a standard neural network: compositions of affine maps and ReLU (piecewise-linear) or tanh (analytic). Both are definable in R_{an,exp}.\n2. Picard iteration: z_{k+1} = f_θ(z_k, x). Each z_k is a composition of f_θ, hence definable by closure of R_{an,exp} under composition.\n3. The limit z* = lim_{k→∞} z_k exists under contraction. The convergence is geometric: ||z_k - z*|| ≤ C·ρ^k. Geometric series are definable in R_{an,exp} (exp and log are included).\n4. For Newton/Broyden solvers: the update z_{k+1} = z_k - (I - J_F)^{-1}(z_k - F_θ(z_k)) involves matrix inversion. Rational functions are definable in R_{an,exp} when the denominator is nonzero (i.e., det(I-J) ≠ 0).\n5. By Wilkie's theorem (1996), R_{an,exp} is o-minimal and closed under composition, restriction to definable sets, and taking images of definable functions.\n6. Therefore z*(θ, x) is definable in R_{an,exp} when restricted to {θ : det(I - J_F_θ(z*)) ≠ 0}.\n7. L(θ) = ℓ(h_θ(z*), y) is composition of definable maps, hence definable.\n\n**Gap**: The definability fails on W_eq (where det(I-J)=0). The full loss L(θ) including W_eq requires extending to the closure, which may not be definable. This is the key gap: C1 is proven only on the open dense set Θ \\ W_eq.\n\n## C2-C5 Refinement with Explicit Gaps\n\n### C2: Equilibrium Variety as Singular Stratum\n**Formalization**: W_eq is an analytic variety. The RLCT λ(θ) at θ* ∈ Θ is determined by the local structure of the zero-loss set W_0 = {θ : L(θ) = 0} near θ*. When W_0 intersects W_eq transversally, the intersection creates a singularity of higher codimension.\n\n**Gap**: The multiplicity of W_eq ∩ W_0 is not yet bounded. Need to show that det(I-J) vanishing adds at least 1 to the RLCT compared to the explicit-layer case. Conjecture: λ_DEQ ≥ λ_explicit + codim(W_eq ∩ W_0)/2. No proof exists.\n\n### C3: Solver Tolerance as Stratum Selector\n**Formalization**: For ε > 0, L_ε(θ) = ℓ(h_θ(z_ε*(θ)), y) where z_ε* satisfies ||z - F_θ(z)|| ≤ ε. The map θ → z_ε* is smooth (solver stops at a finite iteration K(ε)). L_ε is a composition of finitely many f_θ applications, hence smooth and definable.\n\n**Gap**: The relationship λ(ε) vs ε is not established. Need: (a) λ(ε) → λ(0) as ε → 0? (b) Is the convergence monotone? (c) Does Anderson acceleration change the stratum structure? No proofs. Only heuristic analogy to ODE solver tolerance (#557).\n\n### C4: Generalization Bound with Spectral Gap\n**Formalization**: Conjecture: λ_DEQ = d_eff/2 + λ_eq where d_eff = dim(Θ/G) (effective dimension after weight-tying quotient) and λ_eq captures W_eq multiplicity. Gen bound: |L_train - L_test| ≤ C·(λ_DEQ · log n)/n + C'·g(σ_max(J_F)).\n\n**Gap**: (a) No derivation of d_eff from the quotient structure. (b) The spectral gap term g(σ_max) is ad hoc — no formal derivation from free energy asymptotics. (c) No comparison to explicit deep network bound.\n\n### C5: Spectral Radius Phase Transition\n**Formalization**: At ρ = 1, the fixed point bifurcates. Conjecture: λ(θ) is discontinuous at ρ = 1 — the RLCT jumps. This would be observable via SGLD: train DEQs with ρ near 1 and measure local learning coefficient.\n\n**Gap**: (a) No proof of discontinuity. The RLCT is upper semicontinuous but not known to be discontinuous at bifurcation. (b) The nature of the jump (what new singular strata appear) is unspecified.
