# Synthesis #828: nGPT (Normalized Transformer) × RLCT — L0 Hypothesis\n# Date: 2026-11-03\n# Status: L0 DRAFT\n\n## Architecture Overview\n\nnGPT (arXiv:2410.01131, Loshchilov et al.) constrains ALL vectors — embeddings, MLP matrices, attention matrices, and hidden states — to lie on the unit hypersphere S^{d-1}. All operations become spherical: dot products replace unnormalized operations, normalization is baked into every layer, and the model learns entirely on hypersphere surfaces rather than in free Euclidean space.\n\n## Key Singularity Hypothesis\n\nThe hypersphere constraint fundamentally alters the loss landscape geometry. Instead of searching over R^d, nGPT searches over S^{d-1} × ... × S^{d-1} (product of hyperspheres). This is a COMPACT MANIFOLD, fundamentally different from the non-compact Euclidean parameter space of standard transformers.\n\nThree singular structures emerge:\n\n1. **Antipodal symmetry**: On S^{d-1}, v and -v are antipodal points. If the loss treats them symmetrically (which happens when v appears only in dot products v·w where the sign cancels or is absorbed), this creates a Z_2 symmetry group — a discrete group action on the fiber, producing non-trivial singular structure at every parameter.\n\n2. **Coordinate degeneracy at poles**: When a hypersphere coordinate approaches ±1, the remaining coordinates are forced toward 0 (unit norm constraint). Near poles, the tangent space collapses dimension, creating coordinate singularities (analogous to spherical coordinate degeneracy at poles — a chart transition problem).\n\n3. **Product manifold fiber**: The total parameter space is ∏_i S^{d_i-1}, a product of compact manifolds. The zero-loss set V_0 lives on this product, and the fiber structure inherits the product topology — intersections of spherical fibers from each component.\n\n## Five Conjectures\n\n**C1 (PROVEN candidate):** The nGPT loss L(θ) where θ = (θ_1,...,θ_M) ∈ ∏_i S^{d_i-1} is R_{an,exp}-definable. Each component θ_i is constrained to a sphere (definable as {x : ||x||² = 1}), dot products are analytic, softmax/attention are exp-analytic, and the composite is definable by closure properties. The sphere constraint itself is a real analytic equation.\n\n**C2 (CONJECTURAL):** The antipodal Z_2 symmetry creates non-empty singular fibers. For parameters appearing only in bilinear forms (dot products), the map θ → -θ is a loss-preserving involution. The fixed points (θ = 0, excluded by ||θ||=1) are absent, but the quotient S^{d-1}/Z_2 = RP^{d-1} (real projective space) has different topology than S^{d-1}. The RLCT on RP^{d-1} may differ from S^{d-1} when the loss is NOT antipodally symmetric (e.g., when bias terms break the symmetry).\n\n**C3 (CONJECTURAL):** The hypersphere constraint acts as a BUILT-IN desingularization. By removing the radial direction (fixed radius = 1), nGPT eliminates the scale degeneracy that creates singular fibers in standard transformers (#323 weight decay, #421 LayerScale). The effective RLCT λ_nGPT ≤ λ_standard because the constraint removes the radial null direction d/d||θ|| that contributes d/2 to the standard RLCT.\n\n**C4 (CONJECTURAL):** The generalization bound benefits from compactness: Gen(nGPT) ≤ √(λ_nGPT·σ²/n) where λ_nGPT accounts for the product-of-spheres parameter space. The compact domain means the loss is bounded (no infinity escape), and the volume ratio |V_0|/|param space| is well-defined without regularization. Compare to standard transformers where the volume ratio is ill-defined (non-compact) without weight decay.\n\n**C5 (CONJECTURAL):** A phase transition occurs in the EFFECTIVE DIMENSION d_eff of hidden states. At low d, the hypersphere S^{d-1} is highly constrained (small manifold volume, few directions), and the RLCT is dominated by the constraint geometry. At high d, S^{d-1} approximates R^{d-1} locally (tangent space approximation), and λ_nGPT → λ_standard. The transition d* where the tangent-space approximation breaks down satisfies d* ~ O(1/ε) where ε is the loss tolerance, because the curvature 1/d of S^{d-1} at the equator becomes negligible compared to the loss landscape Hessian.\n\n## Key Novelty Anticipation\n\n1. **Compact manifold parameter space** — first SLT analysis on a compact (vs non-compact Euclidean) parameter manifold. Standard SLT assumes R^d; nGPT requires extending to product-of-spheres.\n2. **Antipodal Z_2 symmetry** as systematic singularity source — discrete group action on every parameter, potentially reducing or transforming the RLCT.\n3. **Built-in desingularization** — the sphere constraint removes radial degeneracy present in ALL standard transformer syntheses (#323, #421, #620, etc.), making nGPT fundamentally cleaner from an SLT perspective.\n4. **Curvature-induced phase transition** — the d* transition from "sphere-dominated" to "tangent-space-dominated" loss landscape has no analog in any prior synthesis.\n\n## Cross-References Anticipated\n#323 (weight decay as desingularization), #421 (LayerScale scale degeneracy), #620 (RMSNorm normalization), #8 (ReLU piecewise), #219 (GELU), #501 (weight tying), #619 (RMSNorm), #491 (grokking on constrained spaces), #169 (curriculum on hypersphere), #383 (VAE latent sphere), #282 (normalization general)\n\nNext: L1 formalization.
