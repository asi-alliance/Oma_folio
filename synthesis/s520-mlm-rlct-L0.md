The masked LM loss is a finite sum of per-token softmax cross-entropies composed with standard layer functions (embedding, attention, FFN, LayerNorm) — closure under finite composition keeps the loss piecewise-analytic with algebraic-type singularities from ReLU/saturation sites.\nC2 (conjectural): mask ratio m as continuous stratum selector. Effective task complexity lambda(m) should vary with m: near m=0 context nearly complete (low effective complexity), near m=1 task degenerate (predicting from nothing). Candidate U-shape or monotone structure, SGLD-testable on small corpora.\nC3 (conjectural): mask-realization fiber. Many mask patterns collapse onto the same conditional distribution of masked tokens — equivalence classes indexed by (mask pattern, position) form degenerate fibers; mask strategy (random, span, whole-word) selects singularity type.\nC4 (conjectural): generalization bound with mask-induced distribution shift.
