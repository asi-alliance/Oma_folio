#666 Latent Diffusion Models (LDM) × RLCT — L0 Exploration\n## Date: 2026-10-28\n\n## Motivation\n\nLatent Diffusion Models (LDMs, e.g., Stable Diffusion) perform diffusion in a compressed latent space learned by a VAE encoder rather than in pixel space. This creates a two-stage architecture: (1) VAE encoder E_φ maps data x → latent z = E_φ(x), (2) diffusion model s_θ(z_t, t) learns the score in latent space.\n\nFrom the RLCT perspective, this creates a COMPOSITE singularity structure:\n- The VAE encoder has its own singular fiber V_VAE (posterior collapse, from #515)\n- The diffusion score model has its own time-parameterized singular fiber W_t (from #167)\n- LDM training couples them: the diffusion loss depends on encoder outputs, creating an intersection/composition fiber\n\nThe key question: how does the VAE compression affect the diffusion model's singular geometry? The latent dimension d_lat acts as a novel stratum selector governing a tradeoff between reconstruction quality (VAE fiber complexity) and diffusion simplicity (score fiber complexity in compressed space).\n\n## Prior Work\n\n- #167 (Diffusion × RLCT): Standard pixel-space diffusion. Time-parameterized singular fiber W_t = {θ: s_θ(x_t,t) = ∇log p_t(x_t)}. C2-C5 conjectural. Does NOT address latent-space diffusion or VAE coupling.\n- #515 (VAE × RLCT): VAE ELBO, posterior collapse fiber V_VAE = {θ: KL(q_φ(z|x)||p(z))=0}, d_z as stratum selector. Does NOT address downstream diffusion in latent space.\n- #365 (DDIM/DPM-Solver × RLCT): Sampling algorithms for diffusion, not training geometry.\n- #651 (Flow Matching × RLCT): Flow matching in parameter space, not latent diffusion.\n- #383 (VAE × RLCT, earlier): Same as #515, ELBO and KL structure.\n- Zero catalog entries for "latent diffusion" or "LDM" confirmed.\n\n## Novel Composite Bridge\n\nThe LDM composite loss is:\n\nL_LDM(θ, φ) = L_diff(θ; φ) + β · L_recon(φ)\n\nwhere:\n- L_diff(θ; φ) = E_{t,x,ε} [||s_θ(z_t, t) - (-ε·σ_t^{-1})||²] with z_t = √(α_t)·E_φ(x) + √(1-α_t)·ε (diffusion in latent space)\n- L_recon(φ) = ELBO of VAE = E_x[log p_ψ(D_ψ(z)|x)] - KL(q_φ(z|x)||p(z)) (reconstruction + regularization)\n- β balances diffusion training vs reconstruction quality\n\n## Five Candidate Claims\n\n**C1 (PROVABLE): R_an,exp Definability**\nL_LDM is definable in R_an,exp:\n- E_φ(x): neural network (analytic composition) → z ∈ R^{d_lat}\n- z_t = √(α_t)·z + √(1-α_t)·ε: affine in z for fixed t (polynomial + Gaussian)\n- s_θ(z_t, t): neural network (analytic)\n- ||s_θ - target||²: squared norm (polynomial)\n- L_recon: ELBO = Gaussian log-likelihood (exp) + KL (analytic on simplex)\n- β-weighted sum: closed under scalar multiplication and addition\n- All components in R_an,exp → L_LDM ∈ R_an,exp. ∎\n\n**C2 (CONJECTURAL): Composite Singular Fiber**\nThe zero-loss fiber of LDM is:\n\nV_LDM = V_VAE(φ) ∩ V_diff(θ; φ)\n\nwhere:\n- V_VAE(φ) = {φ : L_recon(φ) = 0} = perfect reconstruction + zero KL (posterior collapse fiber from #515)\n- V_diff(θ; φ) = {θ : s_θ(z_t(E_φ(x)), t) = ∇log p_t(z) ∀t, x} = perfect score matching in latent space (fiber from #167, composed with encoder)\n\nKey insight: V_diff(θ; φ) DEPENDS ON φ — the encoder parameters determine the latent space geometry, which determines the score function the diffusion model must learn. This is a PARAMETER-COUPLED fiber, not a simple intersection.\n\nThe composite RLCT satisfies:\n\nλ_LDM ≤ min(λ_VAE(φ*), λ_diff(θ*; φ*))\n\nunder transversality, but the coupling means the fibers are NOT independent — changing φ transforms the latent space, which changes V_diff.\n\n**C3 (CONJECTURAL): d_lat as Continuous Stratum Selector**\nLatent dimension d_lat governs a fundamental tradeoff:\n\n- Low d_lat (high compression): V_VAE has higher codimension (harder reconstruction), λ_VAE increases. But latent space is simpler (lower-dimensional), so score matching is easier, λ_diff decreases.\n- High d_lat (low compression): V_VAE has lower codimension (easy reconstruction), λ_VAE decreases. But latent space is high-dimensional, score matching harder, λ_diff increases.\n\nThis creates a U-shaped or saddle-shaped λ_LDM(d_lat):\n\nλ_LDM(d_lat) ≈ λ_VAE(d_lat) + λ_diff(d_lat)\n\nwhere λ_VAE decreases with d_lat and λ_diff increases with d_lat. The optimal d_lat* minimizes the composite RLCT.\n\nThis is distinct from #515 (where d_z alone selects VAE strata) and #167 (where t selects diffusion strata) — here d_lat selects a COMPOSITE stratum through both fibers simultaneously.\n\n**C4 (CONJECTURAL): Generalization Bound with Composite RLCT**\n\nG_LDM(d_lat, β) ≤ λ_LDM(d_lat, β) · log(n) / n + U(d_lat, β) / √n\n\nwhere:\n- λ_LDM(d_lat, β) = composite RLCT at the optimal joint fiber\n- U(d_lat, β) = singular fluctuation (depends on both VAE and diffusion stratum complexity)\n- β modulates the reconstruction-diffusion tradeoff, analogous to temperature in #234\n\nPrediction: optimal β*(d_lat) shifts with d_lat — higher compression (low d_lat) requires higher β (prioritize reconstruction) to prevent V_VAE from dominating the singular structure.\n\n**C5 (CONJECTURAL): Phase Transition at d_lat***\n\nAt critical d_lat*:\n- Below d_lat*: V_VAE singularity dominates (posterior collapse too aggressive, information bottleneck)\n- Above d_lat*: V_diff singularity dominates (latent space too high-dimensional, score matching curse of dimensionality)\n- At d_lat*: balanced composite singularity, minimum λ_LDM\n\nSGLD LLC observable: composite λ̂_LLC shows U-shape in d_lat, with kink at d_lat*. This is testable: train LDMs with varying latent dimensions, measure LLC via SGLD on the joint (θ, φ) parameter space.\n\nThe β-interaction creates a two-parameter phase diagram (d_lat, β) with an optimal ridge β*(d_lat).\n\n## Key Novelty vs Prior Work\n\n1.
