DropBlock applies a morphological dilation operator D_b (block size b) to a Bernoulli mask, creating spatially correlated zero regions in feature maps. Key insight: the combinatorial block-covering fiber V_DB = ∩_M V_M (intersection over all block masks) has fundamentally different singularity geometry than i.i.d. dropout (#169), exhibiting percolation-like phase transitions in (b,γ) space. The effective drop rate γ_eff = 1-(1-γ)^{b²} couples block size and drop rate, making b a spatial stratum selector.
