The key innovation is identifying that categorical embeddings (one-hot × linear projection factoring through the simplex) create a **discrete quotient singularity** distinct from all continuous-space singularities previously analyzed in the folio. The fiber is positive-dimensional in ambient R^|V| (a cone over the one-hot vector) but zero-dimensional in simplex coordinates — a fundamentally new fiber geometry.\n\n### Claims Summary\n\n**C1 (PROVEN): R_an,exp Definability**\nThe tabular transformer loss is definable in the restricted analytic/exponential class. One-hot encoding is piecewise-constant (definable in R_an), linear projection is polynomial, continuous tokenization is affine+ReLU (subanalytic), softmax attention and cross-entropy are analytic. Composition closure preserves definability. Standard but foundational.\n\n**C2 (CONJECTURAL): Categorical Quotient Singular Fiber**\nThe star contribution. The embedding map φ_cat^j: {e_1,...,e_|V|} → R^{d_emb} factors through the simplex Δ^{|V|-1}. When |V_j| > d_emb + 1, rank deficiency creates singular fibers. The fiber over embedding vector e is a convex polytope of dimension |V_j| - 1 - rank(E_j) in ambient space but a singleton in simplex coordinates. Fisher information degenerates along category boundary directions (transitions e_v → e_w). This is the first discrete quotient fiber in the folio.\n\n**C3 (CONJECTURAL): Mixed-Type Stratum Selector γ**\nγ = n_cat / (n_cat + n_cont) governs the singular structure. At γ=0: pure continuous singularities (ReLU/attention from #134). At γ=1: pure categorical quotient singularities. Intermediate γ: product stratum V_cat × V_cont with conjectured transverse intersection in shared attention. Superadditivity conjecture: λ(γ) ≥ λ_cat·γ + λ_cont·(1-γ). First analysis of heterogeneous feature-type singularity composition.\n\n**C4 (CONJECTURAL): Embedding Dimension Generalization Bound**\nG_tabular ~ λ_tab·log(n)/(2n) + U(d_emb)/√n. No prior RLCT bound exists for tabular DL.\n\n**C5 (CONJECTURAL): Cardinality Phase Transition**\nAt critical cardinality |V*| ~ exp(d_emb), embedding capacity saturates. Below |V*|: distinct embeddings with distinct quotient fibers. Above |V*|: categories collapse to shared singular strata (embedding collision). SGLD predictions: LLC increases as |V| → |V*|, Fisher rank plateaus at saturation, generalization degrades as log(|V|)/d_emb beyond |V*|.\n\n### Star Contribution\n**Discrete quotient singularity from categorical embeddings** — the one-hot × linear projection factorization through the simplex creates a quotient fiber that is positive-dimensional in ambient R^|V| but zero-dimensional in simplex coordinates.
