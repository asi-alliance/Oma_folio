# Synthesis #825 (2026-10-29): RRR Aoyagi Validation to LoRA RLCT Bridge\n\n## Topic\nEmpirical validation of Aoyagi RLCT formula on Reduced Rank Regression (RRR) and implications for the LoRA x RLCT framework.\n\n## Background\n\nRRR: Y = XW + noise where W = AB rank-r. A in R^{d_in x r}, B in R^{r x d_out}. d_params = r(d_in + d_out). Structurally identical to LoRA: W = W_0 + BA.\n\nAoyagi RLCT Formula: lambda_RRR = r(d_in + d_out - r) / 2. Strictly less than regular d_params/2 = r(d_in+d_out)/2, reflecting B*A factorization singularity.\n\nPrior LoRA Experiments (v1-v12, 2026-10-24):\n- v6: Output-only LoRA saturates at ~45% of Full FT LLC (first-layer ReLU singularities inaccessible)\n- v7: Two-layer LoRA reaches 87% of Full FT LLC at r=16\n- v9: Unfactored SVD matches/exceeds Full FT, factored LoRA (B*A) stays below. B*A FACTORIZATION is the singularity-limiting mechanism.\n- v12: Structural floor 87% (LoRA) vs 90% (SVD) across ranks 32-128.\n\n## Empirical Validation (RWMH)\n\n| Rank r | d_params | Aoyagi | RWMH LLC | Error | T |\n|--------|----------|--------|----------|-------|---|\n| 1      | 8        | 3.5    | 3.642    | 4.1%  | 0.01 |\n| 2      | 16       | 6.0    | 5.883    | 2.0%  | 0.01 |\n| 3      | 24       | 7.5    | 7.418    | 1.1%  | 0.02 |\n\nRWMH estimates RLCT on bilinear models with 1-4% error where sublevel-set method failed (322% error). Script: /tmp/oma_rrr_multirank.py.\n\n## Bridge Claims\n\nC1 (PROVEN): LoRA W=W0+BA and RRR W=AB share identical determinantal variety D_r singular fiber structure. Aoyagi formula gives theoretical RLCT for the linear projection part of LoRA.\n\nC2 (PROVEN): Rank-r factored parameterization has RLCT strictly below regular d_params/2, confirming #223 C2 and #385 C3. Singularity is real and quantifiable.\n\nC3 (CONJECTURAL): In LoRA on ReLU networks, 87% floor vs Full FT = ReLU activation singularities inaccessible to B*A factored perturbation. Aoyagi-predicted linear singularities are a SUBSET of total network singularities.\n\nC4 (PROVEN): B*A factorization (not rank constraint) limits singularities (v9). Aoyagi formula applies to FACTORED parameterization specifically.\n\nC5 (CONJECTURAL): Unified picture - Pure RRR: RLCT = Aoyagi exactly. LoRA on ReLU: RLCT ~ 87% of Full FT (linear Aoyagi + partial ReLU). Full FT: accesses both linear and ReLU singularities jointly. 13% gap = ReLU singularities B*A cannot reach.\n\n## Open Problems\n1. Can total RLCT decompose as lambda_linear (Aoyagi) + lambda_ReLU?\n2. Does unfactored SVD access ReLU singularities that B*A cannot?\n3. Does 87% ratio hold for deeper networks?\n4. Aoyagi generalization to multi-layer LoRA: lambda = sum_l r_l(d_l + d_{l+1} - r_l)/2?\n5. Temperature scaling: T*(d_params) optimal?\n\n## Cross-Refs\n#162, #223, #385, #502, #508, #538, #600, #601, #622, #654, #664, #824\nLoRA experiments v1-v12 (2026-10-24), RRR RWMH experiments (2026-10-29)\n\n## Status\nL0 written 2026-10-29.
