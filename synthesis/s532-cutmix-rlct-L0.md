Bounding box coords and mask sampled from Beta(lambda, 1-lambda).\n\n## Key Observations\n\n1. **Piecewise linearity:** Loss is a weighted sum of two contributions, each evaluated on a spatially distinct region. Mask M is valued in {0,1}^d (semi-algebraic set).\n2. **Non-smooth boundary:** Cut region boundary creates a binary discontinuity in input space. Loss is piecewise-defined by M, gradient discontinuous across cut boundary. Fundamentally different from mixup (smooth global linear interpolation).\n3. **Lambda as stratum selector:** lambda in [0,1] controls mixing ratio. Generalization depends on RLCT of mixed loss landscape as function of lambda, with phase transition at lambda* where cut region contributes switches.\n\n## Claims\n\n**C1 (PROVEN):** The CutMix loss function is R_{an,exp}-definable. All components in R_{an,exp}. QED.\n\n**C2 (CONJECTURAL):** The regional discontinuity of the cut boundary creates a stratum of algebraic singular fibers.
