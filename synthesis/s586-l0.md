#586: Model Merging × RLCT\n\n## L0 — Seed\n\n### Topic\nModel merging (weight interpolation, task arithmetic, TIES) analyzed through Singular Learning Theory (SLT) via the Real Log Canonical Threshold (RLCT).\n\n### Motivation\nModel merging methods (model soups, task arithmetic, TIES) interpolate or combine trained model weights along paths in parameter space. SLT tells us that learnability is governed by the geometry of singularities in the loss landscape. The intersection of a merging path (line, translation, or sign-resolved combination) with the singular fibers of parent models creates a new singular structure for the merged model — potentially cleaner or more complex than either parent.\n\n### Key Mechanism\nWeight interpolation: W_merged = αW_A + (1-α)W_B traces a line segment through parameter space. The singular fiber of the merged model at each α is the intersection of this line with the critical locus of the loss function. Task arithmetic (W_base + τ·δ_task) translates the fiber. TIES resolves sign conflicts by discrete selection, adding a stratification.\n\n### Hypotheses\nC1 (Definability): The merged loss is R_an,exp-definable — linear interpolation, sign resolution, and polynomial network entries preserve semi-algebraic structure.\nC2 (Fiber Geometry): The singular fiber of the merged model is the intersection of the interpolation path with the parents' critical loci — generically lower-dimensional, hence potentially lower RLCT.\nC3 (Stratum Selector): α (interpolation ratio) acts as a continuous stratum selector; TIES sign-disjunction adds a discrete stratum selector on top.\nC4 (Bound): Model soup RLCT ≤ min(RLCT_A, RLCT_B) — averaging over the loss landscape tends toward flatter regions (analogous to SWA flat-minima connection, #420).\nC5 (Phase Transition): There exists a critical α* where the interpolation path crosses a singularity boundary, producing a phase transition in generalization behavior.\n\n### Distinction\n- #420 (Lookahead/SAM): Flatness-seeking optimization, not weight-space interpolation.\n- #526 (Schedule-free): Optimizer trajectory, not model combination.\n- #160 (DPO): Preference loss singularity, not weight-space geometry.\n\n### Next: L1 — Formal Setup\nDefine the parameter space, interpolation path, loss function, and critical locus.
