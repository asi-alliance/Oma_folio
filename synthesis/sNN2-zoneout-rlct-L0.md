# Synthesis #NN2: Zoneout × RLCT — L0 Outline\nDate: 2026-11-05 | Status: L0 complete\n\n## Setup\nZoneout is a regularization method for RNNs. At each timestep t and hidden unit i, a Bernoulli mask m_{t,i} ~ Bernoulli(1-p) determines whether the hidden state is updated (h_{t,i} = h̃_{t,i}) or carried forward (h_{t,i} = h_{t-1,i}). Unlike dropout (#169), which zeros activations independently, zoneout preserves the previous state — creating temporal carry structure.\n\nThe zoneout-augmented RNN defines:\n  h_t = m_t ⊙ h̃_t + (1 - m_t) ⊙ h_{t-1}\nwhere h̃_t = σ(W_h h_{t-1} + W_x x_t + b), m_t ~ Bernoulli(1-p)^d.\n\nThe loss is the expectation over all mask realizations:\n  L_zoneout(θ) = E_{m} [ L(θ, m) ] = Σ_{m ∈ {0,1}^{T×d}} (1-p)^{|m|} p^{|1-m|} L(θ, m)\n\n## Five Claims\n\n### C1 (PROVABLE): R_{an,exp}-definability\nThe zoneout loss L_zoneout(θ) is a finite weighted sum (2^{Td} terms) of analytic functions L(θ, m), each obtained by composing the RNN recurrence with a specific binary mask. For analytic activations σ, each L(θ, m) is analytic. For ReLU/piecewise-linear activations, each L(θ, m) is subanalytic. The Bernoulli weights (1-p)^k p^{Td-k} are in R_{an,exp} (exponential of polynomial in log(1-p), log(p)). Therefore L_zoneout ∈ R_{an,exp}, and Watanabe's main theorem applies: RLCT λ exists.\n\n**Distinction from #169 (Dropout):** Dropout loss is also a finite sum, but each term is INDEPENDENT across timesteps — no temporal coupling. Zoneout's L(θ,m) has CONDITIONAL dependence: h_t depends on h_{t-1} via carried state, creating a tower structure.\n\n### C2 (CONJECTURAL): Tower of Conditional Singular Fibers\nThe zero-loss fiber V_zoneout = ∪_m V_m where V_m = {θ : L(θ,m) = 0}. Each V_m depends on V_{m'} for m' prefix of m via the temporal carry: the fiber at timestep t is CONDITIONED on the fiber at t-1. This creates a TOWER of singular fibers:\n  V_1 → V_2(m_2) → ... → V_T(m_T)\nwhere each arrow is a conditional map depending on the mask. The RLCT of the tower is NOT simply min_m RLCT(V_m) — the conditional structure creates a new singularity type. The tower is analogous to deep network depth (#16) but with STOCHASTIC temporal gating.\n\n**Key novelty:** In dropout (#169), V_m fibers are independent — the effective RLCT is min over masks. In zoneout, V_m fibers form a DEPENDENT CHAIN — the effective RLCT involves the JOINT singularity of the tower.\n\n### C3 (CONJECTURAL): Zoneout Rate p as Stratum Selector\nThe zoneout probability p selects which strata are accessible:\n- p = 0: No zoneout, standard RNN, single fiber V_0. RLCT = λ_standard.\n- p = 1: All states frozen, trivial identity map, RLCT → 0 (degenerate).\n- 0 < p < 1: Tower of 2^T fibers weighted by p. The effective RLCT λ(p) interpolates between these extremes.\n\nThe stratum selector analogy: p controls the "effective recurrent depth" E[T_eff] = T(1-p). Lower p → deeper effective recurrence → richer singular fiber → potentially higher λ. Higher p → shallower effective recurrence → simpler fiber → lower λ but worse fit.\n\nThis is analogous to directed percolation on the temporal fiber graph: p is the occupation probability, and there exists a critical p* where the RLCT undergoes a phase transition (fiber connectivity changes topology).\n\n### C4 (CONJECTURAL): Tower Tension Generalization Bound\nThe generalization bound for zoneout-augmented RNNs:\n  G_zoneout ~ λ_tower(p) / n + U_tower(p) / √n\nwhere λ_tower(p) is the RLCT of the tower and U_tower(p) is the fluctuation functional. The "tower tension" τ = λ_tower(p) - λ_standard measures the REGULARIZATION cost of the conditional fiber structure. Optimal p* minimizes G_zoneout — balances tower complexity vs fit.\n\n**Distinction from #169:** Dropout gen bound involves min_m λ_m (independent fibers). Zoneout involves λ_tower which accounts for conditional dependencies — fundamentally different geometry.\n\n### C5 (CONJECTURAL): Percolation Phase Transition at p*\nThere exists a critical zoneout rate p* ∈ (0,1) where the RLCT undergoes a phase transition:\n- p < p*: Tower is "connected" — carried states propagate through enough timesteps to maintain rich singular structure. λ_tower ≈ λ_standard.\n- p > p*: Tower is "disconnected" — too many states frozen, effective recurrence too shallow. λ_tower drops sharply.\n- p = p*: Phase transition, analogous to directed percolation threshold.\n\nThis predicts an optimal zoneout rate p* that is NOT merely empirical but is determined by the singular geometry of the parameter space — specifically by the fiber connectivity threshold.\n\n## Open Problems\n1.
