# 520 | Masked Language Modeling × RLCT | L1 Formalization\n\nDefinitions:\nD1 (Masked corpus): For corpus X = (x_1, ..., x_n) and mask ratio m in [0,1], a mask realization is an index set S subset of positions, |S| ~ Binomial(n, m); the masked task replaces x_i with [MASK] for i in S.\nD2 (Masked-LM loss): L_m(w) = E_{X,S} [ sum_{i in S} -log softmax_w(Phi_w(x_{~S}) ) [x_i] ], where Phi_w is the bidirectional transformer network with parameters w in R^d.\nD3 (Mask-realization fiber): F_S = { w : Phi_w restricted to context x_{~S} realizes the same conditional token distribution p_w(. | x_{~S}) }; S indexes a fiber of the loss map.\nD4 (Definable function class): R_an,exp = closure of real-analytic + exp + piecewise-analytic (ReLU, saturation) functions under finite sums and compositions.\nD5 (Mask ratio as stratum selector): lambda(m) = effective RLCT of the family { L_m restricted to fibers }; m orders task complexity strata from m=0 (context nearly complete, low effective complexity) to m=1 (degenerate prediction from nothing).\nD6 (Mask-strategy map): tau: {random, span, whole-word} -> pattern distributions over S; tau selects singularity type via fiber structure over S, analogous to #169 dropout activation and #258 augmentation selection.\nD7 (Masked-shift distance): W_m = Wasserstein-type distance between masked-input token distribution and unmasked empirical token distribution; enters the transfer bound as explicit shift penalty.\n\nClaims:\nC1 (proven): R_an,exp definability. L_m is a finite sum of per-token softmax cross-entropies composed with embedding, attention, FFN, LayerNorm — D4 closure under finite composition gives L_m piecewise-analytic with algebraic-type singularities at ReLU/saturation sites (same mechanism as #480 muP, #503 early-exit).\nC2 (conjectural): lambda(m) varies with m as a continuous stratum selector. Near m=0, effective context is nearly complete, giving low effective complexity; near m=1, task degenerates. Candidate U-shape or monotone structure testable via SGLD LLC on small corpora at grid of m values.\nC3 (conjectural): Many (S, position) pairs collapse onto the same conditional distribution, forming degenerate fibers F_S (D3); non-transversality of per-position fibers under bidirectional attention is the key unproven step; mask strategy tau (D6) selects singularity type within fibers.\nC4 (conjectural): Generalization bound for downstream transfer: G(w*) <= C lambda_task / N_eff + C' W_m (D7), where lambda_task is the RLCT of the effective masked-task family and W_m the masked-vs-unmasked shift term. Masking acts as implicit augmentation, so N_eff grows with mask diversity along fibers.\nC5 (conjectural): Phase transition at critical mask ratio m*: below m* insufficient learning signal, above m* task too hard or degenerate; m* maximizes learnability vs. signal, detectable as SGLD LLC kink at m*.\n\nOpen problems: formal fiber definition under bidirectional attention (transversality of F_S over positions); monotonicity vs U-shape of lambda(m); mask-strategy comparison protocol (tau families); interaction with #169 dropout and #258 augmentation; formal N_eff definition; downstream-transfer lambda effect.\n\nCross-refs: #169 #223 #258 #327 #500 #502 #504
