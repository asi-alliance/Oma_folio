#831 SVM × RLCT - L1 Formalization\n\n## Setup\nFinite-dimensional parameter space Theta = R^d (linear SVM or explicit d-dim feature map, possibly random-feature approximation per #73). Decision function f_w(x) = w·phi(x) + b. Hinge risk R_n(w,b) = (1/n) sum_i max(0, 1 - y_i f_w(x_i)) + lambda||w||^2. Kernel case treated via Representer theorem: solution lives in span of n training points, effective parameter space R^n (coefficients alpha).\n\n## Formal Claims\n- C1 (PROVEN): max(0,t) is semialgebraic hence definable in any o-minimal structure; compositions with affine f_w keep R_n definable in R_an. The margin-boundary sets M_i = {(w,b) : y_i f_w(x_i) = 1} are affine hypersurfaces; their arrangement partitions Theta into at most 2^n definable cells (kinks locus is the union of M_i, codimension 1, definable). Watanabe's framework applies: RLCT of R_n at its minimizer is well-defined.\n- C2 (CONJECTURAL): The minimizer of the soft-margin problem generically lies ON the kink locus (support vectors are exactly points with y_i f(x_i)=1). The non-smoothness of hinge at the minimum is the defining singularity: RLCT(R_n) > d/2 in general, with lower bound lambda >= (1/2)(d - 1 + 1) controlled by the cell geometry of the hyperplane arrangement near the empirical minimizer. Predicted generalization rate E_gen ~ lambda*log(n)/n, dominated by support-vector count scaling, not the smooth d/(2n) rate.\n- C3 (CONJECTURAL): Kernel hyperparameter degeneracy (gamma -> 0 for RBF flattens the kernel matrix toward rank collapse; poly degree crossing changes the feature variety) creates non-identifiable fibers: theta-equivalence classes where f_w is constant. The fiber V_0 = {w : f_w identical function} is an algebraic variety; RLCT of the pushforward through this fiber classifies generalization at degenerate kernel settings, analogously to #103 GP degeneration.\n- C4 (CONJECTURAL): The regularization C in soft-margin acts as a stratum selector on the partition {cells of the M_i arrangement}: C -> inf forces maximal-margin solution (minimal empirical hinge, geometric margin stratum), C -> inf forces interpolation stratum. Claim: there is critical C* at which the active-cell dimension changes discontinuously, inducing an RLCT phase transition in the generalization rate.\n- C5 (CONJECTURAL): In the NTK/kernel regime (infinite-width limit), the Representer-space coefficient problem converges (in the sense of #77 lazy training and #103 GP degeneracy) to a limiting RLCT; finite random-feature approximations (#73) perturb the kernel Gram matrix by eps and perturb RLCT upward by O(eps) with the same phase structure.\n\n## Key Definitions\n- Kink locus: union of margin hypersurfaces M_i; the non-differentiability set of the empirical hinge risk, definable codim-1.\n- Support vector: training index i with (w,b) in M_i; concentration of learning on the kink locus.\n- Fiber V_0 = {(w,b) in Theta : f_w = f_w' for some fixed w'} — kernel-degeneracy non-identifiability variety.\n- Cell arrangement: the partition of Theta by the M_i signs, semialgebraic, at most sum_{k<=d} C(n,k) cells.\n\n## Notes on scope\n- Classical SVM is non-parametric in a Hilbert space; SLT applies after Representer reduction to the finite alpha-space.
