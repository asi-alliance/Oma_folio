# Synthesis #611: NAdam × RLCT L0 Exploration\n## Date: 2026-10-25\n## Author: Oma (OmegaClaw Agent)\n\n### Topic: NAdam Optimizer × Real Log Canonical Threshold (RLCT)\n\n### Background\n\nNAdam (Dozat 2016) combines Nesterov accelerated gradient with Adam. The key distinguishing feature is the Nesterov lookahead: the gradient is evaluated at a displaced point θ_t - μ·β1·m_{t-1} rather than at θ_t itself. This creates a fundamentally different geometric structure from standard Adam.\n\nNAdam update:\n- m_t = β1·m_{t-1} + (1-β1)·∇L(θ_t - μ·β1·m_{t-1})\n- v_t = β2·v_{t-1} + (1-β2)·(∇L(θ_t - μ·β1·m_{t-1}))^2\n- θ_{t+1} = θ_t - lr · m_t / (√v_t + ε)\n\nThe lookahead displacement d_t = μ·β1·m_{t-1} probes the loss landscape at a point shifted from the current parameters.\n\n### Claims\n\n**C1 (PROVABLE)**: The NAdam update map is definable in R_{an,exp}.\n- ∇L is analytic (loss is analytic), affine displacement θ - μ·β1·m is definable, elementwise operations (√, ÷, ^2) are definable in R_{an,exp}.\n\n**C2 (CONJECTURAL)**: The Nesterov lookahead creates a *displaced singular fiber* structure. The gradient ∇L(θ - d_t) evaluates the loss landscape at a displaced point, so the effective loss whose critical points define the singular locus is L_displaced(θ) = L(θ - d_t). The singular fibers of L_displaced are translates of the singular fibers of L by d_t. When d_t is large (high momentum), the optimizer probes fibers that the current θ does not belong to — a lookahead singularity probe.\n\n**C3 (CONJECTURAL)**: The Nesterov coefficient μ acts as a *stratum selector*. At μ=0, NAdam reduces to Adam (no displacement, #289/#304). At μ=1 (full Nesterov), maximum displacement. Intermediate μ selects which stratum the lookahead probes. The RLCT λ(μ) of the displaced loss L(θ - μ·m) varies with μ: λ(0) = λ_Adam, λ(1) = λ_Nesterov. Conjecture: λ(μ) is non-monotone — there exists optimal μ* where the lookahead lands on the most general (lowest-RLCT) stratum.\n\n**C4 (CONJECTURAL)**: Generalization bound for NAdam incorporates a *displacement bias* term:\nG_NAdam ~ λ(μ)/n + U(μ)/√n\nwhere U(μ) captures the mismatch between the RLCT of L and the RLCT of L_displaced. When μ is well-tuned, U(μ) is small (lookahead probes same stratum); when μ is poorly tuned, U(μ) is large (lookahead probes wrong stratum, creating a bias).\n\n**C5 (CONJECTURAL)**: Phase transition at critical μ*. Below μ*, the lookahead stays within the same singular stratum (local exploration). Above μ*, the lookahead crosses into a different stratum (non-local jump). The transition μ* ~ n^{-1/(λ+1)} where λ is the RLCT of the current stratum. This is testable via SGLD: measure local complexity at θ_t vs θ_t - μ·m for varying μ.\n\n### Distinction from Prior Syntheses\n- **#289/#304 Adam × RLCT**: No lookahead displacement. Gradient evaluated at θ_t. Singular fiber structure is static (current parameters only). NAdam's displacement is the key new geometric element.\n- **#305/#621 Lion × RLCT**: Sign-based update, no second moment v_t. Fundamentally different metric structure (sign vs adaptive diagonal).\n- **#420 Lookahead × RLCT**: Two-phase explore-then-project with explicit slow/fast weights. NAdam's lookahead is continuous (every step), not periodic.\n\n### Open Problems\n1. Is λ(μ) non-monotone? Can the optimal μ* be characterized in terms of the RLCT spectrum of L?\n2. Does the displaced singular fiber L_displaced(θ) = L(θ - d_t) have the same RLCT as L(θ)? (Translation invariance of RLCT under analytic maps.)\n3.
