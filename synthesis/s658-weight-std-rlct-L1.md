# Synthesis #658: Weight Standardization × RLCT — L1 Formalization\n## Date: 2026-10-26\n## Status: L1 Formalization\n\n## Definitions\n\n### D1: Weight Standardization Transform\nFor each output channel j, the WS transform maps raw weights W to normalized weights:\n  W'_ij = gamma_j * (W_ij - mu_j) / (sigma_j * sqrt(fan_in))\nwhere mu_j = (1/fan_in) * sum_i W_ij, sigma_j^2 = (1/fan_in) * sum_i (W_ij - mu_j)^2.\nThis is a smooth map W in R^d -> W' in S^{d-1} × R_+ (direction × scale), where the direction lies on a unit sphere after centering + normalization.\n\n### D2: R_an,exp Definability (C1 PROVEN)\nThe WS transform is semialgebraic: mean is linear, variance is polynomial, division by sqrt(var+eps) is rational. The indicator function 1{l(x,y,W') > 0} composed with WS is definable in R_an,exp by Tarski-Seidenberg closure. Hence the loss landscape L_n(W') and the KL divergence K(W') are definable, and RLCT lambda_WS is well-defined.\n\n### D3: Sphere-Constrained Singular Fiber (C2 CONJECTURAL)\nDefine the WS-constrained parameter space: Theta_WS = {W' : ||W'_j - 0|| = gamma_j for all j} (product of spheres of radius gamma_j). The singular fiber V_0^WS = {W' in Theta_WS : K(W') = 0} lies on this compact product manifold. Unlike the unconstrained V_0 subset R^d, radial escape directions (||W'|| -> inf with K=0) are eliminated.\n\nConjecture: lambda_WS <= lambda_unconstrained. Rationale: compact domain restricts the analytic continuation of the zeta function; escape-to-infinity singularities contribute to RLCT in flat space but are absent on S^{d-1}.\n\n### D4: Gain Gamma as Stratum Selector (C3 CONJECTURAL)\nFor per-block gain gamma_j, define strata:\n  S_0: gamma_j = 0 (filter killed, degenerate fiber, dim 0)\n  S_1: gamma_j in (0, gamma*): small radius, restricted singular directions\n  S_2: gamma_j in (gamma*, inf): large radius, approaches unconstrained behavior\n\nThe RLCT lambda(gamma) is conjectured U-shaped: at gamma=0, the filter is dead (trivial RLCT=0 but no learning); at gamma->inf, WS effect vanishes (lambda -> lambda_unconstrained); optimal gamma* minimizes the generalization bound.\n\n### D5: WS-Stratified Loss Family\nDefine the family F_WS = {L_n(W'; gamma) : gamma in R_+^J}. For each gamma, the effective parameter space is a product of spheres of varying radii. The family is stratified by gamma into strata S_k with distinct RLCT values lambda_k.\n\n### D6: Generalization Bound (C4 CONJECTURAL)\nG_WS(n, gamma) <= lambda_WS(gamma) * log(n) / (2n) + U_WS(gamma) / sqrt(n)\nwhere U_WS(gamma) captures the compactness bias — the restriction to S^{d-1} reduces the effective complexity term. Conjecture: for optimal gamma*, lambda_WS(gamma*) < lambda_unconstrained, yielding tighter generalization than the same architecture without WS.\n\n### D7: Phase Transition (C5 CONJECTURAL)\nAt critical gamma*, the sphere of radius gamma* intersects the dominant singular fiber of the unconstrained loss landscape. Below gamma*, the sphere misses the most singular directions (reduced RLCT). Above gamma*, the sphere encompasses them (RLCT approaches unconstrained). This transition is detectable via SGLD LLC: a kink in the learning coefficient as gamma crosses gamma*.\n\n## Claim Status Summary\n- C1 PROVEN: Standard R_an,exp definability via algebraic/rational closure.\n- C2 CONJECTURAL: Sphere constraint reduces RLCT by removing radial singularities.
