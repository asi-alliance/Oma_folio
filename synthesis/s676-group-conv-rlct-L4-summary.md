## Synthesis #676: Group Convolution × RLCT -- L4 Summary\n**Date:** 2026-10-28\n**Status:** L4 RIGOR & COMPARISON\n\n### Claim Validation\n- **C1 (PROVEN):** Group conv loss L(θ) = squared error over group conv + ReLU layers. Convolution is polynomial in weights, ReLU is subanalytic, squared loss is analytic. All closed under composition in R_{an,exp}. Definability established.\n- **C2 (CONJECTURAL):** Block-diagonal union fiber V_g(r) = ⋃_i V_i(r). Each V_i is a determinantal variety of codim (m-r+1)(n-r+1) with m=C_out/g, n=C_in/g·k². Union structure follows from block-diagonal constraint. Confidence: high — algebraically clear, but RLCT of unions of determinantal varieties in separate blocks needs formal proof.\n- **C3 (CONJECTURAL):** g as discrete stratum selector. Strata Θ_g are disjoint (different block structures). Non-nested, unlike dropout/width selectors. RLCT subadditivity gives λ_GC(g) ≤ g·λ_single. Confidence: moderate — subadditivity is standard, but the product fiber decomposition assumption needs verification for non-separable losses.\n- **C4 (CONJECTURAL):** Generalization bound G_GC ~ λ_GC(g)/n + U_GC(g)/√n. U-shaped curve from λ decreasing in g vs U increasing (expressivity loss). Confidence: moderate — the U-shape is empirically observed in group conv architectures (ResNeXt, ShuffleNet) but the specific RLCT-based formulation is novel.\n- **C5 (CONJECTURAL):** Phase transition at g* ~ O(√(C_out·C_in)). Confidence: low — this is a dimensional analysis heuristic. Empirical validation needed.\n\n### Comparison Table\n| Feature | Dense Conv (g=1) | Group Conv (g<C) | Depthwise (g=C) |\n|---------|-----------------|-----------------|-----------------|\n| Fiber type | Single det. variety | Union of g det. varieties | Union of C rank-1 varieties |\n| RLCT λ | λ_dense (highest) | λ_GC(g) ≤ g·λ_single | λ_dw (lowest) |\n| Expressivity | Full coupling | Partial coupling | No cross-channel |\n| Stratum | Θ_1 (dim d_1) | Θ_g (dim d_1/g) | Θ_C (dim d_1/C) |\n| Fisher | Dense matrix | Block-diagonal (g blocks) | Diagonal |\n| Gen bound | λ/n + U/√n (high λ) | λ_GC/n + U_GC/√n (balanced) | λ/n + U/√n (high U) |\n\n### Key Novel Contributions\n1.
