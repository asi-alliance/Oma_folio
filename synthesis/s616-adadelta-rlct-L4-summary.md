#616 Adadelta x RLCT - L4: Summary\n\nDate: 2026-10-25\nStatus: L4 COMPLETE\n\n## Plain-Language Summary\n\nAdadelta is unique among adaptive optimizers: it uses NO learning rate. Instead, the update magnitude is determined by the ratio of two exponential moving averages — one over past gradients (r), one over past updates (s). Near the loss minimum theta*=0, both EMAs decay to zero, creating a 0/0 indeterminacy. This is not a bug — it is a singularity.\n\nWe resolve this with a blow-up: the ratio u = s/r becomes a projective coordinate on P^1. The effective learning rate is eta_eff = sqrt(u), and the parameter update is theta_{t+1} = (1 - sqrt(u)) * theta_t. Stability requires |1 - sqrt(u)| < 1, i.e., 0 < u < 4.\n\n## Claims\n\n- C1 (PROVEN): Adadelta update map is definable in R_{an,exp}. Uses sqrt, +, *, /, all subanalytic. EMA is polynomial. QED.\n- C2 (CONJECTURAL): The 0/0 singularity resolves to an exceptional divisor on the blow-up, giving a projective fiber type not seen in other optimizers.\n- C3 (CONJECTURAL): The ratio u = s/r acts as a self-emergent stratum selector — no hyperparameter chooses it; it arises from trajectory history.\n- C4 (CONJECTURAL): Effective RLCT is a function of u: lambda_eff(u). At u=1 (balanced), RLCT = 1/2. At u=0 (frozen), RLCT >= 1. Generalization bound becomes n^{-lambda_eff(u)/(2m+lambda_eff(u))}.\n- C5 (CONJECTURAL): Phase boundary at u_c ~ 4 is self-oscillating: oscillation grows u (positive feedback), damping shrinks u (negative feedback). Boundary emerges from dynamics, not from hyperparameters.\n- C6 (CONJECTURAL): Scale invariance of u = s/r (ratio is invariant under theta -> a*theta) implies RLCT is parameterization-invariant. First optimizer with this property.\n\n## Key Novelty\n\nThree firsts: (1) projective singularity from EMA ratio blow-up, (2) scale-invariant RLCT conjecture, (3) emergent oscillating phase boundary.
