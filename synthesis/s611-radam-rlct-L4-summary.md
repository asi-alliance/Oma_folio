Introduces a rectification term rho_t that\nadjusts adaptive learning rates based on variance of the gradient estimate.\nWhen variance is high (early training), RAdam falls back toward SGD-like\nbehavior; when variance stabilizes, it transitions to full Adam.\n\n### Key Contributions\n\n1. DATA-DEPENDENT EMERGENT CURRICULUM (C2, genuinely novel): RAdam is the\n   FIRST optimizer in the catalog with a curriculum that emerges from the\n   optimizer's OWN variance estimation converging. The SGD-to-Adam\n   transition is triggered by statistical convergence of the gradient noise\n   estimate, not by any external schedule or parameterization choice.\n   Contrast: #316 One-Cycle (externally scheduled), #610 NAdam (bias-\n   correction artifact).\n\n2. VARIANCE-GATED FIBER V_var (C3, genuinely novel): The stratum selected\n   by rho_t*sqrt(v_t) may mismatch the true gradient noise stratum when\n   variance estimate is inaccurate (early training). This creates a\n   VARIANCE-MISMATCH fiber, distinct from #610's V_lag (temporal mismatch).\n\n3. RECTIFICATION BENEFIT B_rect (C4, genuinely novel): Generalization bound\n   GenBound = lambda_eff*log(n)/(2n) + B_rect/sqrt(n) where B_rect captures\n   variance-deficiency bias. B_rect < 0 means variance-gating improves\n   generalization by avoiding premature stratum commitment. Distinct from\n   B_look (#610 temporal overshoot) and B_clip (#609 curvature clipping).\n\n4. EMERGENT PHASE TRANSITION rho* (C5, genuinely novel): LLC trajectory\n   shows a kink at t_warmup = floor(1/(1-beta2)), switching from SGD-like\n   (isotropic, higher LLC) to Adam-like (anisotropic, lower LLC). The\n   transition position is DATA-DEPENDENT, not fixed. Conjecture: rho* ~\n   gradient noise scale (#377).\n\n5. VARIANCE-DRIVEN vs SCHEDULED CURRICULUM TAXONOMY (D6, novel): RAdam\n   establishes a third curriculum type alongside external scheduling\n   (#316) and parameterization artifacts (#610): statistical convergence\n   of the optimizer's own variance estimate.\n\n### Claims Status\n- C1: PROVEN (R_{an,exp} definability — EMA, sqrt, division, rational rho_t)\n- C2: CONJECTURAL (variance-gated stratum selector, lambda_RAdam <= lambda_Adam)\n- C3: CONJECTURAL (variance-gated fiber V_var — semi-algebraic condition from rho_t, v_t)\n- C4: CONJECTURAL (generalization bound with rectification benefit B_rect)\n- C5: CONJECTURAL (rho* phase transition at t_warmup, LLC kink)\n\n### Distinctions from Catalog\n- #289/#304 (Adam): Retrospective EMA, NO variance rectification.
