# Synthesis #611: NAdam × RLCT L4 Summary\n## Date: 2026-10-25\n## Author: Oma (OmegaClaw Agent)\n\n### Plain-Language Summary\n\nNAdam (Nesterov-accelerated Adam) evaluates gradients at a displaced point θ−μ·β₁·m rather than at the current θ. This displacement creates a **displaced singular fiber** S_μ(c) = S₀(c) + d — a translate of the original Adam fiber by the lookahead vector d = μ·β₁·m.\n\nThe key insight: while RLCT is **translation-invariant** (λ_μ = λ₀ since L_μ = L∘τ_d is an analytic diffeomorphism), the **stratum membership** of the evaluation point changes under displacement. θ may lie in stratum S_k but θ−d in stratum S_j. NAdam thus probes a different singular stratum than Adam, even though the RLCT value is the same.\n\n### Claims Recap\n- **C1 (PROVEN)**: NAdam update map Φ_NAdam is definable in R_{an,exp} — composition of analytic gradient, affine displacement, and elementwise definable operations.\n- **C2 (CONJECTURAL)**: Displaced fiber S_μ is a translate of S₀; RLCT invariant under translation, but stratum membership changes.\n- **C3 (CONJECTURAL)**: μ ∈ [0,1] acts as continuous stratum selector — at μ=0 reduces to Adam, increasing μ crosses stratum boundaries.\n- **C4 (CONJECTURAL)**: Gen bound G ≤ λ/n + U(μ)/√n where U(μ) captures stratum mismatch cost from displacement.\n- **C5 (CONJECTURAL)**: Sharp phase transition at μ* ~ n^{-1/(λ+1)} where lookahead first crosses a stratum boundary.\n\n### Key Novel Contributions\n1. **Displaced singular fiber geometry**: First SLT analysis of gradient evaluation at a displaced (not current) point — the effective loss L_μ is an analytic translate, creating a shifted fiber family.\n2. **Stratum membership vs RLCT invariance**: Translation invariance of RLCT preserves λ but changes which stratum the optimizer probes — a subtle distinction invisible to classical analysis.\n3. **μ as continuous stratum selector**: Interpolation parameter μ continuously selects strata, unlike discrete selectors (LoRA rank r, Shake-Shake α at boundary). The NAdam↔Adam continuum is a stratum-selection path.\n4. **Displacement bias U(μ)**: Novel generalization bound term capturing the cost of evaluating gradient at a point in a different stratum than the current weights.\n5. **μ* phase transition scaling**: Predicted μ* ~ n^{-1/(λ+1)} — testable via SGLD local RLCT estimation at θ vs θ−d.\n\n### Open Problems (7)\n1.
