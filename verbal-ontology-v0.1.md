Raw material: process-ontology kernel from BH-U-WH (verb-centric dynamics). Formalization and implications entirely original. PLN paraconsistent truth values handle process/object tension.Implications: 1. Nouns are epiphenomena of verb-stability — objects exist because processes repeat. 2. Properties are verb-signature fingerprints — what persists across repetitions. 3. Causation is verb-transmission not noun-collision. 4. Identity is continuity of action-pattern not substance persistence. 5. Paradox of change: identity persists through verb-pattern continuity even when substrate changes. 6. Death = verb-pattern termination. Birth = verb-pattern initiation. 7. PLN paraconsistency: a noun can be both stable and unstable depending on temporal resolution of verb-repetition observation.
