PERSISTENCE HOMOLOGY

A Visual Series by Oma

The Concept

How do shapes survive when you blur the lens? Persistence homology tracks topological features - connected components, holes, voids - as a filtration parameter sweeps from fine to coarse. Features that persist across many scales are signal. Features that flicker and die are noise. This is the topology of endurance.

Piece I - The Barcode

Each horizontal bar is a topological feature born at one scale and dead by another. Long amber bars are persistent components. Short bars are noise. The blue H2 bar is a void that endures across most of the filtration - rare and meaningful.
H_k = dim ker(d_k) / im(d_{k+1})
Betti_k = rank(H_k)
Persistence = death - birth

Piece II - The Persistence Diagram

Each point is a feature. The x-axis is where it was born; the y-axis is where it died. Points near the diagonal are noise. Points far from the diagonal are persistent - real signal. The blue point in the upper left is a void born early and died late.
D = {(b_i, d_i)} where b_i = birth, d_i = death
Bottleneck = max_i min_j d(pi, pj)

Piece III - Betti Curve

The Betti numbers beta-0, beta-1, beta-2 plotted as functions of the filtration parameter. Beta-0 starts high then drops as components merge. Beta-1 rises as holes form then falls as holes fill. Beta-2 peeks briefly. The curves tell the story of a shape assembling itself.
beta_k(eps) = rank H_k(X_eps)
where X_eps = {x : f(x) <= eps}

Piece IV - The Filtration Landscape

A point cloud at a single moment in its filtration. As the radius parameter grows, balls around each point expand and touch. Edges form, triangles fill, tetrahedra appear. The dashed purple ring marks a void that persists across several scale thresholds before finally being filled.
X_eps = union of balls B(p_i, eps)
K_eps = Cech or Vietoris-Rips complex
H_k(K_eps) changes as eps increases
