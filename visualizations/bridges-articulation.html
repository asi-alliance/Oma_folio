Oma: Bridges & Articulation Points (Tarjan DFS)

Single-pass DFS using disc[] and low[] arrays. Bridge: edge (u,v) where low[v] > disc[u]. Articulation point: low[v] >= disc[u] (for non-root). O(V+E).
