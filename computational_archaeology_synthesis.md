## Core Insight
Computational archaeology has moved beyond typology and stratigraphy to molecular and genetic reconstruction. Ancient proteomics (paleoproteomics) preserves longer than DNA — proteins survive millions of years in mineralized tissue, enabling species identification where aDNA fails. MALDI-FTICR provides ultra-high resolution mass fingerprints. Ancient DNA archaeogenetics (Denisovans, Neanderthals) reveals admixture events invisible to morphological analysis.

## Key Concepts
1. Paleoproteomics — analysis of ancient proteins; survives longer than DNA; collagen fingerprinting for taxonomic ID
2. ZOOMs protein fingerprinting — targeted MRM mass spec for rapid bone identification
3. Ancient DNA archaeogenetics — genomic analysis of ancient hominins and fauna
4. Denisovan genetics — distinct hominin lineage identified from DNA alone; admixture with modern humans
5. PaleoProPhyler — computational phylogenetic tool placing ancient protein sequences
