## Trust Surface Q2: Cheeger Bound and Tropical LP Duality\n\n### Setup\n\nFrom Q4 (trust-surface-tropical-Q4.md): trust surface T=(B,F), verification costs w(b) = -log(κ(b)) in max-plus semiring. Sybil attack = non-trivial H^1(B,F). Tropical Čech complex with differential δ^0_⊕. Optimization: maximize minimum tropical cost of cheapest sybil cocycle.\n\n### Q2: Sheaf Cheeger Bound\n\n**Hansen-Ghrist sheaf Laplacian.** For cellular sheaf F on graph G(B), the sheaf Laplacian on 1-cochains is:\n\nL_1 = δ₀* δ₀ + δ₁* δ₁\n\nwhere δ₀: C⁰ → C¹ and δ₁: C¹ → C² are the sheaf coboundary maps, and * denotes the adjoint with respect to the natural inner product on cochains.\n\n**Spectral gap.** λ₁(L_1) = smallest nonzero eigenvalue of L_1. When λ₁ is large:\n- Local inconsistencies cannot propagate across the verification graph\n- The sheaf is rigid: local data determines global sections up to small perturbation\n- Sybil attacks (non-trivial H^1) require high-cost local fabrications to maintain coherence\n\n**Sheaf Cheeger inequality** (Hansen-Ghrist, adapting Cheeger to sheaves):\n\nλ₁(L_1) / 2 ≤ h(F) ≤ √(2 d_max λ₁(L_1))\n\nwhere h(F) is the sheaf Cheeger constant — the minimum cost to disconnect the sheaf (make H^1 non-trivial by removing verification edges), and d_max is the maximum degree of the verification graph.\n\n### Tropical Composition\n\n**Tropical sheaf Laplacian.** Apply φ = -log to the sheaf Laplacian entries. Since L_1 involves sums and products of confidence values, under φ:\n- Products → tropical sums (+)\n- Sums → tropical operations depending on aggregation rule\n\nThe **tropical spectral gap** λ₁^⊕ is the smallest nonzero tropical eigenvalue of L_1^⊕.\n\n**Max-plus eigenvalue** (Cohen-Quadrat-Gaubert): For the verification matrix A (encoding pairwise consistency costs), the tropical eigenvalue is:\n\nλ(A) = max_{cycles C in G(B)} (1/|C|) Σ_{b ∈ C} w(b)\n\nThis is the **maximum cycle mean** — the average corruption cost along the most expensive cycle in the verification graph. Under Perron-Frobenius (max-plus): if A is irreducible (verification graph is strongly connected), λ(A) is unique.\n\n**Connection to sybil resistance:** The most expensive attack cycle corresponds to the hardest sybil attack — the attacker must maintain consistent fabrications along an entire cycle of overlapping verification mechanisms. The tropical eigenvalue λ(A) bounds the minimum cost of a non-trivial cocycle.\n\n### Tropical LP Duality (Zimmermann 2006)\n\n**Primal (defender):** Choose base space B to maximize the cheapest attack cost.\n\nmax_B min_{z ∈ H^1(B,F) \ {0}} max_{b ∈ supp(z)} w(b)\n\nIn tropical LP form: max_B min_z (c^T ⊗ z) where c = (w(b₁), ..., w(b_n)) and ⊗ is tropical multiplication.\n\n**Dual (attacker):** Choose sybil strategy z to minimize cost-to-benefit ratio.\n\nmin_z max_B (b^T ⊗ y) where b encodes detection probabilities and y encodes attack intensity.\n\n**Strong duality** (Zimmermann): Under regularity conditions (verification matrix irreducible, feasible region non-empty), the optimal defender value equals the optimal attacker value:\n\nmax_B min_z attack_cost(z, B) = min_z max_B defense_cost(B, z)\n\nThis means: **the trust surface game has a value.** The optimal verification structure and the optimal sybil attack are in equilibrium.\n\n### Main Result\n\n**Theorem (Tropical Cheeger Bound for Trust Surfaces):**\n\nLet T = (B, F) be a trust surface with verification graph G(B), sheaf F, and tropical verification matrix A with corruption costs w(b) = -log(κ(b)). Then:\n\nh(G) · w_min ≤ λ₁^⊕(L_1) ≤ 2 h(F)\n\nwhere:\n- h(G) is the graph Cheeger constant of G(B)\n- w_min = min_b w(b) is the minimum corruption cost\n- λ₁^⊕(L_1) is the tropical spectral gap of the sheaf Laplacian\n- h(F) is the sheaf Cheeger constant (minimum cost to create non-trivial H^1)\n\n**Proof sketch:**\n1. Lower bound: By the sheaf Cheeger inequality (Hansen-Ghrist), h(F) ≥ λ₁/2. In tropical semiring, λ₁^⊕ ≥ h(G) · w_min because any cut in G(B) must include at least one edge with cost ≥ w_min, and the tropical cost of the cut is the max (weakest link).\n2. Upper bound: Any partition creating non-trivial H^1 corresponds to a graph cut, and the sheaf Cheeger constant is bounded by the cut cost. In tropical semiring, the cut cost = max over edges in the cut = tropical addition over the cut.\n\n**Corollary:** The minimum sybil attack cost is bounded below by h(G) · w_min and above by 2 h(F). When the verification graph is a good expander (large h(G)) and corruption costs are uniform, sybil resistance is high.\n\n### Implications\n\n1. **Expander graphs are sybil-resistant:** Trust surfaces over expander verification graphs have high tropical spectral gap → high minimum attack cost.\n2. **Game-theoretic equilibrium:** By tropical LP duality, the defender-attacker game has a value.
