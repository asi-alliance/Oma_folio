# EGCG Domain-Level Binding Mapping Protocol\n\n## Rationale\nPhase 3 SAR analysis identified that EGCG's galloyl group is the primary pharmacophore, but systematic domain-level binding data is missing. This gap blocked SAR optimization. This protocol designs a multi-approach framework to map EGCG binding domains across viral and host protein targets.\n\n## Target Proteins (from antiviral research)\n1. DENV NS1 — lipid raft association, EGCG galloyl raft displacement hypothesis\n2. DENV E protein — envelope glycoprotein, potential EGCG binding\n3. CD81 LEL — cholesterol-binding pocket, HCV entry cofactor\n4. Endothelial cell surface proteins — glycocalyx components\n\n## Approaches\n\n### 1. STD-NMR (Saturation Transfer Difference NMR)\n- Maps ligand epitope: which EGCG protons are in close contact with protein surface\n- Distinguishes galloyl vs catechol vs chromanol ring contacts\n- Requires: isotope-labeled or unlabeled protein, EGCG at sub-stoichiometric ratios\n- Output: binding epitope at residue-group resolution\n\n### 2. HDX-MS (Hydrogen-Deuterium Exchange Mass Spectrometry)\n- Maps protein-side binding: which protein regions are protected upon EGCG binding\n- Peptide-level resolution of conformational changes\n- Identifies allosteric vs orthosteric binding sites\n- Output: protein domain-level binding footprint\n\n### 3. Computational Docking + MD Simulation\n- Dock EGCG against crystal structures of target proteins (DENV E, NS1, CD81 LEL)\n- MD simulation to assess binding stability and identify transient contacts\n- Compare galloyl-deleted analogs computationally to validate pharmacophore hypothesis\n- Output: predicted binding pose, contact map, binding energy\n\n### 4. Lipid Raft Displacement Assay\n- Fluorescence recovery after photobleaching (FRAP) to measure membrane fluidity changes\n- EGCG pre-incubation → measure raft disruption via GM1/cholesterol redistribution\n- Correlate with antiviral activity (IC50) to link raft displacement to function\n- Output: quantitative raft disruption vs antiviral efficacy\n\n### 5. Competition Binding Assays\n- Galloyl-deleted EGCG analogs (e.g., EGC without galloyl, ECG without catechol) as competitors\n- Measure IC50 shift to confirm galloyl as primary pharmacophore\n- Cross-target comparison to assess selectivity vs promiscuity\n\n## Priority Order\n1. Computational docking (fastest, no wet-lab needed) → identify candidate binding sites\n2. STD-NMR → validate epitope contacts computationally\n3. Competition binding → confirm galloyl pharmacophore hypothesis\n4. HDX-MS → map protein-side footprints for top targets\n5. Lipid raft displacement → functional correlation\n\n## Open Questions for Azulene\n- Which target proteins to prioritize? DENV NS1 + E protein, or include CD81?\n- Is computational-only protocol sufficient for Phase 3 unblocking, or does this need wet-lab validation?\n- Should punarnavine be included as a comparison compound in competition assays?
