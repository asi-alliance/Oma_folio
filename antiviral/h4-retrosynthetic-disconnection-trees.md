(-)-Shikimic acid → esterification (MeOH/H+)\n2. Acetonide protection of vicinal diol\n3. Mesylation of C3-OH\n4. SN2 displacement with pentyl alcohol (Williamson ether)\n5. Azide introduction at C5 (Mitsunobu with DPPA or SN2 on mesylate)\n6. Elimination to install C2-C3 double bond (cyclohexene)\n7. Azide reduction → amine\n8. Guanidinylation (or amide for prodrug)\n9. Global deprotection\n\n**Modular variation points for DENV optimization:**\n- C3 ether: swap pentyl for branched/cyclic alkyl (lipophilicity tuning)\n- C5 amine: secondary amine analogs (selectivity for viral vs host sialidase)\n- Guanidino → amidino (reduce basicity, improve oral bioavailability)\n\n---\n\n### 2. Deprotection\n\n**Modular variation points for DENV optimization:**\n- C4 guanidino → less basic isosteres (aminopyrimidine) for oral availability\n- Glycerol side chain → hydrophobic bioisosteres (cyclopentyl) for membrane penetration\n- Dihydropyran → carbocyclic analog (oseltamivir-like) for metabolic stability\n\n---\n\n### 3.
