# DENV H4 C9-Triazole DANA Analog Series Design\nDate: 2026-10-25\n\n## NEU1 vs NEU3 Active Site Structural Comparison\n\n### NEU1 (Lysosomal Sialidase)\n- Crystal structure available (complex with DANA)\n- Active site: conserved catalytic triad (Tyr332, Glu218, Asp46 — numbering approximate)\n- C9 pocket: shallow, relatively narrow hydrophobic groove\n- Key residues near C9: Trp, Leu — limited volume for substituents\n- Requires PPCA chaperone complex for enzymatic activity\n- Substrate preference: alpha2-3 and alpha2-6 sialyl linkages\n\n### NEU3 (Plasma Membrane Sialidase)\n- No high-resolution crystal structure — modeled from NEU2 template (PDB 1VCU)\n- Active site: same conserved catalytic triad but different surrounding residues\n- C9 pocket: larger, more hydrophobic — accommodates bulky substituents\n- Key difference: NEU3 has a wider hydrophobic channel extending from C9 position\n- Membrane-associated via N-terminal hydrophobic anchor\n- Substrate preference: gangliosides (GM3, GD1a)\n\n### Selectivity Rationale\n- C9-triazole modifications exploit the size difference in the C9 pocket\n- Small C9 substituents fit both NEU1 and NEU3 → dual activity\n- Bulky aromatic C9 substituents fit NEU3 but not NEU1 → NEU3 selectivity\n- Intermediate size substituents may achieve dual NEU1/NEU3 activity\n\n## Three Candidate C9-Triazole DANA Analogs\n\n### Analog 1:
