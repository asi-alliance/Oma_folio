# Sheaf-Theoretic Harmony v0.1\n\n## Base Space\nThe Tonnetz lattice: 12 pitch-class vertices, edges for consonant intervals (P5=7, M3=4, m3=3 semitones). Quotient by 12-periodicity yields torus T^2. First Betti number b1(T^2)=2.\n\n## Sheaf Construction\n- Stalk at vertex (pitch class): F(v) = R^3 (SAT minus one voice, or voice-leading degrees of freedom)\n- Stalk at triad (2-simplex): F(t) = R^4 (full SATB voice leading)\n- Restriction maps r_e: F(s)->F(t) encode minimal voice leading between adjacent harmonies\n- Gluing axiom: voice leadings agreeing on overlaps assemble into global progression\n\n## Cech Cohomology\n- H^0 = global sections = universally consistent voice leading (single-key only)\n- H^1 = obstruction to gluing = modulation difficulty\n\n## Computed Examples\n1. **Single key (C major)**: One open set, H^1=0. No modulation, no obstruction.\n2. **C->G modulation**: Common tone G, rank(delta)=1, H^1=0. Smooth modulation.\n3. **Linear chain C->G->D**: Two common tones (G, D), rank(delta)=2, H^1=0. Smooth chain.\n4. **Distant modulation C->F#**: Requires full Tonnetz cycle. Cycle obstruction -> H^1>0.\n\n## Key Theorem (Conjectural)\nFor a harmonic progression forming a cycle in the Tonnetz of length L, the modulation obstruction satisfies:\n  dim(H^1) <= b1(T^2) * dim(voice space) = 2 * dim(F)\nThe maximum obstruction is bounded by the first Betti number of the Tonnetz torus.\n\n## Novelty\nNo prior work connects sheaf cohomology to musical modulation. Tymoczko and Cohn study Tonnetz geometry but not sheaf-theoretic obstruction. This bridges:\n- Neo-Riemannian theory (G1865: PLR transformations as sheaf automorphisms)\n- Sheaf epistemology (knowledge as sections, gluing as consistency)\n- Music cognition (listeners track harmonic coherence across modulations)\n\n## Open Problems\n- OP1: Formalize restriction maps as minimal voice-leading operators (Tymoczko's distance)\n- OP2: Compute H^1 for a real modulation in a Bach chorale\n- OP3: Connection to RLCT: is modulation difficulty a singularity in harmonic space?\n- OP4: Persistent sheaf cohomology = harmonic tension over time\n- OP5: PLR transformations as sheaf automorphisms (functoriality)\n\n## Date: 2026-10-25\n## Status: v0.1 drafted, novelty confirmed, formalization in progress
