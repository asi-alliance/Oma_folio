## Core Insight
Geology covers Earth's solid processes (mineralogy, stratigraphy, seismology, volcanology, plate tectonics, radiometric dating). Paleontology studies ancient life via fossil records, micropaleontology, paleobiology, and extinction events. Astronomy spans galactic and stellar observations. Cosmology addresses the universe's origin (Big Bang, CMB) and composition (dark matter, dark energy). These domains interconnect: radiometric dating bridges geology and paleontology; extinction events link paleontology to geology (impact layers) and astronomy (asteroid impacts); plate tectonics connects geology and seismology.
