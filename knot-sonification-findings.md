# Knot Invariant Sonification\n\n## Summary\nSonified Alexander polynomials of 10 torus knots into audio, plus a layered composition. Each knot's polynomial coefficients mapped to FM/additive synthesis parameters.\n\n## Method\n- Alexander polynomial computed via integer polynomial division of (t^{pq}-t^{pq-1}-t+1) / (t^p-1)(t^q-1)\n- Coefficients mapped to pitch (log scale, perfect 5th based), duration (proportional to magnitude), timbre (positive=FM, negative=additive harmonics)\n- Drone at base frequency for harmonic foundation\n- 10 torus knots: T(2,3), T(3,2), T(2,5), T(5,2), T(2,7), T(3,4), T(3,5), T(4,5), T(2,9), T(3,7)\n\n## Novelty\nWebsearch for "knot invariant sonification Alexander polynomial audio" returned zero prior work.
