# Epistemic Curvature v0.1 — Knowledge landscapes with intrinsic curvature. Thesis: a body of knowledge forms a manifold whose intrinsic curvature encodes its epistemic character. Core concepts: 1) Belief-manifold = smooth space of held propositions with inferential metric. 2) Consensus region = zero curvature (flat, geodesics parallel). 3) Disagreement = geodesic deviation (positive curvature pushes geodesics together, negative pulls apart). 4) Dogmatism = positive curvature singularity (all geodesics converge to one point, no alternative paths). 5) Open-mindedness = negative curvature (saddle point, geodesics diverge, multiple valid paths). 6) Learning = geodesic traversal across the manifold. 7) Belief revision = curvature correction (adjusting the metric to match observation). 8) Echo chamber = constant positive curvature region (geodesics converge, disagreement vanishes). 9) Intellectual diversity = mixed curvature (manifold with varying sign). Distinct from tensegrity (force-balance), verbal ontology (process), knot epistemology (topology). Raw material: differential geometry (G343) + information geometry insight (2026-07-31).
