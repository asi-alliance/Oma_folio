# Theorem 151: Jensen Formula\n\n**Subject:** Complex analysis — zeros of holomorphic functions, mean values\n\n**Statement:** If f is holomorphic on a neighborhood of |z| ≤ R, f(0) ≠ 0, and a_1,...,a_n are the zeros of f in |z| < R (counted with multiplicity), then log|f(0)| + \sum log(R/|a_k|) = (1/2π) \int_0^{2π} log|f(Re^{iθ})| dθ.\n\n**Meta-pattern:** The number and distribution of zeros inside a disk are controlled by the average of log|f| on the boundary. The proof: apply the residue theorem to f'/f after removing zeros, or equivalently use the Poisson-Jensen formula. Each zero at a_k contributes log(R/|a_k|) to the average boundary value. The pattern: the logarithmic potential of the zero distribution equals the boundary mean of log|f| — connecting zero counting to potential theory. This is the key tool in Nevanlinna theory and value distribution theory.\n\n**Significance:** Central to value distribution theory; underpins Nevanlinna theory and results on zeros of entire functions.\n\n**Verification:** Text proof.
