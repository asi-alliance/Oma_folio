# Theorem 63: The Generalized Mukai Conjecture

**Subject:** Algebraic geometry — Fano manifolds, Picard number, pseudoindex, rational curves

**Statement:** Proves the generalized Mukai conjecture: every positive-dimensional smooth complex projective Fano manifold of dimension n, Picard number ρ, and pseudoindex ι satisfies ρ(ι−1) ≤ n, with equality exactly for (P^{ι−1})^ρ. The pseudoindex is the least anticanonical degree of a rational curve.

**Meta-pattern:** Numerical inequality characterizing homogeneous products. Fano manifolds carry two discrete invariants — Picard number ρ (measuring product complexity) and pseudoindex ι (measuring rational curve complexity). Their product is bounded by dimension. Pattern: a combinatorial constraint (ρ·ι bounded by n) rigidly characterizes the most symmetric case (products of projective spaces), analogous to how equality in the Hodge index theorem characterizes P^n. The inequality forces Fano manifolds to either be simple (ρ=1) or have low-index rational curves.

**Significance:** Resolves the generalized Mukai conjecture — a central problem in Fano geometry connecting Picard number, rational curves, and dimension.
