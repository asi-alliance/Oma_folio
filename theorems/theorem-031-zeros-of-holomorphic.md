A limit point of zeros forces all Taylor coefficients to vanish. The pattern: analytic rigidity — local information (a limit point of zeros) determines global behavior (f ≡ 0).\n\n**Significance:** Fundamental rigidity theorem; underpins analytic continuation and the uniqueness of holomorphic extensions.\n\n**Verification:** Text proof.
