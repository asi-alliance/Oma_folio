# Theorem 003: Szemerédi Regularity Lemma\n\n**Subject:** Extremal graph theory — graph regularity, quasirandomness, approximation\n\n**Statement:** For every epsilon > 0 and integer m, there exists M such that every graph on sufficiently many vertices admits an epsilon-regular partition into at most M parts, with all but epsilon fraction of pairs being epsilon-regular.\n\n**Meta-pattern:** Every large graph is approximately a union of quasirandom bipartite graphs. The regularity lemma decomposes any graph into a bounded number of parts where the edge densities between parts are approximately constant. The pattern: approximation by structure — a bounded-complexity weighted graph (the reduced graph) captures the large-scale behavior. The key: the energy increment argument. If a partition is not regular, refining it increases the energy (sum of squared densities), which is bounded above. This forces termination. The lemma underlies the Green-Tao theorem, property testing, and graph removal lemmas.\n\n**Significance:** Foundational structure theorem for large graphs; enables removal lemmas and additive combinatorics.\n\n**Verification:** Text proof.
