The proof for convex bodies uses the Prékopa-Leindler inequality (a functional form). The pattern: the n-th root of volume is concave under Minkowski addition — convexity of sets translates to concavity of the volume functional. This is the geometric analogue of the AM-GM inequality and implies the classical isoperimetric inequality.\n\n**Significance:** Central inequality in convex geometry; implies isoperimetric inequality and has functional analogues.\n\n**Verification:** Text proof.
