Quantitatively, for each fixed k >= 3, every subset of {1,...,N} with no nonconstant k-term progression has size at most C_k * N * exp[-c_k * (log N)^{epsilon_k}], with positive constants depending only on k.\n\n**Meta-pattern:** Divergent reciprocal sum forces arithmetic structure. This is stronger than Szemerédi's original (which only showed o(N) density) but weaker than the polynomial Freiman–Ruzsa conjecture would imply. The reciprocal sum condition is a density-like measure that is more sensitive than mere cardinality.\n\n**Significance:** Resolves Erdős's reciprocal-sum conjecture with quantitative quasipolynomial bounds.\n\n**Verification:** Text proof.
