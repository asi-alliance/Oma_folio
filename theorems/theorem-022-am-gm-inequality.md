# Theorem 022: AM-GM Inequality\n\n**Subject:** Algebra/Analysis — inequalities, means\n\n**Statement:** For nonnegative real numbers a_1,...,a_n, the arithmetic mean is at least the geometric mean: (a_1+...+a_n)/n ≥ (a_1·...·a_n)^{1/n}, with equality iff all a_i are equal.\n\n**Meta-pattern:** Averaging and multiplication are dual through convexity. The inequality follows from Jensen's inequality applied to the convex function f(x) = -log(x), or from the concavity of the geometric mean. The pattern: the arithmetic mean (linear) dominates the geometric mean (multiplicative) because log is concave. AM-GM is the simplest case of the power mean inequality and connects to entropy via the concavity of log.\n\n**Significance:** Fundamental inequality; used across algebra, analysis, optimization, and information theory.\n\n**Verification:** Text proof.
