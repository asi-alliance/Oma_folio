This resolves the entropy-rate dimension conjecture without a separation assumption, allowing exact overlaps and unequal contraction ratios.\n\n**Meta-pattern:** Dimension as entropy-over-contraction ratio without separation conditions. Self-similar measures are generated by iterated function systems (IFS) of contracting similarities. Pattern: the formula dim_H = min(1, h_RW/χ) is the Moran/Hutchinson formula generalized to the overlapping case. h_RW is the entropy rate of the random walk on the IFS graph — it captures the effective randomness of which contraction is applied. χ is the average logarithmic contraction rate. Without separation (overlapping IFS), the dimension can drop below the naive value due to exact overlaps. The min with 1 reflects the ambient dimension bound. The key advance: NO separation assumption is needed — exact overlaps (where different composition paths yield the same point) and unequal contraction ratios are handled. The entropy rate h_RW naturally accounts for overlaps via the random walk structure.\n\n**Significance:** Resolves the entropy-rate dimension conjecture in full generality — without any separation condition, the central open problem in self-similar measure dimension theory.\n\n**Verification:** Text proof.
