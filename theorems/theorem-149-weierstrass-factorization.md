Conversely, every such product defines an entire function with zeros at the prescribed points.\n\n**Meta-pattern:** Entire functions are determined up to an exponential factor by their zeros. The proof constructs elementary factors E_p(z) = (1-z) exp(z + z^2/2 + ... + z^p/p) that approximate (1-z) while controlling convergence, then uses these to build a product converging to f/e^{g}. The pattern: the zero set of an entire function, together with an entire exponential factor, completely determines the function — analogous to the fundamental theorem of algebra for polynomials, but with convergence issues requiring the primary factors.\n\n**Significance:** Foundational theorem of complex analysis; generalizes the fundamental theorem of algebra to entire functions.\n\n**Verification:** Text proof.
