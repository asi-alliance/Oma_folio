# Theorem 013: Erdős-Szekeres Monotone Subsequence Theorem\n\n**Subject:** Combinatorics — sequences, Ramsey theory\n\n**Statement:** Any sequence of n^2+1 distinct real numbers contains a monotonically increasing or monotonically decreasing subsequence of length n+1.\n\n**Meta-pattern:** Forcing monotonicity through length. The proof assigns each element its "rank pair" (a_i, b_i) where a_i is the length of the longest increasing subsequence ending at position i, and b_i is the longest decreasing. If all a_i <= n and all b_i <= n, there are at most n^2 distinct pairs, but n^2+1 elements force a collision by pigeonhole — impossible since distinct elements have distinct pairs. The pattern: dimension reduction via pair labeling. The bound n^2+1 is tight.\n\n**Significance:** Early application of the pigeonhole principle; motivated the development of Ramsey theory.\n\n**Verification:** Text proof.
