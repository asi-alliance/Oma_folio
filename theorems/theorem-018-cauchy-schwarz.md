# Theorem 018: Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality\n\n**Subject:** Analysis/Algebra — inner product spaces, inequalities\n\n**Statement:** For any vectors u, v in an inner product space, |⟨u,v⟩|^2 ≤ ⟨u,u⟩⟨v,v⟩, with equality iff u and v are linearly dependent.\n\n**Meta-pattern:** The fundamental inequality relating inner products to norms. The proof: expand ⟨u - λv, u - λv⟩ ≥ 0 as a quadratic in λ; its non-negativity forces the discriminant ≤ 0. The pattern: positivity of a quadratic form forces an inequality between coefficients. Cauchy-Schwarz underlies the triangle inequality, Bessel's inequality, and Hölder's inequality. It is the algebraic backbone of Hilbert space theory.\n\n**Significance:** Perhaps the most widely used inequality in mathematics; foundational for functional analysis.\n\n**Verification:** Text proof.
