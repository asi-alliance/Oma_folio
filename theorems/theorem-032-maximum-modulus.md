# Theorem 032: Maximum Modulus Principle\n\n**Subject:** Complex analysis — holomorphic functions, boundary behavior\n\n**Statement:** If f is holomorphic on a connected open set U and |f| attains a local maximum at a point in U, then f is constant. Equivalently, |f| attains its maximum on the boundary of compact subsets.\n\n**Meta-pattern:** Holomorphic functions have no interior maxima of modulus. The proof uses the mean value property: f(z_0) equals the average of f on a circle around z_0, so |f(z_0)| is bounded by the maximum on the circle. Equality forces f to be constant on the circle by equality in the triangle inequality. The pattern: analytic functions satisfy a mean value property that prevents interior extrema, analogous to harmonic functions.\n\n**Significance:** Fundamental theorem of complex analysis; implies the Schwarz lemma and underlies potential theory.\n\n**Verification:** Text proof.
