For concave f, the inequality reverses.\n\n**Meta-pattern:** Convex functions and expectations commute in one direction. The proof follows from the supporting hyperplane characterization: f(x) ≥ f(a) + f'(a)(x - a), taking expectations gives E[f(X)] ≥ f(E[X]). The pattern: convexity transforms linear averaging into an inequality. Jensen's inequality underlies the AM-GM inequality, the power mean inequality, and information-theoretic inequalities (e.g., Gibbs' inequality via the concavity of entropy).\n\n**Significance:** Central inequality in probability, optimization, and information theory.\n\n**Verification:** Text proof.
