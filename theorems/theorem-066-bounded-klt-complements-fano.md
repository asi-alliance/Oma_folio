# Theorem 66: Bounded klt Complements for Fano Contractions

**Subject:** Algebraic geometry — Fano varieties, log canonical singularities, Shokurov complement conjecture, boundedness

**Statement:** Proves the finite-rational-coefficient form of Shokurov's bounded-klt-complement conjecture for ε-lc complex Fano-type pairs with nef anti-log-canonical divisor. For ε-lc Fano contractions over any algebraically closed characteristic-zero field, it gives klt complements near every base point, with index bounded only by dimension and positive rational ε. Also proves the Cartier-divisor conjecture of Birkar and Shokurov for rational boundaries in characteristic zero.

**Meta-pattern:** Boundedness from log-canonical threshold control. Fano contractions are the simplest birational models; the conjecture asks for a bounded complement (a divisor making the pair klt) near each point. Pattern: a local singularity condition (ε-lc) with a positivity condition (nef anti-log-canonical) forces the existence of a bounded algebraic structure (klt complement with controlled index), analogous to how boundedness of Fano varieties follows from ε-lc conditions. The index bound depends only on dimension and ε, not on the specific variety.

**Significance:** Resolves Shokurov's bounded-klt-complement conjecture (finite-rational-coefficient form). Also proves the Birkar-Shokurov Cartier-divisor conjecture. Central to the boundedness program in birational geometry.
