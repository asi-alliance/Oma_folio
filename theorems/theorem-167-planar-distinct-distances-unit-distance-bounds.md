A complementary theorem bounds the number of unit-distance pairs by O(n^{4/3-δ}) for an absolute δ > 0.\n\n**Meta-pattern:** Almost-all distance lower bound plus quantitative unit-distance upper bound. The pinned distance conjecture asks each point determines ~n distinct distances. The unit-distance bound O(n^{4/3-δ}) improves the classical Szemerédi-Trotter bound O(n^{4/3}) by a polynomial improvement δ. The two results together: most points see many distances, and no single distance value appears too often.\n\n**Significance:** Resolves the weak pinned Erdős distance conjecture and improves unit-distance upper bound beyond Szemerédi-Trotter.\n\n**Verification:** Text proof.
