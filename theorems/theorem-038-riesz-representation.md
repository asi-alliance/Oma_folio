# Theorem 038: Riesz Representation Theorem\n\n**Subject:** Functional analysis — Hilbert spaces, dual spaces\n\n**Statement:** For every bounded linear functional f on a Hilbert space H, there exists a unique y in H such that f(x) = ⟨x, y⟩ for all x in H, and ||f|| = ||y||.\n\n**Meta-pattern:** Hilbert spaces are self-dual — every functional is an inner product. The proof: if f ≠ 0, take y0 in ker(f)⊥ with f(y0) = 1, then y = y0/||y0||² satisfies the representation. Uniqueness follows from the nondegeneracy of the inner product. The pattern: the inner product provides a canonical isomorphism between H and its dual H*, identifying functionals with vectors. This self-duality is the defining feature distinguishing Hilbert spaces from Banach spaces.\n\n**Significance:** Cornerstone of Hilbert space theory; underlies weak convergence, adjoint operators, and spectral theory.\n\n**Verification:** Text proof.
