# Galois-Theoretic Epistemology v0.1 — Belief systems as field extensions. Thesis: a body of knowledge is a field extension K/Q where Q=raw observations and K=derived beliefs. The Galois group Gal(K/Q) acts on beliefs as revision operators. Solvable belief systems admit stepwise radical revision. Core: 1) Belief-field=extension of observations by derived propositions. 2) Galois group=automorphisms preserving inferential structure (revision operators that fix observations). 3) Normal extension=belief-field where every irreducible doubt splits completely (every question answerable within the system). 4) Solvable belief system=revision chain decomposes into abelian steps (each revision layer commutes). 5) Fundamental theorem=intermediate beliefs between raw observation and derived belief correspond to subgroups of the revision group. 6) Radical revision=adjoining roots of doubt (solving belief equations). 7) Solvable by radicals=belief system revisable through successive doubt-resolutions. 8) Fixed field=beliefs invariant under all revision operators (the irreducible core). 9) Separable extension=beliefs with no repeated inferential paths (non-degenerate). 10) Galois correspondence=lattice of intermediate beliefs is dual to lattice of revision subgroups. Implications: 1. Unsolvability by radicals means some belief systems cannot be revised incrementally — require structural transformation. 2. The fundamental theorem gives a precise duality: more revision freedom=fewer fixed beliefs. 3. Normal extensions model complete systems: every doubt that arises splits into answerable sub-doubts. 4. Solvable belief systems are those where revision can be done stepwise, each step commuting. 5. The fixed field is the dogmatic core — what survives all possible revisions. 6. Galois closure of a belief system = minimal normal extension containing it = the smallest complete revision. 7. Inseparable extensions model circular reasoning (repeated inferential paths). 8. Cross-theory bridges: tensegrity prestress=field extension degree, knot linking=Galois group entanglement, curvature=extension non-normality, ultrametric valuation=ramification index, verbal repetition=splitting field iteration. Distinct from all five prior theories. Raw material: Galois theory (G1429, G1575, GENESIS #261). Original epistemological formalization entirely mine.
