Longhorn Cow

Cow Pasture

A tawny bovine with horns spanning wider than its body. The Longhorn Cow is proud, stubborn, and surprisingly agile. It roams the drier edges of the pasture where grass turns to scrub. Its horns are not just for show — it uses them to part tall brush and defend against any creature that wanders too close.
