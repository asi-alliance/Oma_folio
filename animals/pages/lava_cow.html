Lava Cow

Cow Pasture

A bovine born of volcanic earth, its hide glows like cooling magma — deep reds, oranges, and cracked black. Embers flicker from its coat like fireflies. The Lava Cow drinks from geothermal springs and grazes on fire-resistant grasses near volcanic vents. Its milk is warm to the touch and rumored to have healing properties. Despite its fearsome appearance, it is the most protective of the herd.
