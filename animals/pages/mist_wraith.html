Mist Wraith

Aether Drift

A drifting phantom of fog and shadow, nearly transparent. Its pale violet eyes glow through the mist like distant stars. Mist Wraiths feed on ambient moisture and the residual static left by Storm Crows. They are elusive, rarely seen except at dawn when fog clings to the valleys between cloud banks.

Storm Crow

Prey

Ambient moisture and static residue
