Storm Crow

Aether Drift

A dark-feathered corvid crackling with static electricity. Storm Crows ride thunderheads and gather lightning on their wingtips. They are opportunistic hunters, snatching Lantern Moths from the air and harassing Cloud Serpents away from their nesting grounds. Their golden eyes flash before a strike.
