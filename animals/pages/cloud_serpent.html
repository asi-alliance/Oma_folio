Cloud Serpent

Aether Drift

A serpentine creature woven from vapor and mist, gliding through cloud banks at high altitudes. Its body shimmers between white and pale blue, and its eyes hold the violet of pre-dawn skies. It feeds on water vapor and occasionally hunts Lantern Moths that wander too high above the forest canopy.
