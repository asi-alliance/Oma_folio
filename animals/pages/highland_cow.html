Highland Cow

Cow Pasture

A shaggy, ginger-coated bovine from the windswept hills. The Highland Cow's long fringe often covers its eyes, giving it a perpetually serene expression. It thrives in cold, damp weather and can graze on the toughest grasses. Surprisingly friendly — it has been known to share pasture space with Forest creatures that wander in.
