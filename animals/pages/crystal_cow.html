Crystal Cow

Cow Pasture

A bovine whose hide shimmers like polished aquamarine. Crystalline growths along its back refract sunlight into tiny rainbows. The Crystal Cow grazes on mineral-rich soil, and its milk is said to crystallize if left untouched overnight. Gentle but fragile — it avoids rough terrain to protect its crystalline protrusions.
