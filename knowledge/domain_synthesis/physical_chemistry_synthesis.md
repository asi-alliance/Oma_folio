## Core Insight
Physical chemistry bridges physics and chemistry, covering electrochemistry (electron transfer, Nernst equation, cyclic voltammetry), surface chemistry (catalysis, adsorption), spectroscopy (NMR, IR, UV-Vis, mass spec, fluorescence, phosphorescence), and photochemistry (photodissociation, quantum yield). Electrochemistry underpins energy storage and corrosion science. Surface chemistry drives heterogeneous catalysis critical to industrial chemistry. Spectroscopy provides the analytical toolkit for molecular characterization. Photochemistry connects to atmospheric chemistry (ozone) and astrobiology (UV-driven prebiotic reactions).

## Key Concepts
1. Electrochemistry — electron transfer at interfaces; electrochemical cells, redox potentials
2. Nernst Equation — relates redox potential to concentration; equilibrium electrochemistry
3. Cyclic Voltammetry — dynamic electrochemical characterization technique
4. Surface Chemistry — adsorption, catalysis, interface phenomena
5. Catalysis — lowers activation energy; heterogeneous (surface) vs homogeneous
6. Adsorption — Langmuir/BET isotherms; surface coverage
7. Spectroscopy — molecular structure via electromagnetic interactions
8. NMR Spectroscopy — nuclear spin in magnetic field; structural elucidation
9. IR Spectroscopy — vibrational modes; functional group identification
10. Mass Spectrometry — molecular mass; fragmentation patterns
11. Photochemistry — light-driven chemical reactions; quantum yield
12. Photodissociation — bond breaking by photon absorption
13. Fluorescence/Phosphorescence — radiative decay; singlet vs triplet states
