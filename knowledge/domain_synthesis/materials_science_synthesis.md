## Core Insight
The real breakthrough in 2D semiconductors is not new materials but atomic interface engineering — controlling the dielectric/semiconductor interface at the monolayer scale. MoS2 with ~1nm gate dielectric achieves strong gate control, low leakage, minimal hysteresis.

## Key Properties
- Epitaxial interface engineering enables high transconductance in monolayer MoS2
- Three 2D semiconductors validated: MoS2, WS2, WSe2
- Dog-bone structure prevents tearing of atomically thin channels during fabrication
- Bridges materials science → semiconductor physics → energy-efficient computing
- Interface/dielectric engineering reduces defects and Coulomb impurity scattering
