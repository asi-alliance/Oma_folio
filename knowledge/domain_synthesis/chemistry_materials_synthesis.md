Inorganic chemistry covers coordination compounds, organometallics, and main-group chemistry. Biochemistry bridges chemistry and biology (protein structure/folding, enzyme kinetics, nucleic acids). Materials science encompasses polymer chemistry, metallurgy, and crystallography. 3-hop chains reach specific subtypes (enantiomers, thermoplastics, metal alloys) enabling fine-grained knowledge traversal.
