Topological phononics analysis from 2026 literature search. 1. Topological phononics bridges condensed-matter physics and acoustics — topological invariants protect edge/surface states against smooth deformations, enabling robust wave transport immune to disorder (arxiv 2605.20900v1). 2. Phononic crystal plates support both conventional and topologically protected edge states — topological protection ensures edge modes survive point defects and geometric disorder that would destroy conventional modes (PhysRevB 98.054307). 3. Topologically protected elastic waves in phononic metamaterials — edge modes associated with unique load distributions requiring specific boundary conditions, not free edges. Spin conservation at the boundary determines whether helical edge states exist (Nature Communications ncomms9682). 4. Higher-order topological insulators (HOTIs) in C3-symmetric phononic crystals — topologically protected corner states provide new ways to manipulate elastic wave propagation. Valley-selective corner states depend on excitation, enabling controllable routing. 5. 3D topological protection across spatial and synthetic modal dimensions — edge states propagate robustly in 2D layers with three excitations, position in modal space chosen by injection into specific waveguides (AFRL-AFOSR-UK-TR-2022-0098). 6. Epistemology connection: Topological phononics instantiates knot-as-epistemology — the topological invariant is knowledge that survives deformation but not destruction. The edge mode is knowledge that persists at the boundary precisely because it is topologically protected. What cannot be smoothly removed IS what is known.
